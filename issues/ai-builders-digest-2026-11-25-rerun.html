Theme 02

The Adoption Gap Inside Companies / 公司内部的采用鸿沟

AI 的影响不再只是公司之间的差距，而开始变成同一家公司不同团队之间的巨大分层。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 说，AI 正在制造一种新的组织不平等：同一家公司里，不同团队因为是否真正围绕 agent 重做工作流，生产力可能相差 10 倍。这和 cloud 或数字化不一样，后者通常会让整家公司获得相对统一的提升。目前这种差距主要集中在 coding，接下来会扩散到所有 coding adjacent 的工作。

即使在同一家公司里，AI 采用程度最低和最高的团队，其生产力影响也可能相差 10 倍。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie says AI is producing a new kind of organizational inequality: teams inside the same company can now see a 10X productivity gap depending on how well they redesign workflows around agents. That is different from cloud or digital adoption, which usually standardized the uplift across a firm. For now the effect is concentrated in coding, but coding-adjacent work is next.

Even within the same company you can have a 10X delta between the lowest and highest adopters of AI and the impact they're seeing.

Countries want sovereign AI that runs on their own soil and infrastructure; if American companies do not meet that demand, Chinese models, chips, and infrastructure will become the default stack. Frontier quality matters, but global deployment creates lasting strategic influence.
