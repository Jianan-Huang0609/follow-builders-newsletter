Theme 01

个人 Agent 的下一站 / The Personal Agent Race

中文

产品作者 Peter Yang 认为，个人 agent 的竞争最后比的可能不是模型 benchmark，而是分发能力和上下文。他把 Muse 看作强势的消费级候选，ChatGPT 仍然拥有 10 亿级用户的先发优势，Grok Bot 偏向工作场景，可能变成多人协作的 agentic Slack；Google 则是黑马，因为 Gmail、Calendar 和其他个人数据本来就在它手里。

真正还没人解决的是多人协作：你还不能轻松把伴侣加进 Muse 对话一起规划旅行，也不能把同事拉进 ChatGPT 或 Grok Bot 的 thread。更实际的建议是，把自己的 skills 和 files 设计成可以在不同 harness 和 agent 之间迁移，否则换工具本身就会消耗掉一半时间。

我对个人 agent 竞争的‘旁观者’想法：Muse 很可能成为领导者……ChatGPT 可能仍是个人 agent 的领导者，因为它已经有 10 亿以上用户……Google 显然是黑马。

说到多人协作，现在还没人真正解决这个问题。我不能轻松把伴侣加进 Muse 对话来规划旅行，也不能把同事拉进 ChatGPT 或 Grok Bot 的 thread。

每个人都应该把自己的 skills 和 files 设计成容易在不同 harness 和 agent 之间迁移。否则你会有一半时间花在从一个工具搬到另一个工具上。

English

Product writer Peter Yang thinks the personal-agent race will be decided less by model benchmarks than by distribution and context. He sees Muse as a strong consumer contender, ChatGPT as the incumbent with a billion-plus users, Grok Bot as a work-focused product that could become multiplayer agentic Slack, and Google as the dark horse because it already owns Gmail, Calendar, and other personal data.

The missing piece is multiplayer: people still cannot easily add a spouse to an agent chat to plan a trip, or bring coworkers into a ChatGPT or Grok Bot thread. His practical advice is to design personal skills and files so they can move between harnesses and agents, otherwise switching tools will consume as much time as using them.

My ‘armchair’ thoughts on the personal agent race: Muse is poised to become the leader... ChatGPT is probably still the personal agent leader because it already has 1B+ users... Google is obviously the dark horse candidate.

Speaking of multiplayer, nobody has figured this out yet. I can’t easily add my spouse to a Muse chat to plan a vacation. I also can’t loop coworkers into ChatGPT or Grok Bot threads.

Everyone should design their personal skills and files so they’re easy to port between harnesses and agents. Otherwise, you’ll spend half your time moving from one tool to another.

agent 测试和 QA 软件的彻底程度是无与伦比的。

Theme 03

Agent 的接口与账户 / Agents Need Money and Rails

NP
No Priors
Coinbase CEO · No Priors
中文

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong 认为，AI agent 最终需要自己的金融账户。银行卡并不适合 agent 之间大量发生的小额交易，而 stablecoin 可以在全球范围内用不到一秒、低于 1 美分的成本转账，因此更像 agentic economy 的底层支付轨道。

实际形态可以是一个有额度边界的 agent spend account，或者一个 self-custodial wallet：agent 可以自己付商品费用、开通云资源、预订服务或收款，而不用每次都停下来让人输入信用卡。Armstrong 的说法很直接：如果我们要让 AI 获得金融服务，就不能让它们处于未银行化状态。

AI agent 自己最终必须拥有账户。

不到一秒、低于 1 美分，就能把钱转到世界任何地方。

我们不希望 AI 处于未银行化状态。

English

Coinbase CEO Brian Armstrong argues that AI agents will need financial accounts of their own. Card payments are badly suited to tiny agent-to-agent transactions, while stablecoin rails can move money globally in under a second and for less than a cent, making them a more plausible base layer for an agentic economy.

The practical model is an agent with a bounded spend account or a self-custodial wallet: it could pay for goods, provision cloud resources, book services, or receive money without asking a human to paste in a credit card every time. Armstrong’s framing is simple: if we want to bank the AIs, they need financial services too.

The AI agents themselves are gonna have to have their own accounts.

Under one second, under 1¢ US to move money anywhere in the world.

We don’t want the AIs to be unbanked.
