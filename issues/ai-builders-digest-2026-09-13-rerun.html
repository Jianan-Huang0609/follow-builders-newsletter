The New Product Layer / 新的产品层

AI 应用的形态正在从记录系统，转向围绕具体领域组织 agent 的 harness。

GT
Garry Tan
@garrytan

Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan 用一句话描述产品形态的转变：一家公司要么作为 system of record 消亡，要么活到成为 domain-specific harness。潜台词是，存储信息不再是终点，真正持久的产品要围绕一个领域来组织工作。

你要么作为 system of record 死去，要么活到成为 domain-specific harness。

English

Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan frames a product transition in one line: a company either dies as a system of record or lives long enough to become a domain-specific harness. The implication is that storing information is no longer the endpoint; the durable product coordinates work around a domain.
