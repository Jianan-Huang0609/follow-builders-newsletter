English

Bob McGrew's central argument is that superintelligence cannot arrive through the digital world alone: the scarce complement to cheap intelligence is physical capacity. Automating researchers can create a large one-time acceleration, but experiments still need GPUs, wet labs, machines, and manufacturing. If data centers need bulldozers, building the bulldozer factory itself also needs bulldozers.

I don't think you are actually gonna be able to get to superintelligence without changes in the physical world.

At the end of the day, you have to run the experiments.
