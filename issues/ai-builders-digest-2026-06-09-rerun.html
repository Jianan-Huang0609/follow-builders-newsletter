Theme 01

Context > Intelligence / 上下文不可替代

Aaron Levie 论证：没有任何模型智能可以替代上下文。对任何足够通用的 AI，你永远需要引导它走向你希望的方向。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 提炼了一条关于 AI agent 的第一原理（216 赞）：「没有任何模型智能能够替代上下文。对任何足够通用的 AI，你永远需要引导它走向你实际想要的方向。」

这是对「AGI 解决一切」叙事的来自企业侧的反驳。模型再聪明，也不可能知道 Box 的客户需要什么、法律约束是什么、产品路线图是什么样——这些不是推理问题，是上下文问题。Levie 的框架暗示：护城河不在模型访问权，而在只有组织才拥有的制度性上下文。

没有任何模型智能能够替代上下文。对任何足够通用的 AI，你永远需要引导它走向你实际想要的方向。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie distilled a first principle about AI agents (216 likes): 'There's no amount of intelligence that can get packed into AI models that replaces the need for context. For any sufficiently general-purpose AI, you will always have to guide it in the direction you actually want.'

This is the enterprise counterpoint to the 'AGI solves everything' narrative. No matter how smart the model gets, knowing what Box's customers need, what the legal constraints are, what the product roadmap looks like — these aren't reasoning problems, they're context problems. Levie's framing implies that the moat isn't in model access, it's in institutional context that only the organization possesses.

There's no amount of intelligence that can get packed into AI models that replaces the need for context. For any sufficiently general-purpose AI, you will always have to guide it in the direction you actually want.
