Theme 02

Hide the Model / 把模型选择藏起来

用户想要的是任务完成，不是每天重新学习哪一个模型适合哪一个工作。

中文

Meta AI 负责人 Madhu Guru 认为，AI 产品应该把模型选择从用户面前抽象掉。要做到这一点，后台得具备三种能力：理解不同规模模型的前沿能力，为具体工作流做好 eval，以及在模型前沿变化时持续适应。换句话说，模型路由会成为产品能力，而不该只是一个设置菜单。

每个 AI 产品都应该努力把模型选择从用户面前抽象掉。 在任何产品里，智能是底层引擎，用户只关心任务有没有完成。 让用户挑模型的认知负担太重了。 过去已经有多次尝试想把模型选择抽象掉，我认为我们终于到了这件事可以做好的阶段。 需要三件事： 1/ 深入理解针对具体用例、不同参数规模模型的前沿能力。 2/ 为工作流做好 eval，知道哪里该用哪个模型。 3/ 有人才和 AI-native 的运营方式，能随着前沿变化持续调整。 我和 Lovable 团队合作过，可以说他们三样都有。

English

Meta AI director Madhu Guru says AI products should abstract model choice away from users. Making that work requires three capabilities behind the scenes: knowing the frontier across model sizes, running strong workflow-specific evals, and operating fast enough to adapt as the frontier moves. In other words, model routing becomes a product competency, not a settings menu.

Every AI product should be working towards abstracting model choice away from users. In any product, the intelligence is the underlying engine - all the user cares about is that the job gets done. Model picking is too much cognitive load for the user. There have been multiple attempts to abstract model choice away from users. I think we’re finally at the point where this can work well. It takes three things: 1/ Deep understanding of the model frontier across different weight classes for your particular use cases. 2/ Great evals for your workflows, so you know which models work best where. 3/ A team with the talent and AI-native operating model to continuously adapt as the frontier moves. Having worked with the Lovable team, I can say they have all three.
