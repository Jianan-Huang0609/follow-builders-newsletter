最值得留意的不是模型又聪明了一点，而是应用层开始承担把智能变成结果的脏活。

中文

模型能力和企业最终想自动化的工作流之间存在巨大的鸿沟，这正是应用层 AI 要填补的机会。

你需要把智能接进工作流，重做流程，汇总正确的上下文和数据，设计合适的人机协作体验，推动变革管理，进行领域评测，并管理数据与流程的安全和治理。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that the biggest opportunity in applied AI sits in the chasm between model power and enterprise workflows. Someone still has to connect the right data, redesign processes, handle human-in-the-loop moments, run domain-specific evaluations, and manage security and governance. Better models do not remove this layer; they make more complex automation possible, which makes the layer more important.

There’s a massive chasm between the power of AI models and the ultimate workflows that enterprises are trying to automate. This gap is the opportunity for the applied AI layer to fill.

You need to connect the intelligence to workflows, often reengineer processes, aggregate the right context and data, allow for the right human in the loop experiences, drive change management, do domain specific evals, manage the security and governance of the data and process, and much more.

当任务边界清楚时，最强模型未必是最好的产品答案。

它的输出不是文字，而是概率。因此，在原本需要 Fable 级模型的场景里，它可以高效地充当 judge，但我们的测试显示它快 25 倍，价格低 600 倍。

English

Every CEO's Dan Shipper says a new model they tested does not generate words; it generates probabilities. That makes it useful as a fast, cheap judge for tasks that would otherwise need a much larger model: in their testing, it was 25x faster and 600x cheaper. The opportunity is not always a better writer; sometimes it is a narrowly optimized evaluator.

Build for the Runway / 按生存路径建公司

AI 提高了执行杠杆，也让资本依赖和产品竞争的错误更快暴露。

中文

投资人兼 builder Nikunj Kothari 提醒创始人，不要默认下一轮融资一定会来。资本是加速器，不是公司的氧气瓶：先设计一条没有充沛资金也能活下来的默认路径，再推演未来 6 到 18 个月额外资本会改变什么。

资本是用来加速的武器。用得好，你无人能敌；但如果公司过度依赖资本，一次普通的下行周期就可能带来糟糕后果。

找出一条能保证公司生存、并能成为优秀自循环生意的默认路径。

English

Investor and builder Nikunj Kothari warns founders not to assume the next round will arrive automatically. Capital is an accelerator, not the company's oxygen supply: first build a default path that survives without abundant funding, then model what extra capital would change over the next 6–18 months.

Capital is a weapon for acceleration. Used well you’re unbeatable. If you are so dependent on it, a simple downturn can lead to not so great consequences.

figure out the default path that ensures the survival of your company and a great self fulfilling business.
