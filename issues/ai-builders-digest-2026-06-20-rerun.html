Theme 02

Agent UX Needs Friction / Agent 体验需要一点摩擦

今天几条 builders 的反馈都指向同一个点：真正有用的 AI 工作流，不是把人完全拿掉，而是让人更容易在关键处介入。

中文

Nan Yu 分享了一个非常实用的产品教训：他们一开始以为 agent 可以在很少用户输入的情况下，一次性写出项目更新；结果产出了一堆 slop，因为用户把脑子从写更新这件事里撤走了。

更好的模式是多轮交互。agent 会问你要强调什么、什么最重要、还缺什么上下文。这是一个很好的提醒：好的 AI UX 有时不是减少摩擦，而是加一点摩擦，把人的判断力重新拉回来。

我们迭代了很多次才找到正确模式。一开始很天真，以为 agent 可以在用户很少输入的情况下，一次性生成项目更新。

结果这种方式产生了很多 slop，因为用户把大脑从写更新的过程中撤走了。

切换到多轮交互后，agent 会提示你、问你要强调什么、什么最重要、缺什么上下文等等。

这是一个为了净收益而引入摩擦的好例子。

English

Nan Yu describes a very practical product lesson: they initially hoped an agent could one-shot project updates with little user input, but that produced slop because users disengaged their brains.

The better pattern was multi-turn interaction. The agent asks what to emphasize, what matters most, and what context is missing. This is a nice reminder that good AI UX sometimes adds friction because the friction restores user judgment.

It took us a lot of iterations to find the right pattern for this. Early on we were very naive and we thought that the agent could one shot project updates without much input from users.

That approach ended up creating a bunch of slop, because users disengaged their brain from the process of writing an update.

By switching to a multi turn interactive mode, the agent prompts you and asks you what to emphasize, what's most important, what context is missing, etc.

A very good example of introducing friction for a net benefit.

Theme 03

The AI Compute Stack Gets Physical / AI Compute 变成物理生意

Lambda 这期播客最有价值的地方，是把抽象的 GPU 算力还原成土地、电力、数据中心、网络、存储、软件和金融结构。

SB
Stephen Balaban on The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
Lambda cofounder and CTO · AI compute infrastructure
中文

核心 takeaway：neocloud 不是一门薄薄的 GPU 出租生意。Stephen Balaban 的判断是，它更像垂直整合的基础设施公司，横跨土地审批、建设、高性能集群设计、虚拟化、云软件和融资。

他最有价值的纠偏是：GPU compute 从来就不是真正的 commodity。瓶颈也不只是芯片，而是他说的 land powered shell：有电力兆瓦承诺的土地、数据中心壳体，以及里面的机电管道系统。

Balaban 还把融资逻辑讲得很具体：2023 年部署的 H100，现在租金反而可能比当初更高，因为需求仍然强，GPU 的经济可用寿命也比怀疑者想得更长。他的原话是：我们总体上仍在 underbuilding。

云计算不是 commodity 服务。它是一种非常复杂、高度垂直整合的服务，横跨土地审批、建设、HPC 高性能计算设计、软件、虚拟化，以及上层云服务。

当前主要瓶颈基本上是 land powered shell，也就是获得一定兆瓦电力承诺的土地，然后当然还有数据中心，以及里面的机械、电气、管道设备。

我们总体上仍在 underbuilding。

我们现在把那些 GPU 租出去的价格，比 2023 年刚部署时还要高。

English

The takeaway: neoclouds are not a thin GPU rental business. Stephen Balaban argues they are vertically integrated infrastructure companies spanning land entitlement, construction, high-performance cluster design, virtualization, cloud software, and financing.

His most useful correction is that GPU compute was never really a commodity. The bottleneck is not just chips, but 'land powered shell': land with utility megawatt commitments, the data center shell, and the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing systems around it.

Balaban also makes the financing point concrete: a 2023 H100 can lease for more now than when it was first deployed, because demand remains high and the economic useful life of GPUs is longer than skeptics assumed. In his words, 'We continue to be generally underbuilding.'

Cloud compute is not a commodity service. It is a very complicated, highly vertically integrated type of service that spans everything from land entitlement, construction, HPC, high performance computing design, software, virtualization, cloud services on top.

The thing that is the main bottleneck is basically land powered shell, which is basically land that is entitled to have a certain amount of megawatt commitment from a utility And then, of course, the data center and the mechanical, electrical, and plumbing equipment.

We continue to be generally underbuilding.

We're now leasing those out at a higher rate now than we were originally in 2023.

Open Models, Regulation, and Agency / 开源模型、监管与能动性

模型能力进入政策和组织层面后，讨论也开始从“能不能用”转向“谁能控制、谁能部署、谁能从中获得杠杆”。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 说，open weights 模型开始在这个能力层级被认真讨论，对 applied AI layer 来说应该是一次很大的认知更新。

他的重点不是抽象地为开源欢呼。接近 frontier 水平的开放模型，意味着企业可以拥有 sovereign AI 选项，可以针对具体 workflow 做 post-train，可以按 workload 优化成本，也能负担得起一些原本太贵的 AI 应用。

open weights 模型能在这个能力水平被认真讨论，对很多人来说都应该是一次巨大更新。

开放模型达到 frontier 性能的意义在于：你总能拥有 sovereign AI，能为自己的具体 workflow 做 post-train，能为不同 workload 做成本优化，也能真正负担得起更多 AI 应用，从而打开完全不同的应用空间。

这是 applied AI layer 的巨大胜利。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie says credible open-weights models at this level of capability should be a major update for the applied AI layer.

His point is not ideological open source cheerleading. Frontier-level open models would give companies sovereign AI options, let them post-train for specific workflows, optimize costs across workloads, and afford applications that would otherwise be too expensive.

The fact that open weights models are being discussed credibly at this level of capability should be a huge update for many.

The implications of open models getting to frontier performance ensures that you can always have sovereign AI, have the ability to post train for your specific workflows, cost optimize for various workloads, and actually afford to do much more with AI (which opens up meaningfully different applications).

Huge win for the applied AI layer.
