今天最值得看的是 AI 从实验室走进制度、成本和权限边界后的样子。Aaron Levie 一边讲 Fable 事件暴露出的模型层监管风险，一边指出模型路由会成为应用层的新基础设施；Madhu Guru 则从发布评审角度解释，为什么 frontier model 永远不能像普通软件那样被完全理解后再上线。更长线的一条来自 No Priors 的 Biohub 访谈：AI 改变科学的关键，不只是更强模型，而是开放工具、湿实验数据和能让更多人参与的系统。

Theme 01

Model Governance Gets Real / 模型治理进入现实层

Fable 相关讨论把一个抽象问题变具体了：当监管盯上模型本身，发布节奏、风险判断和创新速度都会被重写。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 把 Fable 出口管制事件看成一次很有用的预演：模型层 AI 监管真正铺开后会是什么样。问题不只是某一个模型有没有风险，而是如果每个重要模型发布都要先经过政府自由裁量，会发生什么。

他的担心是，实验室和监管者可能会围绕每次发布拉扯几个月：模型到底有什么能力、能不能被 jailbreak、风险判断有多主观。结果就是发布堆积，AI 进展变得像一个迟缓僵硬的行业。

Levie 更倾向的答案是：尽可能监管 AI 的危险应用，而不是把模型本身变成主要瓶颈。

整个 Fable 出口管制事件，对监管讨论反而是净正面。它提前展示了当 AI 监管发生在模型层，而不是具体应用层时，规模化之后会是什么样。

政府会对一个模型何时可以向公众发布拥有唯一裁量权，依据是一堆它们本身控制的因素。

所以只要可能，我们应该监管 AI 的应用使用方式。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie reads the Fable export-control situation as a useful preview of model-layer AI regulation. The issue is not just whether one model is risky, but what happens if every major model release needs government discretion before reaching the public.

His concern is that labs and regulators could spend months arguing over capabilities, jailbreakability, and subjective risk judgments for each release. That would create a backlog and make AI progress look like a slow, sclerotic industry.

Levie's preferred answer is to regulate dangerous applied uses of AI wherever possible, instead of making the model itself the primary bottleneck.

This whole Fable export control situation is actually net positive to regulation discourse. It’s an early peek into what AI regulation would end up looking like at scale when enacted at the model layer instead of the specific application of the AI.

The government would have sole discretion over when a model can be released to the to public, based on a bunch of factors that they inherently control.

This is why, wherever possible, we should be regulating the applied use of AI.

Madhu Guru 解释了为什么发布 frontier LLM 在结构上不同于发布传统软件。你放出去的不是一个功能边界清晰的产品，而是一个拥有近乎无限使用场景和失败模式的黑箱。

实验室可以做 eval、red-team、反复比较 checkpoint，但 early-access partners 仍然会发现没被预料到的行为。能做的不是消除不确定性，而是把不确定性降到足够可以发布。

这几乎就是模型治理难题的一段话版本：每次能力提升，都会同时扩大有价值的应用和潜在滥用面。发布决策对实验室和监管者都会越来越难。

发布 LLM 不像发布传统软件，你是在决定是否发布一个有近乎无限用途和近乎无限失败模式的黑箱。

然后 early-access partners 仍然会发现你没预料到的行为。

随着整个行业的 frontier models 变得更聪明，这个决定对实验室和监管者都会更难。

English

Madhu Guru explains why launching a frontier LLM is structurally different from shipping traditional software. You are releasing a black box with effectively infinite use cases and failure modes, not a product with a bounded feature surface.

Labs can run evals, red-team, iterate over checkpoints, and still have early-access partners uncover behaviors they did not anticipate. The best they can do is reduce uncertainty enough to launch, not eliminate it.

That is the hard governance problem in one paragraph: every capability gain increases both useful applications and possible misuse. The launch decision gets harder for labs and regulators at the same time.

Launching an LLM isn't like shipping traditional software - you're making a decision about a black box with effectively infinite use cases and infinite failure modes.

Then early-access partners still uncover behaviors you didn't anticipate.

As frontier models become smarter across the industry, that decision will get harder - for labs and regulators.

Theme 02

Routing Is the New Control Plane / 路由成为新的控制层

当模型之间的成本、能力和可用性差异都很大时，应用层的竞争力会落到调度能力上。

中文

Aaron Levie 说，能把任务路由给最合适 AI 模型的那一层，会明显变得更值钱。他给出三个理由：成本优化、能力最大化、风险缓释。

具体模式很好理解：用 frontier intelligence 做规划和复核，再用更便宜或 open-source 的模型完成大部分执行。这让路由变成 workflow 架构问题，而不是单纯的供应商偏好。

Fable 事件又加了一层韧性视角。如果模型可能因为 provider、政府或安全评审而受限，能在模型之间迁移 workload 就会变成战略保险。

能把具体任务路由到最佳 AI 模型的那一层，价值会大幅上升。

成本优化：很多场景里，你有些任务需要 frontier intelligence，另一些任务用便宜得多的模型就够了。

应用 AI 层能有效在模型之间路由，会越来越成为战略优势。

English

Aaron Levie says the layer that routes a job to the best AI model will become substantially more valuable. He names three reasons: cost optimization, capability maximization, and risk mitigation.

The concrete pattern is easy to imagine: use frontier intelligence for planning and review, then use a cheaper or open-source model for most of the execution. That makes routing a workflow architecture issue, not a vendor preference.

The Fable situation adds a resilience angle. If models can become constrained by providers, governments, or safety reviews, the ability to move workloads across models becomes strategic insurance.

The layer that can route to the best AI model for the particular job is going to increase in value substantially.

Cost optimization: there are plenty of use cases where you need frontier intelligence for some tasks and something far cheaper for others.

Ultimately, it’s going to increasingly be a a strategic advantage for the applied AI layer that they can effectively route between models.

Theme 03

Open Tools for Scientific Work / 科学工作的开放工具

No Priors 的 Biohub 访谈把 AI 基础设施问题放进生物学：没有现成互联网级数据，就必须自己造数据、造工具、造协作方式。

NP
No Priors
Biohub with Mark Zuckerberg, Priscilla Chan, and Alex Rives
中文

核心 takeaway：Biohub 赌的是，生物学需要 frontier AI 和 frontier 湿实验在同一个循环里工作。和语言不同，生物学没有足够多现成的互联网数据，所以新数据必须通过成像、细胞工程、传感器和其他科学方法被创造出来。

Mark Zuckerberg 把 open source 说成一种分发策略：Biohub 不是要独自治愈疾病，而是要更快把工具交到更多科学家手里。Priscilla Chan 补充说，这对罕见病和长尾问题尤其重要，因为传统 biotech 激励机制经常不会优先照顾它们。

Alex Rives 给出的模型证据是 ESM Fold：它从数十亿蛋白序列中学习，预测了超过 11 亿个蛋白结构，并把蛋白和 antibody 设计变成一种涌现能力。下一步是 virtual cell，把 genetic、transcriptomic、proteomic 和 phenotype 层连成科学家可以提问的模型。

我们只是想把工具交给整个科学共同体。

这不是某个地方有一个工厂，你付钱就能生产数据。你真的需要发明新的科学方法。

我们折叠了超过 11 亿个蛋白，并预测了它们的结构。

下一个大挑战是 virtual cell。

English

The takeaway: Biohub's bet is that biology needs frontier AI and frontier wet-lab science in the same loop. Unlike language, biology does not have enough ready-made internet data, so new data has to be created through imaging, cellular engineering, sensors, and other scientific methods.

Mark Zuckerberg describes open source as the distribution strategy: Biohub is not trying to cure diseases alone, but to put tools in more scientists' hands faster. Priscilla Chan adds that this matters especially for rare diseases and long-tail problems that normal biotech incentives often leave behind.

Alex Rives gives the model proof point: ESM Fold learned from billions of protein sequences, predicted structures for over 1.1 billion proteins, and can support protein and antibody design as an emergent capability. The next big step is the virtual cell, linking genetic, transcriptomic, proteomic, and phenotype layers into a model scientists can query.

We just wanna give tools to the whole scientific community.

It's not just like there's some factory somewhere that you can pay to produce the data. You actually need to invent new novel scientific approaches.

We folded over 1,100,000,000 proteins and predicted their structures.

The next big challenge, which I think is, you know, the the virtual cell.
