Theme 03

The Cost of Scale / 规模化的真实成本

前沿 AI 的关键指标正在从纸面性能转向真实 workload、可靠性和恢复能力。

TD
Training Data
Google AI Infrastructure
中文

Google AI 基础设施负责人 Amin Vahdat 解释了为什么 AI data center 正在变成 purpose-built 系统，而不是通用建筑。硬件、供电、散热和网络都要围绕具体 workload 协同设计，因为功耗达到数百千瓦的 AI rack，不能再按普通存储基础设施来规划。

当规模来到 10 万个 accelerator，真正有用的指标不是理论 FLOPS，而是 delivered goodput：经历故障、恢复和协调开销后，系统最终交付了多少真实工作。如果系统不断计算又不断重启，那是 throughput，不是 goodput。

AI data center 很可能会更专用，与硬件共同设计，甚至包括散热和供电分配。

我们最终真正关心的是 workload 交付出来的性能。

看的是 delivered goodput，而不是理论 benchmark throughput。

English

Google AI infrastructure chief Amin Vahdat explains why AI data centers are becoming purpose-built systems rather than generic buildings. Hardware, power distribution, cooling, and networking are being co-designed around specific workloads, because a rack that reaches hundreds of kilowatts cannot be treated like ordinary storage infrastructure.

At 100,000-accelerator scale, the useful metric is not theoretical FLOPS but delivered goodput: how much real work reaches an answer after failures, recovery, and coordination overhead. In his framing, a system that keeps computing but repeatedly restarts is doing throughput, not goodput.

An AI data center likely is gonna be much more purpose built, co-designed with the hardware, even to the point of cooling and power distribution.

What we really care about in the end is what's the performance delivered by workload.

It's delivered goodput, not theoretical benchmark throughput.

Nikunj Kothari describes the uncomfortable reality for seed-stage founders whose products are growing but whose runway is shrinking.
