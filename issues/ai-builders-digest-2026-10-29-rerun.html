Theme 02

The Human in the Loop / 人仍然在环里

AI 越来越会替你执行，越需要重新讨论什么不该被自动化，以及谁拥有系统的理解权。

中文

Ryo Lu 认为，AI 进步不该只用「有多少工作不用我们做了」来衡量，更要看那些原本无法参与的人，现在能做什么。他理想中的 computer 是思维的延伸：把还没想清楚的念头放进去，移动、拆解，直到新的连接出现。

这也意味着，不是每一个困难步骤都该被视为 overhead。有时做出错误版本、重塑系统、弄懂它为什么这样工作，本身就是学习。判断 empowerment 的标准很简单：机器变得更强时，人有没有因此变小？

增强人类智力。计算机有趣，不是因为它能离开我完成工作，而是因为我能用它做什么。机器可以变得更强，而不让人变得更小。不是一个不再需要我们的世界，而是一个我们更有能力塑造的世界。

English

Ryo Lu argues for a larger definition of AI progress: not how much work happens without us, but what becomes possible for the person who could not participate before. His ideal computer is an extension of thought, a place to move ideas around until a better question appears.

That also means not treating every difficult step as overhead. Sometimes making the wrong version, reshaping the system and understanding why it works are how a person learns. His test for empowerment is simple: can the machine become more capable without making the person smaller?

augmenting human intellect when i started making software, i wasn’t dreaming of a factory. i loved that something on a screen could become an extension of my mind. a place to put a thought before i understood it. to move things around until new connections appeared. to make little systems that worked the way i worked, even if it made no sense to anyone else. the computer wasn’t interesting because it could do work without me. it was interesting because of what i could do with it. ... the machine can become more capable without making the person smaller. ... not a world that no longer needs us. a world we have more ability to shape.

Worlds, Models & Creative Leverage / 世界模型与创作杠杆

模型还没有抵达终点，但已经足够让创作者重新想象电影、游戏和个人表达的生产方式。

中文

Peter Yang 认为，world model 现在还处在 GPT-3.5 阶段：demo 很惊艳，但一致性和 gameplay 仍然明显不够。他预计，等它们达到 GPT-5 或 GPT-6 的可靠程度，影响会大得多。

他举的例子不只是更便宜的视觉特效：导演给一帧画面，就能生成完整的巨龙大战；游戏创作者也能少花时间处理画面和声音，把精力放回真正决定游戏好不好玩的核心循环和机制。

我觉得 world model 现在还处在 GPT 3.5 阶段，但不会太久。它们的 demo 很惊艳，却还没真正到位。等它们达到 GPT-5 或 GPT-6 阶段，我认为会对很多行业产生巨大影响：电影里的大特效镜头可以从一帧画面生成完整场景；游戏方面，world model 会让任何人更容易做出画面和声音很棒的游戏，但 gameplay 还没到位。

English

Peter Yang thinks world models are still in their GPT-3.5 era: impressive demos, but obvious limits in consistency and gameplay. He expects a much bigger shift when they reach the reliability of GPT-5 or GPT-6.

His concrete prediction is not just cheaper visual effects. A director could create one frame and generate a full dragon-battle scene; a game maker could spend less time on graphics and sound and more people could build games whose core loops are genuinely their own.

I think world models are still in the GPT 3.5 era, but not for long. Like the old Will Smith eating spaghetti videos, world models are impressive but not quite there yet. But once they hit the GPT-5 or GPT-6 stage, I think they'll have massive implications for a lot of industries: Movies - A big effects shot like a dragon battle takes a VFX studio months of work. With a great world model, a director could create a single frame and have AI generate the full scene from it. Games - From my time working in gaming, I learned the real work is core loops, progression systems, and the mechanics that make a game fun. World models are already good at graphics and sound, but the gameplay isn't there yet. Still, they'll make it much easier for anyone to build their own game that looks and sounds amazing.

AI & I by Every
Fred Graver
中文

核心 takeaway：对创作者来说，AI 最有价值的角色不是替你写，而是一个 virtual writers' room。拿过四座 Emmy 的 filmmaker 和 technologist Fred Graver 从 National Lampoon、电视业一路走到 Disney Imagineering、Twitter 和 conversation design，亲眼经历过每一种新媒介如何改变创作者与观众的关系。

他的做法很具体：准备一份「我是谁」的 writer 文档，再准备一份正在探索的想法文档，让 AI 帮你检验两者的关系、寻找潜在受众，甚至模拟一个 focus group。更大的设想，是把领域经验、个人文化和制作决策放进同一个共享核心，让 AI 减少写作、营销和运营之间的摩擦。

重点不是消灭作者，而是把流水线式生产变成更循环、更协作的过程，让创作者有更多空间发现自己真正想表达什么。

你应该创建一份"我是谁"的 writer 文档，放入你的灵感、喜欢的东西、读过的东西和想做的事；再准备一份正在创作的东西。这里不是让 AI 替你写，而是把 AI 当成一个 virtual writer's room、一群帮助你思考的 intelligence。影视行业还在用 Henry Ford 造汽车的方式做东西，但应该把流水线做成一个圆。

English

The takeaway: AI is most useful to a creative person when it becomes a virtual writers' room, not a machine that writes in their place. Emmy-winning filmmaker and technologist Fred Graver's career moved from National Lampoon and television to Disney Imagineering, Twitter and conversation design, so he has watched each new medium change the relationship between creators and audiences.

His practical setup is unusually specific. Keep a document about who you are as a writer, another about the idea you are exploring, and use AI to test the relationship between the two, identify an audience and simulate a focus group. His larger ambition is to put domain expertise, personal culture and production decisions into one shared core, turning AI into a way to reduce friction across writing, marketing and operations.

The point is not to remove authorship. It is to make the production process more circular and collaborative, so creators can spend more time discovering what they actually mean.

You should create a who I am as a writer document, And you should throw in your inspirations, what you love, what you read, what you aspire to do, all of that. And then you also have a document that this is the thing I'm working on.

None of that is about AI writing for you. Everything is about having AI as kind of a virtual writer's room, as a group of intelligences that can help you think through things.

People in show business still make things the way Henry Ford made cars. There's this assembly line. And if you say to them, No, make it a circle.

Theme 04

Scale, Cost & the Builder Economy / 规模、成本与 Builder 经济

当 agent 真正进入工作流，token、推理成本和模型供给都会从后台问题变成产品设计的一部分。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 预计，agent 消耗的 token 会达到今天人类使用量的 1000 倍，因为它们会进入工作流、处理企业数据、构建软件、保护企业安全。如此规模下，不可能只依赖一个 frontier model：系统需要闭源模型、能力更强的 open weights，以及适合大工作负载的低成本方案。

这不是在讨论哪个模型榜单第一，而是在讨论需求的数量级。如果 agent 成为软件里的默认 worker，推理经济学和模型多样性就会变成核心基础设施。

Agent 消耗的 token 将是今天的 1000 倍，因为它们会运行在工作流里，处理数据、构建几乎所有软件、保护企业安全。要规模化，需要多种 frontier AI 路线：继续推进闭源模型，同时提升 open weights 的能力、安全性，并让它们更适合大规模工作负载。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie expects agents to consume 1,000 times more tokens than people do today because they will operate inside workflows, process company data, build software and secure enterprises. That scale makes a single frontier model insufficient: the stack will need closed models, capable open weights and cheaper approaches for large workloads.

The argument is less about a flashy model leaderboard than about demand. If agents become the default workers inside software, inference economics and model diversity become core infrastructure.

Agents are going to consume 1,000X more tokens than we consume today. Because they’re going to be running in our workflows, processing all of our data, building out nearly all software, securing enterprises, and much more. To do this at scale we will need a variety of approaches at the frontier of AI. This includes continuing to push the boundaries of closed models, while also increasing the capability of open weights models, making them more secure, as well as making them more efficient for large workloads.
