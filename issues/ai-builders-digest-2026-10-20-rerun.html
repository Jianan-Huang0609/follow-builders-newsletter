Theme 01

Compute Becomes a Workforce / 算力开始像一支工作队伍

当 agent 能调用工具、写代码、处理数据并彼此协调，很多过去受限于人工时间的工作，第一次有机会按计算量扩张。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，agent swarm 的关键价值，是把算力变成经济活动里的新变量。网络安全、生命科学、材料研究、交易、QA 和数据处理，很多时候不是缺想法，而是受限于人类能投入多少规划、执行、复核和协调时间。

真正的变化不只是 agent 变多，而是它们可以调用工具、写代码、处理数据，并围绕同一个目标协作。只要模型能力足够，增加 token 和 compute，就有可能替代目前卡住流程的人力小时。

Agent swarm 之所以会非常重要，是因为我们终于能让 compute 成为大量重要经济任务里的关键变量。

当 agent 能调用工具、写代码、处理数据、拥有领域深度并彼此协调时，只要设定正确目标并投入足够 token，就可以开始解决任意复杂的问题。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that agent swarms matter because they turn compute into a new variable for economic work. Cybersecurity, life sciences, materials research, trading, QA, and data processing are often limited less by ideas than by the amount of planning, execution, review, and coordination humans can afford.

The important shift is not simply having many agents. It is that agents can use tools, write code, process data, and coordinate toward a shared goal. If the models are good enough, adding tokens and compute can begin to substitute for the scarce human hours that currently bottleneck these workflows.

Here’s why agent swarms are going to be a very big deal. We can finally make compute a critical variable in a large number of important tasks in the economy which wasn’t possible before.

The breakthrough is that in a world where agents can use tools, write code, process data, have strong domain depth, and can coordinate with each other, you can begin to solve arbitrarily complex problems by setting the right goals and applying enough tokens to the problem.

English

Reflection AI CEO Misha Laskin's account of building Beam is a reminder that frontier open models are not one clever algorithm away. A lab has to assemble talent, data, compute, usable infrastructure, pre-training, and reinforcement learning at the same time; his blunt summary is that you need to get roughly 30 things right.

The strategic bet is that open models and reinforcement learning can make a new lab more capital-efficient while still requiring end-to-end control. Laskin says the field is moving from asking whether models can keep improving to asking where the resulting intelligence creates enough economic value to justify more compute.

You actually have to get 30 things right, and that’s why everything is hard because you have to get 30 things right.
