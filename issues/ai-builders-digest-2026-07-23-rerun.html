New Engineering Intuitions / 工程师的新直觉

当工具足够强大时，工作方式本身变成了需要重新学习的技能。

AK
Andrej Karpathy
@karpathy
中文

Andrej Karpathy 分享了一个他最近常用的 LLM 工作模式：语音漫谈（voice ramble）。当你懒得打字把全部上下文写出来时，往后一靠、切到语音模式，用意识流漫谈 10 分钟——完全混乱、想到什么说什么。然后 LLM 能从这一团乱麻里重建出比你原本想法更清晰的版本。

34K 赞说明这事引起了巨大共鸣。Karpathy 描述的其实是从 prompt engineering 到 mind melding 的转变——人类思考时那种混乱但高带宽的方式，正在变成最优的输入格式。模型不需要你精确，它需要你充分。

Karpathy：我发现 LLM 的一个好用模式是长时间漫谈。有时模型需要更多信息来理解你的目标，但你懒得打字。往后一靠切语音，漫谈 10 分钟，全意识流。LLM 非常擅长重建这些语无伦次的长篇大论，输出比你想的更清晰。

English

Andrej Karpathy shares a pattern he's been using with LLMs: the voice ramble session. When you're too lazy to type out your full context, you lean back, switch to voice mode, and ramble for 10 minutes in stream of consciousness. The LLM then reconstructs your tangled thoughts into something cleaner than what you started with.

34K likes on this is telling. Karpathy is describing a shift from prompt engineering to mind melding—the messy, high-bandwidth way humans actually think is becoming the optimal input format. The model doesn't need you to be precise; it needs you to be thorough.

One pattern I find useful for working with LLMs is a nice long ramble session. Sometimes the LLM needs more bits to understand what you're trying to achieve, but you're too lazy to type them.

I like to lean back, switch to /voice and just ramble for like 10 minutes, total mess, anything goes, full stream of consciousness.

I find that the LLMs are somehow very good at reconstructing long incoherent rambles and often their echo of your own tangle of thoughts comes out quite a bit cleaner than what you started with.

Factory Podcast: The Dark Factory / 黑暗工厂播客

Factory CEO Matan Grinberg 讲述软件工程自动化的终极形态——以及企业为什么会主动退回所有收入来重新开始。

TD
Training Data
Co-founder & CEO, Factory
中文

Factory 联合创始人兼 CEO Matan Grinberg 描绘了一个软件工程的未来图景：「黑暗工厂」——灯关了，机械臂到处在动，代码自己异步构建。他的预测：12-24 个月内，90% 的 AI 编程 token 都会是异步的，由信号触发而非人类指令。

他关于 harness 设计的最反直觉观点：模型与 harness 联合设计是一个陷阱。之前大家都天真地以为「把模型和 harness 一起训，它们会一起变好」——但实际恰恰相反。让一个 harness 暴露在多个模型下，能避免它过拟合任何一个模型的特性。他打了个比方：就像训练 AI 应该用整个互联网的数据，而不是只用一个人的。

他最难忘的商业决策：Factory 收入快做到 200 万美元时，他意识到产品没让开发者真正满意，于是主动把所有收入退给了客户。「没有安慰奖。要么做成，要么没做成。」20 人团队没一个人离开。他把这归功于早期招聘时对使命对齐的极端重视。

关于 token 经济：企业正在从「token 滥用」（用 Opus 查天气）转向智能路由。Factory 的路由器让 CIO 用自然语言写策略——「Pat 只能用 Gemini Flash，COBOL 团队用微调模型，高可靠性任务 OpenAI 生成 + Anthropic 审核。」开源模型现在占他们 token 消耗的两位数百分比且增长迅猛——GLM 5.2「不可思议」，性能和上一代前沿模型持平。

Grinberg：黑暗工厂——灯关了，东西自己运转——这就是软件开发的未来。

如果你一起训模型和 harness，它们会一起变好——这不对。让 harness 适配多个模型才能避免过拟合。

我们主动把所有客户的钱都退了。那是我做过最艰难的决定之一。

GLM 5.2 不可思议。我们一半 token 用开源模型。

English

Matan Grinberg, co-founder and CEO of Factory, describes a future where software engineering becomes a 'dark factory'—lights off, robotic arms everywhere, code building itself asynchronously. His prediction: within 12-24 months, 90% of AI coding tokens will be asynchronous, triggered by signals rather than human prompts.

Grinberg's contrarian insight about harness design: model-harness co-design is a trap. 'If you train the model and build the harness, you're gonna make them better together' was the naive belief—but exposing a harness to multiple models prevents overfitting to any one model's quirks. He compares it to training AI on the whole internet versus just one person's data.

His most memorable business decision: Factory hit nearly $2M in revenue, realized the product wasn't making developers happy, and proactively returned every dollar to customers. 'There's no consolation prize. It's either you do the thing or you don't do the thing.' No one on the 20-person team quit. He credits ruthless hiring for mission alignment.

On the token economy: enterprises are moving from 'token maxing' (using Opus for checking the weather) to intelligent routing. Factory's router lets CIOs write natural-language policies—'Pat gets Gemini Flash, the COBOL team gets the fine-tuned model, the reliability-critical stuff gets OpenAI to generate and Anthropic to review.' Open models now account for double-digit percentage of their tokens and growing fast—GLM 5.2 is 'incredible' and 'just as performant' as frontier-minus-one models.

The idea of a dark factory where the lights are off and things are just happening—that is where software development's going.

If you train the model and you build the harness, you're gonna make them better together. Much to the chagrin of many of my friends at OpenAI and Anthropic, this is not true.

We proactively gave all of those customers their money back. That was one of the most difficult decisions to make.

GLM 5.2 is incredible. Half of our tokens are open to open models.

Madhu Guru：越依赖第二大脑，主脑就越笨。脑子里装着半成品想法和松散线索有巨大价值，潜意识在后台不停工作。
