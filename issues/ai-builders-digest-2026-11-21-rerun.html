Theme 01

Agents Get More Room to Think / Agent 开始获得更大自主权

能力跃迁的关键，不只是回答更快，而是 agent 能在更大的解空间里自己找到方案。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 的判断是，agent 最有价值的用法越来越不是照着人类写好的步骤执行，而是给它一个目标，让它在更大的解空间里自己找到更好的方案。模型能连接的相关知识，已经超过单个人可以长期装在脑子里的范围，所以人的工作会从规定每一步，转向设定方向、决定该给 agent 多大自由度。

这会直接改变产品设计：agent 不再只是听话的界面，而开始成为会探索的协作者，尤其适合金融、法律、生命科学和网络安全这类连问题都很难完整定义的领域。

今年模型能力有了明显跃迁：与其精确告诉 agent 怎么做，不如让它解决那些你自己都很难定义清楚的问题。以前你必须明确告诉 agent 如何设计和开发产品想法，现在 agent 越来越可能给出比你原本想到的更好实现，因为它能在比你更大的解空间里连接信息。最终，我们和 AI 的关系会改变：不再是明确指挥每一步，而是给它目标和足够的自由，让它用最好的方式完成。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that the best use of agents is increasingly not giving them a precise recipe, but giving them a goal they can solve better than we could. As models connect more relevant knowledge than any one person can hold, the human job shifts from specifying every step to setting direction and deciding how much freedom to grant.

That is a meaningful change in product design: agents are moving from obedient interfaces toward exploratory collaborators, especially in finance, legal, life sciences, and cybersecurity where the problem itself is hard to fully define.

This year we’ve had a clear jump in model capability where instead of telling agents precisely what to do, it’s better to use these systems to solve problems you can’t easily define yourself. A year ago you’d need to very clearly tell agents the how to design and develop your product idea, or there was almost no chance of success. Now, increasingly, the agent likely can come up with a better implementation than you’d have thought of because you can have it connect the dots between a far greater solution space than you can. This will be increasingly true in basically every domain of kneeler work. We’re going to see this impact in finance, legal, life sciences, cyber, and many other spaces where no person can hold onto all the available relevant industry knowledge to think of the best solution. Ultimately this is going to reshape our relationship with AI and how we deploy agents in our work. It won’t be about explicitly telling them, but instead giving them goals and enough freedom to pursue them in the best way they can.

Build for the Workflow, Not the Demo / 产品要进入真实工作流

今天几个 builder 的共同提醒是：用户不会为“看起来像 AI”付费，而会为真正改变工作方式的细节留下来。

不要把 Claude Artifacts / ChatGPT sites 发给客户。客户会觉得：“那我自己 prompt Claude/Codex 不就行了？”

English

Zara Zhang gives a sharp warning to AI consultants and product builders: don't hand clients a raw Claude Artifact or ChatGPT site. If the deliverable looks like something the client could have prompted themselves, you have exposed the lack of product value. The wrapper needs to encode judgment, workflow, or execution, not merely display a model response.

中文

Linear COO Cristina Cordova 的核心经验是：公司开始增长，不等于马上该招一个 COO；真正重要的是先找出组织当前能承接的能力。Linear 从 product-led motion 走向更大客户时，先把目标改成一个可解决的近场问题，再用真实的 go-to-market 项目作为候选人的 work trial。

她区分了两种完全不同的销售：让正在成长的公司自然用上你的产品，和说服一家已经信任旧系统的公司把它拆掉重来。后者需要产品优势、客户口碑和组织准备度，不只是把销售目标写得更大。

我通常不认为每家公司都需要 COO。创始人必须真正理解：你需要什么，以及你希望带进团队的人解决什么问题？

好处是，这本身就是工作。你不是给候选人一个和入职后工作无关的虚构项目。

English

The takeaway from Linear COO Cristina Cordova is that scaling is not a license to hire executives early; it is a process of discovering which capability the company can actually support. Linear moved from a product-led motion toward larger customers by setting a solvable near-term goal, then using a real go-to-market project as a work trial for the person who would own it.

Her most useful distinction is between selling to companies that are growing into your product and convincing an existing company to rip out a system it already trusts. The latter requires product strength, references, and organizational readiness, not just a bigger sales target.

I generally don't believe every company needs a COO. ... You have to really understand as a founder, what are the things you need and what are the things you're looking for in the people that you bring onto the team?

The good thing about that is that, you know, that is the job. ... You're not giving someone some fictional project that's not really like representative of what they would do when they join the company.
