Theme 04

Data Becomes the Control Plane / 数据成为控制层

企业 AI 的难题不只是模型，而是找到、理解、保护并激活已有数据。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast / EON co-founders
中文

EON 联合创始人 Ofir Ehrlich 和 Gonen Stein 认为，模型和算力的可替代性越来越高，而一家公司长期积累的数据才是更难复制的资产。眼下的问题是，这些数据分散、昂贵、缺少清晰的映射，而且常常锁在没人完全理解的老系统里。

他们提出的数据底座会对结构化和非结构化数据做映射、分类，补上上下文和访问控制，再接入 AI workflow，同时避免敏感信息被随意暴露。安全问题也变了：agent 是拥有合法权限的非人类行为者，组织需要假设 breach 可能发生，并能以更高速度检测和恢复破坏性行为。

最值得记住的一句话很简单：‘你真正拥有的最有价值的东西，其实就是你的数据。’

你真正拥有的最有价值的东西，其实就是你的数据。

让它成为护城河。

我们需要假设 breach 会发生，无论它是恶意的还是非恶意的，并做好处理和应对。

English

EON co-founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein make the case that models and compute are increasingly replaceable, while a company's accumulated data is the harder-to-copy asset. The immediate challenge is that this data is scattered, expensive, poorly mapped, and often locked inside systems nobody fully understands.

Their proposed data foundation maps and classifies structured and unstructured data, adds context and access control, and connects it to AI workflows without casually exposing sensitive information. The security problem changes too: agents are non-human actors with legitimate permissions, so organizations need to assume breach and be able to detect and recover from destructive behavior at much higher velocity.

The memorable line is simple: ‘the most valuable thing that you have is actually your data.’

the most valuable thing that you have is actually your data.
