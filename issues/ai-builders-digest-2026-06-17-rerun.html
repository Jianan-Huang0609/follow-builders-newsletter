Custom Intelligence Wins / 可定制智能胜出

Levie 今天继续在讲同一个商业判断：模型大小不是全部，能把 intelligence 变成组织自己的东西更关键。

中文

Aaron Levie 转发了一段他认为能看出 AI 未来方向的话：最有意思的不是某一个模型变得更聪明，而是 intelligence 变得越来越可定制。

他的商业判断很清楚：胜出的公司会把自己的独特数据、工作流，以及能把任务路由给最合适模型的智能层组合起来。换句话说，能用模型不是护城河，把 intelligence 变成组织独有的东西，才可能是。

AI 里最有意思的事情，不是某一个模型变得更聪明，而是 intelligence 正变得越来越可定制。赢的公司不一定是拥有最大模型的公司，而是能把 intelligence 变成自己独有东西的公司。

把你的独特数据、工作流，以及一个能把 intelligence 路由给最适合该任务模型的层组合起来，这显然就是未来。

English

Aaron Levie points to a post that he says gives insight into the future of AI: the most interesting thing is not one model getting smarter, but intelligence becoming increasingly customizable.

His business takeaway is clear: the companies that win will be the ones that combine unique data, workflows, and a layer that routes intelligence to whichever model performs the task best. In other words, model access is not the moat; turning intelligence into something uniquely yours might be.

The most interesting thing happening in AI isn't that one model is getting smarter. It's that intelligence is becoming increasingly customizable. RL, Test-Time Compute & Science / RL、推理计算与科学发现

今天的播客把产品层的 agent 讨论拉远了一步：模型为什么能在数学和科学问题上走更长、更反直觉的路。

TM
The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
Podcast
中文

Matt Turck 采访了 OpenAI 的 Dan Roberts，他负责 foundations of reinforcement learning team，主题是为什么 AI 系统正在从“辅助已知工作流”走向“自己做出科学发现”。

最有记忆点的例子是最近的 Erdos 类数学进展：Roberts 说，ChatGPT 能够假设一个大家普遍相信的猜想是错的，沿着一条很长的计算路径坚持这种反直觉判断，并把问题连接到另一个数学领域。这背后需要 persistence、exploration，以及跨领域的专业知识。

Roberts 更大的框架是：RL 帮模型使用 test-time compute。模型会在给最终答案前生成一段很长的、类似草稿纸的思考过程。用他的话说，RL 训练的输出，就是让模型具备“会思考”的能力。

OpenAI 的 Dan Roberts：为什么 AI 现在能做出发现

ChatGPT 能做到的一件事，是假设它是错的。

当你逆着共识做一件反直觉的事时，你必须对自己正在做的事情有很强的信念，才能沿着一条很长的计算路径坚持下去。

我认为 RL 过程的结果可能是，模型随后可以在 test time 思考。

所以 RL 训练的输出，就是让模型具备思考的能力。

English

Matt Turck interviews OpenAI's Dan Roberts, lead of the foundations of reinforcement learning team, about why AI systems are starting to make scientific discoveries rather than just assist with known workflows.

The most memorable example is the recent Erdos-style math work: Roberts says ChatGPT was able to assume a widely believed conjecture was false, stay contrarian for a long calculation path, and connect the problem to another area of mathematics. That required persistence, exploration, and expertise across fields.

Roberts' broader frame is that RL helps models use test-time compute: they generate a long scratchpad-like thought process before producing the final answer. In his words, the output of RL training is the ability to have a model that thinks.

OpenAI's Dan Roberts: Why AI Can Now Make Discoveries

One of the things that CHAT GPT was able to do was assume it was false.

When you go against the grain and do something contrarian like that, you really have to have strong conviction in what you're doing in order to persevere down a really long calculation path.

I think maybe the result of the RL process is that the model can then think at test time.

And so the output of of doing RL training is the ability to have a model that thinks.
