The Harness Is the New Variable / 编排层成为新变量

当任务从几百万 token 涨到几亿，怎么拆活、怎么路由，比模型本身更决定成败。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 断言：harness（编排层）——负责分解工作、在正确的时间把任务路由给正确的模型——即将成为 AI 技术栈中仅次于模型能力的最重要变量。

他的论据是规模：当任务只消耗几十万、几百万 token 时，harness 几乎无关紧要；但当任务跑到几千万、几亿 token 时，你怎么分解工作、怎么路由，就成了准确率与浪费之间的分野。

他的收尾是：这条路我们才刚刚起步，前方是巨大的机会。

不知道这些具体数字能否推广到所有任务，但方向是明确的：harness 即将成为 AI 技术栈中仅次于模型能力的最重要变量。

harness 以最高效的方式分解工作、在正确的时间路由到正确模型的能力，将成为最大化准确率、降低成本的关键变量。

我们实际上还处在这条路的极早期。当任务只消耗几十万或几百万 token 时，harness 没那么重要。但当任务达到几千万、几亿 token 时，它就变成了主要变量。前方是巨大的机会。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie makes the case that the harness — the layer that breaks work down and routes it to the right model at the right time — is about to become the most important variable in the AI stack, right next to model capability.

His reasoning is about scale: the harness barely mattered when tasks consumed hundreds of thousands or millions of tokens, but when tasks run into tens and hundreds of millions of tokens, how you decompose and route work becomes the difference between accuracy and waste.

And his kicker: we're still incredibly early in this journey. Huge opportunity ahead.

No idea if these specific numbers generalize across tasks, but directionally it's clear that the harness is going to become the most important variable -right next to model capability- in the AI stack.

The ability for harnesses to break down work in the most efficient way and route to the right model at the right time is going to be a huge variable for maximizing accuracy and reducing costs.

We're actually still incredibly early in this journey. The harness didn't matter that much when tasks only took hundreds of thousands or millions of tokens. But as we have tasks that take tens of millions and hundreds of millions of tokens, this becomes a major variable. Huge opportunity ahead.

Podcast: Igor Babushkin on River AI / 播客：xAI 联创谈模型开发的未来

从 DeepMind 到 OpenAI 再到 xAI，这位站在模型开发最前线的人，押注个人 AI 与本地硬件。

UL
Unsupervised Learning
Unsupervised Learning Podcast
中文

Igor Babushkin——在 DeepMind 带过 StarCraft 和 AlphaCode 项目、在 OpenAI 参与早期 reasoning 工作、联合创办 xAI 并参与建设 Colossus——在 Unsupervised Learning 播客里讲了他的新公司 River AI 的三个赌注。

一是 River API，一个强化学习与微调服务；二是 personal AI，打破「为平均用户训练」的假设，让 agent 为每个个体差异化适配；三是本地硬件，目标是让前沿推理跑在你家里的一个小盒子上——动机一半是掌控感和隐私，一半是延迟。

他对闭源模型厂商作为企业的最尖锐警告是：如果你公司的独特性全部都在互联网上、可以被爬取，你就处在最危险的位置。

而他最担心的当下风险不是 AI 接管——而是 AI 放大不平等，把人甩在后面。「这是最紧迫的风险。」

我们目前在做三个不同的项目。一是 River API，可以把它想成一个强化学习和微调服务。二是个人 AI 与个性化——如果我们打破那个假设，允许模型为它所服务的每个个体表现出不同行为会怎样。第三个大赌注是硬件——能不能把推理算力带到用户本地。

最糟糕的位置是：你所有的知识都在互联网上。如果你公司独特和特别的一切都已经在那里、可以被爬取，那是一个非常危险的位置。

AI 有可能放大世界上的不平等……我认为这是最紧迫的风险。

English

Igor Babushkin — who led StarCraft and AlphaCode at DeepMind, worked on early reasoning at OpenAI, and co-founded xAI where he helped build Colossus — unpacks his new company River AI on Unsupervised Learning.

Three bets: the River API, a reinforcement learning and fine-tuning service; personal AI that breaks the 'train for the average user' assumption and lets agents adapt to each individual; and local hardware, aiming to run frontier inference on a box in your home. His reasoning for the hardware bet is as much about control and privacy as latency.

His sharpest warning is for closed-source model providers as businesses: if everything that makes your company unique is already on the internet and can be scraped, you're in the most dangerous position.

And the immediate risk he worries about isn't takeover — it's AI amplifying inequality while people get left behind. 'The most immediate urgent risk.'

there are really three different projects that we're working on at the moment. One is the River API, which you can think of as a reinforcement learning and fine tuning service. The second one is around personal AI and personalization — what if we break that assumption and allow the model to behave differently for each individual that it's serving. The third big bet is on hardware — can we bring the inference compute locally to the users.

the worst place you can be in is if all of your knowledge is on the internet. If everything that makes your company unique and special is already out there and can be scraped, that's a very dangerous place to be.

potentially, AI could amplify inequality in the world... I think that's the most immediate urgent risk.
