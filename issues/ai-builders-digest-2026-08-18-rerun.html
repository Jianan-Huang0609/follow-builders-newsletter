中文

Engineering & Craft / 工程与手艺

eval 怎么练、创意工作该交给哪种模型——两条来自一线的实操判断。

中文

Meta AI 高级总监 Madhu Guru（之前在 Google 带过 Gemini、Veo 和 Nano Banana）给出了一个异常务实的「怎么做好 eval」方法论：选一个你非常熟悉的工作流，想办法让它的质量变得可测量。去研究真实 traces——典型用户会有一连串什么样的 prompt，每一步和端到端的好的回答应该长什么样。

然后再把你产品失败的 traces 也抓进来：混乱的工具调用响应、缺失的上下文。有了好 eval 之后，想清楚怎么让它能反复自动运行，并且持续镜像真实流量，因为用户模式会变。这就是「我们有 eval」和「我们的 eval 跟得上现实」之间的区别。

练好 eval 的最好方法：选一个你非常熟悉的工作流，想办法让它的质量可测量。研究真实 traces——典型用户的一系列 prompt、每一步和端到端的好回答应该是什么样。研究你产品失败的地方，创建能捕捉它们的 traces——混乱的工具调用响应、缺失上下文等。有了好的 eval，再想怎么让它易于反复自动运行。然后想怎么让 eval 持续镜像真实流量，因为用户模式会演化。

English

Madhu Guru, Senior Director of AI at Meta (previously led Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana at Google), offers a refreshingly concrete method for getting good at evals: take a workflow you know really well and figure out how to make its quality measurable. Study the actual traces, the sequence of prompts typical users have and what good responses look like at each step and end-to-end.

Then capture the traces where your product fails: messy tool-call responses, missing context. Once you have a good eval, make it easy to run repeatedly and automatically, and keep it mirroring live traffic as user patterns evolve. That's the difference between 'we have evals' and 'our evals track reality.'

The best way to get good at evals is to take a workflow you know really well and figure out how to make its quality measurable. Study the actual traces - the sequence of prompts typical users have, what good responses would look like at each step and for the end to end outcome. Study where your product fails and create traces that capture them - messy tool call responses, missing context etc. Once you have a good eval, think about how you would make it easy to run repeatedly and automatically. Once you have that, think about how you will ensure your eval continues to mirror live traffic as your users patterns evolve.
