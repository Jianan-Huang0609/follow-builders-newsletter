Theme 01

Inference Is the Product / 推理本身就是产品

当模型质量越来越接近，速度、并行性和单位成本开始决定真实体验。

NP
No Priors
Inception co-founder & CEO · No Priors
中文

Stefano Ermon 是 Stanford 教授，也是 Inception 的联合创始人。他押注 diffusion model 会在最重要的地方挑战 autoregressive LLM：推理。理由很直接：autoregressive 生成仍然是串行的、受内存带宽限制，而 diffusion 可以并行处理多个 token，更适合 GPU。

所以速度不只是 benchmark 指标，它会改变 voice agent、RL post-training 的 rollout，以及部署 intelligence 的成本结构。Inception 甚至在自己做 diffusion language model 的 serving engine，因为现有 LLM 基础设施原本就是为另一种生成方式设计的。

Autoregressive 模型在推理时仍然是串行的，必须先生成前面的 token，才能生成第十个 token，这种工作负载并不适合 GPU。

Diffusion model 在推理时可以同时处理多个 token。

English

Stefano Ermon, the Stanford professor who co-founded Inception, is betting that diffusion models can challenge autoregressive LLMs where it matters most: inference. His argument is straightforward: autoregressive generation is still sequential and memory-bound, while diffusion can process many tokens in parallel and map better to GPUs.

That makes speed more than a benchmark metric. It changes voice agents, rollouts for RL post-training, and the economics of serving intelligence. Ermon's company is now building its own serving engine for diffusion-based language models, because the existing LLM stack was designed around a different generation pattern.

Autoregressive models are still sequential at inference time. You cannot generate the tenth token until you've generated everything that comes before it, and that workload does not map well to GPUs.

A diffusion model is built to have, at inference time, a workload where you process many tokens at the same time.

Theme 03

The Cost of Slop / Slop 的代价

AI 生成内容的价值不只取决于产出速度，也取决于它有没有帮助人理解问题。

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 认为，AI slop 正在让“阅读”本身变得不被信任。问题不只在糟糕的代码或嘈杂的 PR：当人们长期被低质量、未经验证的 AI prose 轰炸，就会连认真阅读这件事都开始打折。他举了一个例子：一项性能提升被错误归因于 compiler 改动，实际原因却是算法和数据结构变了。

他的标准很简单，也很高：AI 应该服务于理解世界，增强人的认知和创造力，而不是让自信但未经核验的解释变得更便宜。

Slop 不只是代码和 PR。低质量、未经验证的 AI prose 不断出现，会让人因为疲惫而完全不再重视阅读。

我希望 AI 服务于理解世界，增强人的认知和创造力。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch argues that AI slop is putting reading itself at risk. The problem is not limited to bad code or noisy PRs: after enough low-quality, unverified AI prose, people start discounting the act of reading. He points to a performance improvement wrongly attributed to a compiler change, when the real cause was a change in algorithms and data structures.

His standard is simple and demanding: AI should serve understanding of the universe and enhance human cognition and creativity, not make confident explanations cheaper than careful thinking.

Slop grenades are not just code and PRs. We run a real risk that reading gets entirely discounted, because of the exhaustion of getting low-quality and unverified AI prose thrown at you all the time.

I want AI in the service of understanding the universe and enhancing human cognition and creativity.
