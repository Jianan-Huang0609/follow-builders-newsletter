Agent Infrastructure & Compute / Agent 基建与算力

当并行算力可以随手拉满、agent 能握着 KVM 去测真实硬件时，瓶颈已经从算力转移到了编排。

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 用一句话点出了 agent 基础设施的方向：「无限 agent 算力——每分钟 10,000 并发 + 5,000 个 CPU 核心，而且这些配额还能往上提。」

潜台词是：agent 的并行能力正在变成可以随手调大的商品，而不是需要精打细算的稀缺资源。当一次部署就能拉起 10,000 个并发 agent 跑，瓶颈就从算力转移到了编排：谁能真正让这么多 agent 协同干活、干出有用的事。

无限 agent 算力：每分钟 10,000 并发 + 5,000 CPU 核心，配额可继续提升

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch dropped a one-liner that sums up where agent infrastructure is heading: 'Infinite agent compute. 10,000 concurrent + 5,000 CPU cores per minute, and these quotas are raisable.'

The message: raw parallelism for agents is becoming a commodity you can dial up, not a scarce resource you ration. When a single setup can spin up 10,000 concurrent agent runs, the constraint shifts from compute to orchestration: who can actually keep that many agents coordinated and doing useful work.

Infinite agent compute 10,000 concurrent + 5,000 CPU cores per minute and these quotas are raisable

The Next Hit AI Product Will Be Social / 下一个爆款 AI 产品是社交的

消费级 AI 的下一个赢家不会只是更聪明的文本框——它需要信任、关注和地位系统。

ST
Sarah Tavel（Benchmark 合伙人，Pinterest 早期成员）
AI & I by Every
中文

一句话结论：下一个爆款 AI 产品不会是又一个单机版文本框，而是一个多人在线、有社交属性的体验，而且会由某个站在终于稳定下来的模型之上的人造出来。

Benchmark 合伙人 Sarah Tavel（Pinterest 早期成员）用消费科技史来读 AI 浪潮：Google 是 95% 的技术基础设施，然后是 Facebook，再到 Pinterest/Snap/Instagram 时代——CEO 们根本不是工程师，而是产品天才。ChatGPT 和 Character AI 到今天还是「94% 后端」，是「一个能力惊人但不懂社交的团队」做出来的。她说现在的 Custom GPT/Gems 状态「criminal」：你看不到创作者放了什么文档、写了什么 prompt，没有信任、没有关注、没有地位系统。

她最尖锐的预判是：「我们和 AI 的对话，终将超过和真人的对话。」而且个人向 AI 的战场可能根本不是 ChatGPT 的：人们会把「工作向」（ChatGPT/Claude）和「个人向」分成两个桶，后者用来放血检报告、家庭信息。「也许这最终会反过来蚕食 ChatGPT。」

对 builder 来说，机会缺口很明确：一个 prompt 的 UGC 社区——能关注真正擅长的人、基于权威的搜索、靠透明建立信任、再用一套地位系统（Eugene Wei 那套）维持活跃。

Custom GPT 和 ChatGPT 在我看来简直「criminal」——一个能力惊人但不懂社交的团队做出来的东西。

我们和 AI 的对话，终将超过我们和真人的对话。

也许这最终会反过来蚕食 ChatGPT。

English

The Takeaway: the next breakout AI product won't be another single-player text box. It'll be a multiplayer, social experience, and someone is going to build it on top of models that are finally stable enough.

Benchmark partner Sarah Tavel, who joined Pinterest early, reads the AI wave through consumer tech history: Google was 95% technical infrastructure, then came Facebook, then the Pinterest/Snap/Instagram era where CEOs were product geniuses rather than engineers. ChatGPT and Character AI are still '94% backend,' built by teams that are 'unbelievably capable but isn't social.' She calls the current custom GPTs and gems state 'criminal': you can't see what documents or prompts a creator used, there's no trust, no following, no status system.

Her sharpest prediction: 'We're all gonna have probably more conversations with an AI than we do with people in our lives.' And the personal-AI space may not be won by ChatGPT at all. People will keep a 'worky' bucket (ChatGPT/Claude) and a 'personal' bucket where they dump blood tests and family info. 'Maybe this actually ends up cannibalizing ChatGPT over time.'

For builders, the actionable gap is clear: a prompt UGC community where you can follow people who are genuinely good at this, with authority-based search, trust built through transparency, and a status system (Eugene Wei style) to keep it alive.

Custom GPTs and chat GPT feels criminal to me. It's clearly made by a team that is unbelievably capable but isn't social.
