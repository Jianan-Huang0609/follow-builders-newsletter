Theme 01

Agents Become Systems / Agent 开始成为系统

下一代 agent 不只回答问题，而是围绕目标设计、执行、测试和持续优化结果。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，agent 正在进入下一阶段：从快速给答案，到在任务集合里被编排，再到很快可以只接收目标，而不是逐步指令。

这类系统会自己设计方案、测试、部署，再根据持续执行的结果不断优化。工程领域可能最先成熟，但客户 onboarding、风险控制和安全也会被同一套模式改写。

我们很快就能给 agent 一组目标，让它自己设计最好的方案，并根据它所构建的东西持续执行的结果不断改进。

这些自主系统可以接收目标、设计最优结果、测试和迭代、部署，然后持续优化它构建的东西。

English

Aaron Levie, CEO of Box, sees agents moving through another level of capability: from quick answers, to orchestrated task sets, and soon to systems that receive goals instead of step-by-step instructions.

Those systems could design a solution, test and deploy it, then keep improving it from ongoing execution. Engineering will likely lead, but the same pattern could improve client onboarding, risk reduction, and security across knowledge work.

We’ll soon be able to give agents a set of goals, and they can go out and design the best solution for that goal, while also continuously improving it based on the ongoing execution of whatever it built.

These autonomous systems will be able to take on a set of goals, design the best outcome, test and iterate, deploy, and then optimize whatever it built on an ongoing basis.

IZ
Ivan Zhao
Notion Founder · AI & I by Every
中文

Notion 创始人 Ivan Zhao 的核心判断是：AI 让 UI 和 procedural logic 更容易被替换，于是数据、上下文和语义反而更有价值。Notion 越来越像是在保管这些 context，把它开放给不同 agent，同时为人类团队提供协作护栏。

他支持 vibe coding，把它看成软件重新成为人人都能书写的媒介；但他也区分了「有权修改工具」和「一个经过充分思考、值得直接使用的好产品」。

真正重要的是数据。你如何保存数据？如何保存 context 和 semantics？对你、对你的业务来说，什么才是重要的？

AI agent 同时打开了这两件事……你可以 vibe code 一个 UI，让它做任何你想让它做的事。所以某种程度上，我们并不依附于那个 UI。

English

Notion founder Ivan Zhao's durable thesis is that AI makes interfaces and procedural logic easier to replace, which makes data, context, and semantics more valuable. Notion's role is increasingly to hold that context, open it to different agents, and give human teams guardrails around it.

He is enthusiastic about vibe coding as a return to software as a medium everyone can write, but draws a line between freedom to modify tools and the value of a well-crafted product someone else has thought through.

What’s really important is the data. How do you hold your data? How do you hold your context, your semantics? What’s important to you, to your business?

AI agent opened up both of them... You can vibe code a UI, whatever you want them to do. So in some sense, we are not married to that.

The Product Boundary / 产品边界重新出现

能生成软件，不等于知道什么软件值得被长期使用。

T
Thariq
@trq212
中文

前 Claude Code 成员 Thariq 不太相信大多数人最终会自己生成软件。理由很简单：好软件是一组强烈的产品判断，而多数人更愿意使用别人已经认真想过的东西。

这不是反对 AI coding，而是在提醒：生成只是产品设计的一部分，决定什么应该存在、什么应该被删掉，依然很难。

这也是我不认为大多数人真的会生成自己的软件的原因之一。

好软件是一组强烈的意见，我更愿意使用别人真正认真想过的东西。

English

Thariq, formerly of Claude Code, pushes back on the idea that everyone will generate their own software. His reason is simple: good software is a set of strong opinions, and most people would rather use something that another person has thought through deeply.

That is not an argument against AI coding. It is a reminder that generation is only one part of product design; deciding what should exist, and what should be left out, remains hard.

this is one of those reasons why I don't think most people will really generate their own software

good software is a set of strong opinions and I'd rather have something that's been really thought through by someone else

Infrastructure and Economics / 基础设施与经济账

Agent 的规模化不仅需要新的协作原语，也会把 compute、token 和供应商整合推到台前。

中文

Guillermo Rauch 说，平均每两天就有人向他推介一家 inference neocloud；与此同时，Vercel AI Gateway 的 token 需求自年初以来按月复合增长约 40%。

他的解释是：compute 正成为新的经济层，token 则像新的能源单位。短期还会继续扩张，之后会因为规模效应、安全、企业要求、资本和监管而进入整合。

自年初以来，Vercel 上 AI Gateway 的 token 需求按月复合增长约 40%。

一种解释是，compute 是新的经济，token 是新的能源单位。

English

Guillermo Rauch says he is being pitched an inference neocloud roughly every two days, even as Vercel's AI Gateway token demand has compounded at about 40% month over month since the beginning of the year.

His reconciliation is that compute is becoming a new economic layer and tokens a new energy unit. Expect more expansion first, then consolidation as scale, security, enterprise requirements, capital, and regulation start to matter.

AI Gateway token demand on Vercel has been ~40% MoM compounded since the beginning of the year.

One way to reconcile this is that compute is the new economy, and tokens the new energy unit.
