Theme 03

Agents Need Product Systems / Agent 要接入产品系统

企业级 agent 的核心不是一个聊天框，而是指标、知识、工具和反馈回路组成的可观察系统。

中文

Madhu Guru 认为，每家公司都应该开始构建 self-improving products。基础设施包括清晰的 primary、secondary 和 guardrail 指标，记录过去产品决策的知识库，连接 dashboards、API、MCP 和内部工具，以及理解端到端研发流程的 harness。区别在于：这不只是加一个 agent，而是在做一个会学习的产品反馈回路。

Self-improving products 是每家公司都应该实施的元想法。核心组成包括：清晰定义 primary、secondary、guardrail 指标；说明战略、路线图和最重要的指标；保存过去的产品决策和原则；连接 dashboards、API、MCP 和 agent 需要观察、操作产品的工具；以及了解端到端产品开发流程的 harness。

English

Madhu Guru argues that every company should be building self-improving products. The stack includes crisp primary, secondary, and guardrail metrics; a record of product decisions; connections to dashboards, APIs, MCPs, and tools; and a harness that understands the end-to-end product process. This is the difference between adding an agent and creating a learning product loop.

Self-improving products is a meta idea every company should be implementing. Some of the core pieces you will need: 1/ a crisp definition of the various metrics you care about : primary, secondary, guardrail 2/ an articulation of your strategy, roadmap and metrics you care about moving the most 3/ knowledge base of past product decisions and principles so the agent knows how decisions get made 4/ connections to your to your internal product systems: dashboards, APIs, MCPs, and tools your agent needs to observe and act on your product. 5/ a harness built with knowledge of your end to end product development flow.
