编者导语 / Editor's Note

今天的主线不是又一个模型发布，而是组织和个人开始重新安排自己的工作方式：有人把追踪 AI 前沿从全公司的负担，变成一个有边界的 Frontier team；有人让 agent 直接进入安全、代码审查和个人工作流。更有意思的是，大家谈的已经不只是“AI 能不能做”，而是怎么让它持续被采用、可审计，并且不把所有人的注意力一起拖走。

Theme 01

A Team for the Frontier / 给前沿单独设一队

Every 的经验很具体：让少数人负责追新、做实验和建立评测，其他人才能专心把已经验证的东西做成可靠产品。

AI & I by Every
Every
中文

核心结论是：公司需要一支 Frontier team，让那些喜欢追新模型的人去做实验，同时不把所有人的工作都变成一场永远开不完的 AI 更新会。Every 给出了一个很实用的分工：pirates 快速试一百个粗糙想法，architects 再把真正有用的部分压缩成全公司能维护的系统。

这支队伍还把 eval、token 花费和落地采用都算进工作本身。目标不是堆 demo，而是让少数实验进入产品和服务，同时不让组织被信息淹没。Dan Shipper 的说法很准确：Frontier team 负责跟上模型变化，其他人负责使用他们学到的东西。

之所以需要 Frontier team，是因为它把事情分开了：一边跟上前沿发生了什么，一边真正把手头的工作做完。

Pirates 是那种只想快速推进的人，哪怕过程有点粗糙，也要在几天里试上一大堆东西。

Architects 擅长面对一团混乱，问一句：怎样把它压缩、塑形成一个真正能用、能维护的东西？

English

The takeaway: companies need a Frontier team so the people who love chasing new models can experiment without turning everyone else's job into a permanent AI update meeting. Every describes a useful division of labor: pirates try a hundred messy things quickly, while architects compress the winners into systems the rest of the company can actually maintain.

The team also treats evals, token spend, and adoption as part of the work. The goal is not to accumulate demos, but to move a small number of experiments into products and services without flooding the organization with context. As Dan Shipper puts it, the Frontier team's job is to keep up with models; everyone else's job is to use what they learn.

The reason why you need a frontier team is it separates things out. So you get this nice separation of concerns where you're both keeping up with what's going on on the frontier and you're actually getting your work done.

Pirates are people like me who are just like, I just want to go really fast and I'm going to do stuff that's kind of be a little bit sloppy, but I'm going to try like a billion things in the space of a couple days.

Architects are people who are really good at being like, there's a whole big messy thing what would it look like to compress it a little bit and form it into a shape that actually works, that we can actually use?

Security Becomes Agent Work / 安全变成 agent 的主战场

当 agent 可以扫描更多日志、代码和流量，攻防双方都获得了新的规模；安全系统和安全人才因此都不会被 AI 时代淘汰。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，网络安全会成为 AI 最深远的影响之一，而不是一个边缘分类。agent 可以检查过去无法承受的海量日志、代码和网络流量，攻击者也会用同样的杠杆更快找到漏洞。

他的实际判断有点反直觉：安全工作越自动化，越需要人来把 agent 指向最重要的问题、复核发现、修补系统和响应事故。新阶段不是人和 agent 二选一，而是一条规模巨大、由 agent 驱动、必须被治理的攻防循环。

网络安全会成为 AI 最深远的影响之一。我们现在拥有了一支不知疲倦的劳动力，可以搜寻、发现、修补或攻击任何曾经存在的系统和软件。

我们正在进入一个新的网络安全阶段。因为可以扫描每条日志、每行代码、所有流量，以前无法想象的规模处理数据，这个循环消耗的 token 可能会达到我们现在想象的 100 倍。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie sees cybersecurity becoming one of the deepest consequences of AI, not a side category. Agents can inspect every log, line of code, and network trace at a scale that was previously impossible, while attackers get the same leverage to discover weaknesses faster.

His practical implication is counterintuitive: more autonomous security work will create demand for more people who point agents at the highest-value problems, review findings, patch systems, and respond to incidents. The new regime is not people versus agents; it is an enormous agent-powered loop that enterprises must learn to govern.

Cyber is going to be one of the most profound aspects of AI. We now have a tireless workforce that can hunt down, discover, patch, or attack any system and software ever created.

We’re entering a new cyber regime, and we’ll probably use 100X more tokens on this loop than we’re imagining right now, because we can scan every log, look at every line of code, look through traffic, and process data at a scale that wasn’t comprehensible before.

The Personal Build Loop / 个人建造循环

AI 的价值越来越像一种直接建造的能力：孩子、设计师和 developer 都在从“使用软件”转向“做出自己的软件”。

中文

Aditya Agarwal 注意到人们和软件的关系正在变：他的儿子看了大约 3 小时《星球大战》，却花了约 20 小时把看到的东西做成游戏，之后反而没怎么玩。真正有趣的是建造本身。

程序员对这种快乐很熟悉。AI 改变的是，过去被挡在编程门外的人也可能进入这个循环。最终产物不一定是游戏或 App，有时只是“我能把一个想法变成能工作的东西”的感觉。

我还在消化这件事……但感觉用 AI 做东西，可能和使用东西一样有趣。我的儿子看了大约 3 小时《星球大战》，然后花了大约 20 小时制作关于它的游戏。但奇怪的是，他只玩了一小会儿，更感兴趣的是把游戏做出来。作为程序员，我能直觉地理解这一点：编程本身就是有趣的。也许现在每个人都能体验这种快乐？

English

Aditya Agarwal notices a subtle shift in how people relate to software: his son spent around 20 hours making Star Wars games after watching the film for three hours, but barely played the games afterward. The building itself was the point.

For programmers, that joy is familiar. What changes with AI is that the loop can now be available to people who were previously locked out of programming. The product is not always the game or app; sometimes it is the feeling of being able to turn an idea into a working thing.

I'm still grokking this...but it does feel like it just as fun to make stuff with AI as it is to "use" it. My son watched Star Wars (EP IV) for ~3 hours...but then spent ~20 hours making games about what he had seen. But weirdly, he only played the games a little bit...he was more interested in just building the game. As a programmer, I understand parts of this intuitively...the very act of programming is fun (most of the time). And I have always felt bad for people who couldn't experience that joy... But maybe everyone will now get to experience that?

When AI Infra Gets Specific / AI 基础设施开始变专用

模型能力之外，真正决定工作流能否跑起来的，是安全、成本和足够贴近场景的基础设施。

中文

Guillermo Rauch 把 DeepSeek v4.1 Flash 定位成一种便宜、快速、带 zero-data retention 的 subagent 模型，已经包含在 Vercel 的 Token Pass 里。这个用法很说明问题：让更大、更贵的模型负责设计计划，再让便宜的 worker 执行重复性的转换任务，不把预算烧在每一步上。

这比“一个模型包打天下”更接近真实的模型编排：未来的 stack 可能是一套模型层级，每个模型都在能力和价格恰好匹配的位置上工作。

DeepSeek v4.1 Flash (1017) 现在包含在 Token Pass 里。速度快、便宜得惊人，并且使用 ZDR（zero-data retention）服务。它非常适合运行执行大型、昂贵模型所制定计划的 subagents。你可以把 TypeScript 转 Rust 十次，还用不完 credits。

English

Guillermo Rauch is positioning DeepSeek v4.1 Flash as a cheap, fast model for subagents, with zero-data retention included in Vercel's Token Pass. The use case is telling: let a larger, more expensive model design the plan, then have inexpensive workers execute repetitive transformations without burning through the budget.

That is a more grounded picture of model orchestration than “one model does everything.” The winning stack may be a hierarchy of models, each assigned work at the price and capability level where it makes sense.

DeepSeek v4.1 Flash (1017) is now included in ▲ Token Pass. Fast, mind-bogglingly cheap, and served with ZDR (zero-data retention). It's a phenomenal model to run subagents that execute plans conceived by larger and more expensive models. You can port TypeScript to Rust 10 times and not run out of credits 😂
