Theme 01

Agents in the Background / 后台运行的 Agent

下一步不是和一个 agent 来回聊天，而是把一群 agent 派出去，等它们带着结果回来。

中文

Aaron Levie 认为，从和 agent 来回聊天，转向让 agent 在后台自行工作，token 消耗可能会达到今天的 100 倍甚至 1000 倍。知识工作的瓶颈一直是搬运数据、审阅文档、检查代码和处理日志，而 agent 可以把这些任务拆开并行执行。

关键变化是从“辅助”走向“委托”：人、工作流、触发器和其他 agent 都可以启动 agent swarm，去保护代码库、审查每次变更、检查文档风险，甚至处理过去根本不会做的新工作。

从聊天转向让 agent 在后台做有用的工作，token 消耗可能是今天的 100 倍或 1000 倍。

这些 agent 会由人、工作流、触发器和其他 agent 以 swarm 的方式部署。

English

Aaron Levie argues that moving from back-and-forth chat to agents running in the background could mean 100X or 1000X more token consumption. The bottleneck in knowledge work is still moving data, reviewing documents, auditing code, and processing logs; agents can turn those individual tasks into parallel work.

The important shift is from augmentation to delegation. People, workflows, triggers, and other agents will spin up swarms to protect codebases, QA every change, audit documents, and tackle net-new work. The remaining constraint is no longer imagination but enough compute.

Moving from chatting back and forth with agents, to having agents that you spin up in the background to do useful work for you is probably on the order of 100X or 1000X more token consumption than what we’re seeing today.

These agents will get deployed as swarms by people, by workflows, by triggers, and by other agents.

Jev 的叙事不是“更会写字的模型”，而是把 AI 变成软件里的决策层。

DA
Diogo Almeida
CEO, TypeSafe
中文

他的反直觉观点是，实用自动化的起点是先定义任务，再扩大模型规模。Jev 用 open-weight models 构建，目标是把 machine-native intelligence 放进邮件自动化等工作流。标准也很直白：“Build prod, not God。”

我认为未来主要会看 intelligence per dollar。

Classification is AI-complete。

Build prod, not God。

English

The Jev thesis is simple but consequential: AI does not have to generate text to be useful. TypeSafe's model is built to make decisions inside software, with probabilities, thresholds, and developer control instead of one giant prompt. Diogo Almeida frames the goal as increasing “intelligence per dollar,” not just intelligence per second.

His contrarian point is that practical automation starts by defining the task before scaling the model. Jev was built in stealth, uses open-weight models, and aims at machine-native intelligence for workflows such as email automation. The memorable standard is: “Build prod, not God.”

What Models Know vs. What They Plan / 认识概念不等于理解意图

能识别一个概念，只说明模型看到了它；不说明我们知道它接下来想做什么。

中文

Dan Shipper 反驳了 interpretability 里一个常见的捷径：找到一个遇到埃菲尔铁塔就亮起的 neuron，并不等于知道模型接下来打算做什么。识别一个概念和理解模型的意图不是一回事，漂亮的 feature-level 解释反而可能让人对 alignment 产生虚假的信心。

知道它能识别埃菲尔铁塔，并不能告诉你它到底在计划什么。

English

Dan Shipper pushes back on a common shortcut in interpretability: finding a neuron that lights up for the Eiffel Tower does not tell you what a model is planning. Recognizing a concept is not the same as untangling the model's intent, and a neat feature-level explanation can create false confidence about alignment.

Knowing it recognizes the Eiffel Tower doesn’t tell you what the fuck it’s planning.
