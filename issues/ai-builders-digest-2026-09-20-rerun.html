Theme 01

Agents Become the New User / Agent 成为新用户

当 agent 可以替用户完成一整段流程，产品需要争取的注意力就不只来自人，也来自能否被 agent 调用。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，personal agent 把「做用户想要的东西」改写成了「做 agent 想要的东西」。产品必须让 agent 接手任务、操作工具或 CLI、完成交易，并把整件事端到端做完。未来被更多使用的，可能是最容易让 agent 下单、订票、接入数据的服务。

Personal agents are the ultimate manifestation of “build something that agents want”.

新的竞争注意力不再只来自用户本身，而是来自 agent。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that personal agents turn ‘build something users want’ into ‘build something agents want.’ A useful product must let an agent take a task, navigate tools or a CLI, transact, and finish the job end to end. That changes the competitive surface of consumer software: the winners may be the services easiest for agents to order from, book through, and connect to.

Personal agents are the ultimate manifestation of “build something that agents want”.

The new attention you need to compete for is not from the user itself but instead for the agent.

AI 变化太快，旁观者很难只靠听别人讲来跟上；持续做小项目，正在成为 builder 和投资人共同的学习方法。

中文

Nikunj Kothari 解释了自己身为 VC 还每天写代码的原因：读论文能判断模型能力会往哪里走，但亲手 build 才能看见哪些工具和 harness 真能让模型完成长链路任务。他的建议很实际：连续十天每天动手，用一手体验替代二手判断。

我每天花约两小时写代码、做 side project、沉迷 tinkering、尝试新 repo。

你需要看它们需要什么工具来完成更长的 long horizon 任务（infra）。

Nikunj Kothari explains why he spends about two hours a day programming despite being a VC: reading papers shows where models may improve, but building reveals which tools and harnesses let them complete long-horizon work. His practical advice is to tinker daily for ten days, replacing second-hand opinions with direct contact.

I spend ~2 hours a day programming, building side projects, tinkering obsessively, trying new repos..

RS

The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck

任何能被 simulation 的东西，AI 都会解决。然后你很快就会进入 recursive self improvement loop，我们相信这会带来巨大的突破。
