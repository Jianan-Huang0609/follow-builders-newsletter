Review Becomes the New Discipline / 审查成为新纪律

Boris Cherny 指出 LLM 的 bug 已经换了形态——从 off-by-one 变成系统设计与上下文缺失——而对抗式审查是目前最有效的捕虫工具。

让开源权重模型在无聊但具体的业务领域做到出色，会有一大笔钱。选一个规模×领域组合然后做深：中型法律、中小零售、企业物流。

云厂商有底层能力，但做不深领域纵深、缺乏钻劲和意愿去把某一类业务做到极致。那就是你的机会。

English

Meta's Senior Director of AI Madhu Guru sees a big open lane: 'a ton of money' in making open-weight models exceptional at boring, specific business domains. His recipe is concrete—pick a size × domain combination and go deep: mid-market legal, SMB retail, enterprise logistics.

His argument: the hyperscalers have the primitives but will struggle with domain depth, scrappiness, and the sheer will to make a model exceptional for one kind of business. 'And that is your opportunity.'

Physical AI / 物理世界的 AI

硅谷都在聊数字世界的 agent 时，Samsara 的 CEO 讲了一个 200 亿美金公司的故事：最大的 AI 部署其实在物理世界——每年 25 万亿个数据点、跑过 99% 的美国公路、一年避免 38 万起车祸。

SB
Sanjit Biswas
The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
中文

没人谈论的最大 AI 部署不在软件里，而在物理世界——公路、工地和电网。Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas 管这叫「物理世界最大规模的 AI 部署」：数百万辆车、每年 25 万亿个数据点、每天跑过 99% 的美国公路，去年帮助避免了约 38 万起车祸。公司刚跨过 20 亿美金 ARR，盈利且保持 30% 增长。

为什么重要：物理运营占了世界 GDP 的 40–50%，但和数字世界不同，它没有几十年积累下来的「比特」可以 tokenize。「这些不是你在网上能找到的 token。你不能靠爬 Reddit 知道工地上发生了什么。」这就是价值被锁住的地方——也是为什么他说，AI 热潮本质上是一场基础设施建设工程。

变化在哪：过去二十年行业做的是「报告」（给你一张表看），现在 AI 能对数据推理，而 agentic AI 可以直接行动——比如因为纽约下雨，自动把整个车队的跟车距离调大，这种事以前必须有个人盯着。他对未来五年的判断：重复性的野外工作（比如凌晨把一段路整平）交给机器人，人类负责判断和处理例外。

这些不是你在网上能找到的 token。你不能靠爬 Reddit 知道工地上发生了什么。

每年 25 万亿个数据点……数百万辆车……我们相信去年帮助避免了约 38 万起车祸。

过去二十年是做「报告」：怎么把数据变成一张能看的表……而最近两三年最棒的是，AI 终于能推理这类信息了。

English

The biggest AI deployment nobody talks about isn't in software—it's on the roads, construction sites, and grids of the physical world. Samsara CEO Sanjit Biswas runs what he calls the largest AI deployment in the physical world: millions of vehicles, 25 trillion data points a year, driving 99% of US roads daily. It helped prevent roughly 380,000 crashes last year. The company just crossed $2B ARR, profitable and growing 30%.

Why this matters: physical operations are 40–50% of world GDP, but unlike the digital world they have no decades of bits to tokenize. 'These are not the tokens you're gonna find online. You can't crawl Reddit and find out about what happened on a construction site.' That's where the value is trapped—and why, as he puts it, the AI boom is really an infrastructure construction project.

The shift: two decades of reporting (tables to look at) are giving way to AI that reasons over the data, and now agentic AI that acts—like automatically increasing following distance across an entire fleet in New York because it's raining, work that used to require a human in the loop. His five-year view: robots take the repetitive field work (grading a road at midnight) while humans handle judgment and exceptions.

These are not the tokens you're gonna find online. Like, you can't crawl Reddit and find out about what happened on a construction site.

We're talking about 25,000,000,000,000 data points... Millions and millions of vehicles... We believe we help prevent about three hundred and eighty thousand car crashes, road accidents in the last year.

The last, call it, two decades was around reporting. How do we ingest the data and give you a really cool table so you can look at it and reason about it... Now what's awesome really in the last two, three years is the AIs are able to reason about this kind of information.
