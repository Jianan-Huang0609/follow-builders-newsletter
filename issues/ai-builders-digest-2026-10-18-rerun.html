Theme 02

Recursive Improvement / 递归改进开始变得具体

模型不只是在被人使用，也开始参与训练、测试和改进下一代模型。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，递归自我改进可能不是某个科幻时刻突然降临，而是先通过工程上的反馈飞轮变得可行：模型能力超过多数人类工程师，长时运行 agent 的 harness 和基础设施成熟，再加上更多数据与大规模并行试验，AI 就能探索远超人类工程师数量的训练方案。这个判断的实际含义是：如果你的预测没有计入 RSI 带来的加速，可能会低估接下来的变化。

我们已经到了这样一个门槛：AI 很快就能比人类更擅长训练模型。前一天递归自我改进还感觉很遥远，第二天我们已经看到了它可能是什么样子。 模型能力终于超过大多数人类工程师，长时运行 agent 的 harness 和基础设施在进步，数据越来越多，再加上可以并行运行大量 agent，去尝试远超任何个人能力的任务、测试和方法，形成了一个惊人的飞轮。 这个飞轮几乎肯定会让模型进步速度远超以往，或者至少以惊人的速度抬高 frontier 的上限。如果你没有把 RSI 会带来的影响计入判断，那可能是个错误。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that recursive self-improvement may arrive through an engineering flywheel before it arrives as a dramatic sci-fi moment. Better-than-human model capability, long-running agent harnesses, more data, and massive parallelization let AI explore more training ideas and tests than human engineers can. His warning is practical: ignoring this acceleration may leave forecasts badly underpriced.

It’s incredible to have reached a threshold where it’s practical that AI will be better at training models than humans are so quickly. One day recursive self improvement feels far off, and the next we already have signs of what it will look like. Model capability finally exceeding most human engineers, advancements in the harness and infra for long running agents, a growing array of data, and the ability to parallelize the agents to work on vastly more tasks and tests and approaches than any person could have before, creates an incredible flywheel. This flywheel will almost certainly accelerate model progress far faster than we’ve seen before - or at a minimum enable pushing up the ceiling of the frontier at an insane rate. If you aren’t pricing in what RSI will lead to, that’s probably a mistake.

From Math to Biology / 从数学突破到科学发现

当 AI 能解决过去难以验证的问题，下一关不是炫技，而是让人类理解、检查并把结果带进真实世界。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast
中文

核心结论是：AI 的科学价值，不该只看它能不能吐出一个人类看不懂的答案，而要看它能把人的能力放大到什么程度。OpenAI President Greg Brockman 认为，最近的数学进展已经说明模型确实能产生新的知识；更难的产品和安全问题，是如何让人理解、检查这些结果。 他把 drug discovery 视为最明确的应用方向之一，但也提醒，生物学受数据瓶颈影响，实验、监管和临床流程同样不能跳过。最值得记住的原则是，人类仍要坐在驾驶位上：「你不只是想要软件，而是想要可靠、安全、值得信任的软件。」能力越强，监督和解释就越是科学流程的一部分。

你不只是想要软件，对吧？你想要的是可靠、安全、值得信任的软件。 我认为，我们现在已经有能力大幅加速科学发现。一直以来，人们都在问 AI 到底能不能产生新的知识，而现在已经无法否认了：答案确实是能。

English

The takeaway: AI's scientific value will be measured by how much it expands human leverage, not by whether it can produce an opaque answer. OpenAI President Greg Brockman argues that recent mathematical progress shows models can generate genuinely new knowledge, while the harder product and safety problem is helping people understand and check it. He sees drug discovery as a particularly clear target, but warns that biology is data-bottlenecked and the pipeline includes experiments, regulation, and clinical work. The memorable principle is that people should stay in the driver's seat: "You don't just want software. You want software that is reliable, that is secure, that you can believe in." As capability rises, oversight and explanation become part of the science itself.

You don't just want software. Right? You want software that is reliable, that is secure, that you can believe in. I think that we now have the ability to massively accelerate scientific discovery and there has always been this question of is AI actually able to come up with new knowledge and at this point, it's just impossible to deny, right? It is definitively, yes, possible.
