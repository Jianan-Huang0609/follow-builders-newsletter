编者导语 / Editor's Note

今天的信号很一致：AI 正从“帮我做一点事”走向“我来管理一支由 agent 组成的团队”。与此同时，真正拉开差距的仍然是底层基础设施、个人对工具链的掌控，以及把一个想法快速做成可用产品的能力。

Theme 01

From Coding to Orchestrating / 从写代码到调度工作

今天最值得注意的变化不是模型又多了一个功能，而是工作本身开始按 agent 的并行能力重新组织。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，知识工作者正在从“让 agent 帮自己做得更快一点”，转向“管理一支 agent 团队”。在 coding、网络安全、法律、生物科技、工程等信息密集型领域，并行执行正在打破工作时长与产出之间的旧关系；越早按 agent 重构流程的组织，越可能获得复利式优势。

越来越多岗位的新职责会是这样，而且不只是 coding。我们显然正在从 agent 帮我们把工作做得更好、更快一点，走向我们帮助 agent 完成它们的工作。 这会把这些岗位上的每个人都变成一支 agent 团队的管理者，而不再只是一个获得 AI 杠杆的个人贡献者。影响最大的会是信息密集型工作，比如 coding、网络安全、法律、生物科技、工程等。 因此，在这些工作中，你的杠杆会逐渐与“投入多少小时、产出多少结果”的通常比例脱钩。知识工作的历史上，假设人才、协调和策略水平固定，唯一能调整的主要变量就是工作时长。 现在变量被拆开了，因为 agent 可以为我们并行执行工作，理论上可以按目标需要执行尽可能多的任务。更早发现这一点、并围绕它重构运营方式的人和组织，会在工作流中获得复利式增长。

English

Aaron Levie, Box CEO, argues that knowledge workers are moving from using agents as incremental productivity tools to managing teams of agents. In information-heavy fields, parallel execution breaks the old link between hours worked and output, so the organizations that redesign their operations around agents early may compound their advantage.

Increasingly this will be the new job description for a lot of roles, not just coding. We’re clearly moving from a world where agents help us do our work incrementally better or faster, to us helping agents do their work. This turns everyone in one of these positions to that of a manager of a team of agents, instead of an individual contributor with just some added leverage from AI. The biggest areas of impact will be information-heavy jobs, like coding, cybersecurity, legal work, biotech, engineering, and many other fields. And as a result, in these jobs your leverage will begin to be decoupled from the normal ratio of hours spent working to output generated. For essentially the history of knowledge work the main variable you had to play with was hours of work done (assuming fixed level talent/coordination/strategy). Now we’ve separated these variables because agents can parallelize work for us, to in theory execute as much as we need to pull off whatever goal we’re trying to accomplish. The people and organizations that discover this sooner, and orient their operations around it, will see compounding gains in their workflows.

Theme 02

Own the Stack / 把工具链握在自己手里

当模型能力越来越接近时，选择什么模型、如何组合工具、谁掌握运行环境，会变成更实际的竞争力。

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 主张把自己的 intelligence layer 握在手里：掌控 harness、数据、连接、runtime 和 agent，而不是把一切押在某家供应商的巨型模型上。他并不是在否定大模型，而是在提醒大家用本地模型和开源保留选择权。

掌握自己的 intelligence。 任何反对这一点的人，都有东西想卖给你（也许是把你自己的 intelligence 再卖回给你）。 本地模型需要继续发展。那些“绝对庞大”的模型目前可能还无法在本地运行，所以最好的选择确实是保留选项。掌控你的 harness、数据、连接、runtime，也就是你的 agent。拥抱开源。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch makes the case for owning your intelligence layer: keep control of the harness, data, connections, runtime, and agents instead of depending on one provider's giant model. His point is less about rejecting large models than preserving optionality through local models and open source.

Own your intelligence. Anyone that opposes this has something to sell you (perhaps your own intelligence back to you.) Local models need to continue proliferating. The “absolute unit” massive models may be out of reach for now locally, so your best option is to, indeed, keep your options open. Own your harness, data, connections, runtime: your agents. Embrace open source.
