Theme 01

Long-Horizon Agents: The Memento Problem / 长时程 Agent：记忆碎片难题

Basis 用一整集播客讲透了长时程 agent 的系统难题，并开源了自己的解法——behavior specs。

MT
Mitch Troyanovsky (Basis) & Matt Turck
The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
中文

Basis 联合创始人 Mitch Troyanovsky 给出了迄今为止对「长时程 agent」最成体系的一整套论述：LLM 工作记忆很大，但既没有短期记忆也没有长期记忆，所以一个要连续工作几小时的 agent，必须像《记忆碎片》里那个失忆主角一样，学会给自己留笔记。

他对 eval 文化的反驳很直接：「你有 100 个 eval，全过了，看着很好——但你就敢说它能泛化到真实生产环境吗？我们的答案是：不敢。」解法是他和 Braintrust 一起开源的 behavior specs——用 markdown 写清楚 agent 应该怎么表现，再由 judge 对着执行轨迹打分。

还有两句值得裱起来的话：「英文更珍贵，因为英文影响性能；代码不影响性能。」以及——真实世界的工作不需要 Move 37 式的神来之笔，客户买的是稳定、可靠和信任，就像你雇一个人时要求的一样。

公司和管理流程本质上就是：如何设计一个系统，让非确定性的实体（也就是人）协作解决一个问题。想通这一点，agent 设计其实是同一回事。

假设你有 100 个 eval，全过了，看起来很好。但你能确信这能泛化到真实世界、到生产环境吗？我们的答案是：不能。

英文更珍贵，因为英文影响性能；代码不影响性能。

客户从我们这里买的，不是史上最好的税表，而是稳定、可靠、可以信任——就像你雇一个真人时会要求的那样。

English

Basis co-founder Mitch Troyanovsky makes the strongest case yet for why long-horizon agents are a systems problem, not a prompting problem: LLMs have a huge working memory but no short- or long-term memory, so an agent working for hours has to write its own notes the way the amnesiac protagonist of Memento does.

His counter to eval culture is refreshingly blunt: 'you have 100 evals, they all pass... are you confident that that now generalizes to production? Our answer has been no.' The fix is behavior specs—markdown files stating how an agent should behave, graded by judges against trajectories—which he just open-sourced with Braintrust.

Two lines worth framing: 'The English is more precious because the English affects the performance. The code does not affect the performance.' And the reminder that for real-world work you don't need Move 37s—you need consistency and trust, the same things you'd demand from a human you hire.

And in many ways, like companies and processes is all about how do you design a system for non deterministic entities to coordinate together to solve a problem? And once you realize that, it's like, well, now it's like kind of agent design.

Let's say you have a 100 evals. Great. They all pass. That looks good. Are you confident that that now generalizes to the real world to production? And our answer has been no.

The English is more precious because the English affects the performance. The code does not affect the performance.

The thing that someone is buying from us is not this will be the best ever tax return. They're buying that it's going to be consistent and reliable and something that they can trust.
