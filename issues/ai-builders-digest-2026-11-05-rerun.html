中文

采访过大约 150 位与现代 AI 实验室有关的人之后，Kevin Roose 认为，未来既不是命中注定，也不会自动安全。他主张在模型公开部署前建立类似药品的行业安全测试，并通过协调机制给安全团队留出开发控制手段的时间。他还提醒：塑造 AI 的很多决定发生在私下，所以记录我们是怎么走到这里的，本身就很重要。

English

Kevin Roose, after interviewing roughly 150 people connected to the labs building modern AI, says the future is neither predetermined nor safe by default. He argues for basic industry-wide testing before public deployment, comparing it to pharmaceutical safety testing, and for coordination that gives safety teams time to develop better controls. His historical point is equally important: many of the decisions that shaped AI happened in private, so recording how we got here matters.

I came away thinking both that there are some real risks ahead and some real needles that we'll need to thread as a society, but that we can do it. The choices that we make right now matter a lot.
