Enterprise Agents Need New Operating Rules / 企业 Agent 需要新规则

企业已经开始部署 agent，但安全、身份、模型选择和旧系统仍然决定了落地速度。

中文

Aaron Levie 从银行、媒体、保险、咨询和信息服务等行业的交流中，带回了一张很务实的企业 AI 快照：大家正在同时部署多个 frontier model，因为不同团队和场景偏好的模型并不一样；与此同时，网络安全、agent 身份和碎片化的 legacy data 仍是主要障碍。

真正的大收益不是给旧流程贴一个 agent，而是围绕 agent 能做什么重新设计工作流。但谁来负责这种 process reengineering，很多公司还没有答案。Evals 也处于早期：大多数组织还不能很好地衡量工作究竟是怎么发生的，以及 AI 在其中表现如何。

大多数公司在企业内部部署多个 frontier model，很难标准化，因为不同团队和场景有不同偏好。

agent 的最大价值在于改变实际工作流，而不是只加一层 agent。

大多数企业还很早期，还没有很好地掌握如何评估自己的工作流。

English

Aaron Levie brings back a pragmatic snapshot from conversations with technology leaders across banking, media, insurance, consulting, and information services. Enterprises are deploying multiple frontier models because different teams and use cases prefer different systems, while cyber risk, agent identity, and fragmented legacy data remain major obstacles.

The biggest upside is not adding an agent to an unchanged process. Companies are finding more ROI when they redesign the workflow around what agents can do, though they still lack clear owners for that kind of process reengineering. Evals are also early: most organizations do not yet have a good way to measure how their work actually happens or how well AI performs against it.

Most companies are deploying multiple frontier models within their enterprise. Too hard to standardize on anything and seeing different preferences across their teams and use cases.

Most companies realizing that the big upside of agents is when they can change the actual workflow itself to get the full gains from AI.

Still very early for most companies to have a good grasp of evals of their workflows.

Theme 02

Measure the Path, Not Just the Answer / 评估过程，不只评估答案

Agent 的效率和可靠性，藏在它如何搜索、调用工具、处理错误和抵达结果的过程中。

中文

Madhu Guru 认为，好的 agent eval 不该只看最终答案，还要测量每一步。两条 trajectory 都可能得到 42，但一条找对了来源、取对了文档，只做了 4 次干净的 tool call；另一条重复搜索、遇到错误，最后用了 17 次调用才到达结果。

建议很具体：先定义完整工作流，再拆分任务，决定每一步怎么测，同时纳入中位难度和 hard case。先研究路径，再看答案。

评估步骤，不只是评估结果。

一个 agent 找对来源、取对文档，干净地调用 4 次工具；另一个调用 17 次，重复搜索 3 次，处理 2 个错误。

清晰定义完整工作流，并想清楚如何评估每一步。

English

Madhu Guru argues that good agent evals should measure the steps, not only the final result. Two trajectories can both return 42, while one finds the right sources, retrieves the right document, makes four clean tool calls, and the other repeats searches, hits errors, and uses 17 calls.

The prescription is concrete: define the whole workflow, break it into tasks, decide how each step should be measured, and include both median and hard cases. Study the path first, then the answer.

Measure the steps, not just the result.

One of them searches the right sources, retrieves the right document, makes 4 clean tool calls. The other makes 17 calls, searches the same thing 3 times, recovers from 2 errors and eventually gets there.

Clearly define your whole workflow and think through how you measure each step.

当模型开始适应训练环境，训练基础设施本身就成了模型能力的一部分。

RS
Richard Socher
The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
中文

Richard Socher 的核心判断是：科学已经从一整块知识，变成了无数高度专业化领域组成的迷宫。AI 可能扮演当年 calculus 对物理学的角色，把复杂的局部重新拼回可以推理的系统，尤其是在 biology、protein、energy 和 materials 这些领域。

更长期的图景是 simulations、verifiers、virtual cells、robotic laboratories 和 agent swarms 组成的循环。瓶颈不只是 compute 或 model，也包括科学数据，以及能否教会 AI 足够准确的现实规则，让它真正提出并验证新想法。

科学很擅长理解越来越小的局部，但要把它们重新组合起来，我们需要 AI。

AI 之于 biology，可能就像 calculus 之于 physics。

凡是能模拟的东西，AI 都会解决，然后很快进入 recursive self-improvement loop。

English

Richard Socher's thesis is that science has turned from a body of knowledge into a labyrinth of specialized fields. AI could play the role calculus once played for physics: reconnecting complex pieces into systems we can reason about, especially in biology, proteins, energy, and materials.

The longer-term picture is a loop of simulations, verifiers, virtual cells, robotic laboratories, and agent swarms. The bottleneck is not only compute or models, but also scientific data and teaching AI the rules of reality well enough to generate and test genuinely new ideas.

Science has gotten really good at understanding smaller and smaller pieces, but to bring them back up, and bring them together, we actually have to use AI.

AI is kind of what calculus did for physics, AI will do for biology.

Anything you can simulate, AI will solve. And before you know it, you're in this recursive self improvement loop.

模型越智能，没有正确的 safeguards 和 monitoring，就越危险。

His point is less about a single forecast than about making these tradeoffs legible enough for everyone to understand and respond to as capabilities accelerate.

As models become more intelligent, without the right safeguards and monitoring they also become more dangerous.

Many capabilities are dual use: a model that codes well can be used to hack critical infrastructure; a model that assists with biology research can also be used to engineer the next pandemic.
