Builder Realities / Builder 的现实

当模型越来越强，builder 的挑战反而更具体了——不是能力不够，是速度、变现和工作流。

中文

Madhu Guru 指出了当下 AI 领域可能是最高杠杆的能力：把混乱的真实工作流适配到基础模型上。「这需要理解工作是怎么完成的、设计评测、通过后训练改进模型、建立持续优化的反馈闭环。这才是通用模型变成特定领域顶尖工具的方式。」

他的观点微妙但重要：问题不在模型的智能程度，而在于「人实际怎么干活」和「模型期望什么输入」之间的桥。今天这项能力仍集中在少数几个实验室里。谁能搭好这座桥，谁就能捕获巨大价值。

未来几年真正的机会在于：把混乱的真实工作流适配到基础模型上。需要理解工作方式、设计评测、通过后训练改进、建立反馈闭环。今天这项能力仍集中在少数实验室。

English

Madhu Guru identifies what might be the highest-leverage skillset in AI right now: taking messy real-world workflows and adapting foundation models to them. 'Doing that requires understanding how work gets done, designing evals, improving models through post-training, and building the feedback loops that continuously improve models.'

His point is subtle but important: the gap isn't the model's intelligence. It's the bridge between how people actually work and what the model expects as input. 'Today, that skillset is still concentrated in a handful of labs.' The people who can build that bridge will capture enormous value.

There is a massive opportunity over the next few years for people who know how to take messy real-world workflows and adapt foundation models to them. Doing that requires understanding how work gets done, designing evals, improving models through post-training, and building the feedback loops that continuously improve models. That's how a general-purpose model becomes exceptional for a specific domain. Today, that skillset is still concentrated in a handful of labs.
