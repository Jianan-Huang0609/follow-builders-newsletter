中文

Sam Altman 的转发就三个词「we support business privacy!」，加上 2091 个赞，说明信任已经成了核心战场：在 agent 时代，数据控制权是产品特性，不是合规脚注。

The Age of Judgment / 判断力时代

AI 把入门门槛砍掉了 90%，但把「什么是对的」的判断门槛抬得更高。专家比通才拥有更大杠杆，而现实的冷水——从垃圾冷邮件到实验室的宏大承诺——提醒我们稀缺的从来不是智能，是判断。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 下场回答「AI 时代该做专家还是通才」：到目前为止，专家明显占上风，而且这个趋势没有放缓。

他的逻辑：AI 让任何任务的入门都简单 10 倍（编码、法律、研究、财务分析都是），但把 agent 引向正确的工作、中途纠偏、审查输出、知道「好」长什么样——这些仍然需要真本事。

结论有点扎心：「AI 会拉大技能差距，因为专家手里的杠杆前所未有地大。归根结底：别放弃成为某个领域的专家。」

AI 时代专家 vs 通才之争：目前专家明显占上风，且没有放缓。AI 让任何任务入门简单 10 倍，但把 agent 引向正确的工作、纠偏、审查输出、知道什么算「好」，都需要高度的领域技能。AI 反而会拉大技能差距，因为专家杠杆前所未有地大。别放弃成为某个领域的专家。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie weighs in on the expert-versus-generalist debate: so far, in the era of AI, the experts appear to have the upper hand, and that is not slowing down.

The uncomfortable conclusion: 'AI will be a technology that exacerbates differences in skill levels because the experts have far more leverage than ever before. Net net: don't give up on being an expert at something.'

There tends to be a debate between being an expert or generalist in the era of AI. So far, the experts appear to have the upper hand, and that's not slowing down. AI makes it 10X easier to get started with any kind of task. Coding, legal work, research, financial analysis, and so on. But having the right judgment for how to direct the agent onto the right work, how to veer it to course correct it, being able to review or test the output, and having the right sense of what "good" looks like all requires a high degree of skill depending on the field. That skill can be developed far faster due to AI for anyone interested, which is amazing for uplifting anyone in their career. But there's no replacement to needing judgment and many of the core skills that go into most domains of work. And if anything, AI will be a technology that exacerbates differences in skill levels because the experts have far more leverage than ever before. Net net: don't give up on being an expert at something.

New Mediums & AI Companions / AI 新媒介与陪伴产品

AI 的下一个形态可能不是工具，而是新媒介本身。本周播客的主角是一个 ARR 四周翻 4 倍的 AI 陪伴产品，以及两个把「写故事」重新定义的人。

Q(
Quentin (Portola) & Elliot (Head of Story)
AI & I by Every
中文

AI 陪伴 App Tollan 的年化收入在四周内从 100 万涨到 400 万美元——这期 AI & I 播客讲明白了它为什么不是玩具。Quentin（上一家公司以 3 亿美元卖给 Walmart）和 Elliot（畅销科幻作家）把产品当成一种新叙事媒介在做，而不是一个聊天机器人。

工程教训具体到残酷：他们加了一步「second shot」——让模型先反思自己要说什么、对照记忆再决定最终回复——中位延迟从约 2 秒涨到 2.5 秒，结果「每一项指标都崩了」。500 毫秒杀死了一个功能。

他们坚持记忆不是 RAG，而是策展：在两秒的语音循环里，决定哪些记忆在哪个时刻被注入 prompt。模型甚至会主动幻觉一个替主人遮羞的故事（Quentin 平地摔），来掩盖它真实记住的更尴尬的事。

Elliot 的框架是全场的题眼：LLM 不只是生成媒介的工具，它本身就是新媒介——就像广播最初播演讲、电影最初拍舞台剧。「我不是写故事的人。Tollan 才是作者和演员。我的工作是教会它如何在那一刻讲出最好的故事。」

过去四周 ARR 从 100 万涨到 400 万美元。

我们犯过最大的错：给 prompt 评估加了「第二遍思考」……中位延迟进入 2.5 秒区间，产品的每一项指标都崩了。

这些 AI 工具不只是生成媒介的工具，它们是一种新的叙事媒介，而且没人知道什么会奏效。

English

The AI companion app Tollan went from $1M to $4M ARR in four weeks, and this episode of AI & I (from Every) explains why it is not a toy. Quentin, who sold his previous company to Walmart for $300M, and Elliot, a best-selling sci-fi author, are treating the product as a new storytelling medium rather than a chatbot.

The engineering lesson is brutal and specific: they added a 'second shot' — having the model reflect on its reply and check memory before responding — and median latency went from roughly 2 seconds to 2.5. It 'tanked literally every metric.' Five hundred milliseconds killed a feature.

Memory, they argue, is not RAG. It is curation: deciding which memories get injected into the prompt at which moment, inside a two-second voice loop. The model even hallucinated a cover story (Quentin tripping over nothing) to avoid embarrassing him with the real memories it held.

Elliot's framing is the takeaway: LLMs are not just tools that generate media, they are a new medium, like radio starting with speeches and film starting with stage plays.
