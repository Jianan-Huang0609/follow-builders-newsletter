The Human Edge / 人的优势还在判断

中文

Every CEO Dan Shipper 认为，AI 会把所有人的能力底线一起抬高，于是模型的默认答案反而没那么值钱了。真正稀缺的是一个人的视角和责任感，它们决定了哪些可能性值得被挑出来、继续做下去。

他的客户调研例子很具体：同一份数据、同一组模型，不同的人看出了销售机会、流失原因、社区洞察和 Claymation 游戏。生成很便宜，排序、判断并承担行动后果，仍然是团队的工作。

让 Claude 生成每个人发现的东西？也许可以。但生成可能性很便宜，知道哪些重要却很难。

价值在于用你的视角和责任感，越过模型给出的基线。

English

Every CEO Dan Shipper argues that AI raises the floor for everyone, so the default answer becomes less valuable. The scarce part is the perspective and responsibility that let someone decide which possibility matters enough to act on.

His customer-survey example is unusually concrete: the same data and models produced a sales opportunity, a churn diagnosis, a community insight, and a Claymation game, depending on who looked at it. Generation is cheap; ranking and taking responsibility are still the team’s job.

Could you ask Claude to produce what each of them found? Maybe. But generating possibilities is cheap. Knowing which ones matter is hard.

The value is in using your perspective and responsibility to push past the baseline.

Theme 03

Where the New Companies Are / 新公司往哪里长

中文

SPC General Partner Aditya Agarwal 认为，现在大体有两类公司机会。第一类是扩散：把 AI 送到工作真正发生的地方，可以理解为 AI 时代的 vertical SaaS。第二类是前沿：用 AI 加速科学进展，再把这些进展变成产品。

他举的例子包括药物发现、基因组学、毒液研究和半导体多物理建模。这一类机会不那么容易被外部看见，但他的判断是，未来大部分由模型驱动的回报，可能都来自这层更深的前沿。

现在基本有两类公司可以做：AI 的扩散，以及 AI 的前沿。

第二类还没有被外界充分理解或讨论，但我认为绝大多数模型驱动的回报都会来自这里。

English

SPC General Partner Aditya Agarwal sees two broad company-building opportunities. The first is diffusion: bringing AI close to where work happens, the AI-era version of vertical SaaS. The second is frontier: using AI to accelerate science and then turning those advances into products.

He points to drug discovery, genomics, venom research, and semiconductor multiphysics as examples of the less visible category. His thesis is that most model-driven returns may ultimately come from this harder-to-see frontier layer.

There are basically two kinds of companies to build right now: Diffusion of AI and Frontier of AI.

Category 2 is not well understood or much talked about externally. It should be because I think that is where the vast majority of model driven returns will come from.

One Long Listen / 一期值得听完的播客

A&
AI & I by Every
Every
中文

在《A New Vision for Human Work After Automation》中，Every 联合创始人兼 CEO Dan Shipper 提出了两种观察 AI 的方式：看整体能力曲线，和贴近一个真实团队的具体工作。前者容易让人觉得人类工作正在消失，后者则能看见责任与视角为什么仍然重要。

他最锋利的观点是：团队之所以有价值，是因为每个人都会用不同的责任去过滤同一个模型。当模型能力广泛可得，默认输出就只是底线。真正稀缺的，是那个能判断什么重要、然后采取行动的人。

自动化之后人类工作的新愿景

English

In A New Vision for Human Work After Automation, Every co-founder and CEO Dan Shipper makes a case for looking at AI through two lenses: the aggregate capability curve and the intimate details of one real team. The first can make human work look doomed; the second shows why responsibility and perspective still matter.

His sharpest idea is that teams are valuable because each person filters the same model through a different responsibility. As models become broadly available, the default output becomes the floor. What remains rare is the human who can decide what matters, then act on it.
