编者导语 / Editor's Note

今天最值得留意的，不是又多了几个 agent，而是 agent 正在从个人玩具变成组织基础设施：它们开始替你过滤信息、协作、审查代码，也开始暴露权限、可靠性和成本的真实问题。与此同时，AI 让“我能不能把它做出来”变得不再稀缺，真正的优势正在转向领域判断、分发和对更大问题的承担。

Theme 01

From Personal Agents to Company Agents / 从个人 Agent 到公司 Agent

agent 的下一步不只是更像一个人，而是成为团队共享、有人维护、能真正完成工作的组织能力。

中文

Every CEO Dan Shipper 使用 OpenAI Dots 后，看到的是一个很实用的区分：个人 agent 的价值在于保护注意力、捞出那些容易漏掉但重要的信息，还能让你离开电脑时用语音继续工作。但人格和角色未必能长久，真正持久的是能力。他判断，公司最终会更需要一个大家共享、有人维护的 company agent，而不是一群脆弱的个人 bot。

问题在权限。Dots 已经能帮忙过滤 Slack 回复、学校邮件和账号申请，但反复审批、批准后仍然失败，会把信任边界直接暴露出来。未来的 agent 不只要主动，还必须知道自己被可靠地授权了什么。

OpenAI 的 Dots 上周发布后，已经占领了 Every。它们能保护注意力，把模型测试结果发到 Slack，再把回复带回来，让我不用打开 Slack 就能继续工作。

我不太相信我们会永远忠于这些角色。我的叫 Boo，我很喜欢它，但我以前也喜欢自己的 OpenClaw agent。更好的东西一出现，我就会切换。一年后我应该还会用 persistent agent，但 Boo 大概已经死了。RIP Boo。

English

Every CEO Dan Shipper's experience with OpenAI's Dots points to a useful distinction: a personal agent is valuable when it protects attention, catches small but important signals, and lets you work by voice away from your laptop. But the character and personality are less durable than the capability. His bet is that companies will increasingly want one well-maintained company agent that everyone can benefit from, rather than a zoo of fragile personal bots.

The catch is permissions. Dots can already filter Slack replies, school emails, and access requests, but repeated approvals and failed actions make the trust boundary visible. The future agent is not just proactive; it has to be reliably authorized.

OpenAI’s Dots launched last week and they've taken over @every. A few things that stand out: They protect your attention. Over the weekend, my Dot posted my model-testing results to Slack and brought back replies. I could keep working without opening Slack and disappearing down a rabbit hole.

I’m less convinced we’ll stay loyal to the characters. Mine is named Boo, and I’m attached to him. But I felt that way about my OpenClaw agent, too. When something better comes along, I switch. In a year, I expect to be using a persistent agent. Boo will probably be dead. RIP, Boo.

中文

一句话总结：Every 从“每个人一个个人 agent”走向“一个公司 agent”，不是因为个人 agent 没用，而是维护、安全和共享经验最终比个性化更重要。个人 agent 很适合喜欢折腾的人，但很多 operator 真正想要的是一个能直接工作的系统。

这段经历也暴露了 AI 产品的一个事实：它的使用面比传统 SaaS 大得多。builder 喜欢旋钮和复杂度，企业需要的却是稳定、共享技能和判断的系统。重点不是消灭个人 agent，而是给公司一个可靠的重心。

我们从 OpenClaw 的每人一个个人 agent，走到了一个公司 agent，而且我们太喜欢公司 agent 了，最后还把它做成了给别人用的产品。

与其让每个人都维护自己的 agent，不如把这些精力放到一个全公司都能受益的 agent 上。

builder 喜欢暴露在复杂度里，喜欢调旋钮；但如果你在经营一家公司，你通常不想处理这些，你只想要一个能工作的东西。

English

The takeaway: Every moved from giving everyone a personal agent to building one company agent because maintenance, security, and shared expertise mattered more than individual customization. Personal agents were great for tinkerers, but many operators wanted something that simply worked.

The experience also exposed a product truth about AI: the surface area is much larger than a traditional SaaS app. Builders enjoy knobs and complexity; a business needs a stable system whose skills and decisions are shared. The result is less about killing personal agents than giving the company a dependable center of gravity.

We for us went from OpenClaw, everyone in the company's got a personal agent to we have a company agent and we liked the company agent so much that we actually just made a company agent for other people to use.

Instead of everyone investing in their own agents and making them better day to day over time, it's like, why don't we take all that energy and put it into one agent that the whole company benefits from?

Builders wanna be exposed to the complexity because everything you wanna do is going in there and tinkering and turning knobs and whatnot. But if you're trying to run a business, you're not really, you don't wanna do that.

Theme 02

The New Software Advantage / 软件优势正在迁移

当写出一个可用产品变得更容易，稀缺性就会从实现能力转移到领域知识、分发和真正值得解决的问题。

中文

Aditya Agarwal 认为，“我能把它做出来”正在变成一个越来越弱的创始人优势。AI 让更多人都能做出可用软件，所以有抱负的工程师应该把技术能力放在仍然需要突破的前沿，或者放在自己真正理解的昂贵问题旁边。

他认为最没意思的路径，是只靠监督 agent 把同一家公司 backlog 做得更快。真正的机会，是利用更强的构建能力，去承担更大的问题。

AI 正让生产可用软件的能力变得更普遍。几年前还做不出产品的人，现在也能走得很远；有经验的工程师可以尝试过去需要一个团队才能完成的项目。

优秀工程依然稀缺，但“我能把它做出来”越来越难解释你具体有什么优势。

利用你能做更多事情这一事实，去承担更大的责任。

English

Aditya Agarwal argues that 'I can build it' is becoming a weaker answer to why a founder has an advantage. AI is making working software accessible to more people, so ambitious engineers should place their technical ability next to a frontier that still needs advancing or deep domain knowledge about an expensive problem.

His least compelling path is simply doing the same company backlog faster by supervising agents. The opportunity is to use increased building capacity to take responsibility for something bigger.

AI is making the ability to produce working software much more widely available. People who couldn’t have built a product a few years ago can now get surprisingly far. Experienced engineers can attempt projects that previously required a team.

Great engineering is still rare. But “I can build it” is becoming a weaker answer to why you, specifically, have an advantage.

Use the fact that you can build more to take responsibility for something bigger.

Theme 03

Agents Need a Real Operating Environment / Agent 需要真实的运行环境

agent 规模化之后，计算、网络、文件系统、安全和停止边界都会成为产品本身的一部分。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 看到的下一波 AI 基础设施，远不只是 token 用量增加。个人 agent、企业防御 swarm、代码安全审查、企业数据工作流和 24/7 后台 agent，都需要模型之外的 computer、networking 和 file system。换句话说，agent 的基础设施建设才刚刚开始，哪怕它的交互界面已经让人觉得很熟悉。

我们即将进入的 AI 阶段，所需的计算量会非常惊人。

个人 agent、保护企业的 agent swarm、审查全部代码安全问题的 agent、处理几乎所有企业数据工作流的 agent、全天候运行的后台 agent，等等。

它们需要计算机、网络、文件系统等。我们还处在这场基础设施建设的早期。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie sees the next AI infrastructure wave as much larger than token consumption. Personal agents, enterprise defense swarms, code-security reviewers, data-processing workflows, and 24/7 background agents will all need computers, networking, and file systems around the model. The implication is that the agent buildout is still at an early stage, even if the interface already feels familiar.

The compute needed for the stage of AI we’re about to enter is going to be insane.

Personal agents, agent swarms defending enterprises, agents that review all of our code for security issues, agents that process nearly all enterprise data in workflows, background agents working on tasks 24/7, and on and on.

They need computers, networking, file systems, and so on. We’re only in the early stages of what this buildout is going to look like.

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 提醒了一个不那么光鲜、但很现实的问题：agent 会很乐意无限加固和优化程序，包括那些用户永远不会走到的路径。token 无限，并不意味着注意力和机会成本消失了。所以好的工程系统需要明确的停止规则和可接受的 trade-off，而不只是更多 autonomy。

每个程序都可以几乎无限地被加固（消灭边界 case、收紧输入、处理错误）和优化（分析性能、做 benchmark、重写）。

即使 token 无限，你也需要知道什么时候停止，以及接受哪些 trade-off。agent 不管怎样都会一直钻下去。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch points out a less glamorous agent problem: agents will happily harden and optimize a program forever, including paths no user will ever touch. Infinite tokens do not remove the cost of attention or opportunity. A good engineering system therefore needs an explicit stopping rule and accepted tradeoffs, not just more autonomy.

Every program can be ① hardened (edge cases squashed, inputs tightened, errors handled) and ② optimized (profiled, benchmarked, rewritten) basically ad infinitum.

Even in an infinite token world, you need to know when to stop and what tradeoffs to accept. Agents will happily drill no matter what.
