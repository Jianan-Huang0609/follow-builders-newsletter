Theme 04

From Prediction to Steering / 从预测走向操控实验

AI for Science 的下一步，不只是建立 world model，而是用它决定下一次实验做什么。

LS
Latent Space
Latent Space · Helmholtz Munich
中文

Fabian Theis 把 computational biology 的路线概括成三步：先 mapping 细胞，再预测 perturbation 后的反应，现在要进一步 steering 下一次实验。他所在团队的 Lab Compass 是一个 inverse generative model，可以从目标生物状态反推下一步该做什么。

难点不只是模型，而是要让研究者愿意花真实的实验时间去验证预测。因此正在形成的壁垒，是模型和通过反复 lab-in-the-loop 产生的独特数据的结合。

我们从 mapping 走向 prediction，但现在可以真正 steering 下一次实验。

Lab Compass 能在有了 forward world model 后，告诉你为了达到某个状态需要做什么。

最终愿景是把一切都 RL 化，也把 RL 放进这个 loop。

English

Fabian Theis describes a three-step arc for computational biology: mapping cells, predicting how they respond to perturbations, and now steering the next experiment. His group's Lab Compass is an inverse generative model that works backward from a desired biological state to suggest what should be done next.

The hard part is not only the model. Researchers must trust a prediction enough to spend real experimental time testing it. That is why the emerging moat is the combination of models and unique data generated through repeated lab-in-the-loop cycles.

From mapping, we went to prediction. But now we can go to actually steering the next experiment.

Lab Compass ... allows you, once you have a forward world model, to tell you what you need to do in order to reach a certain state.

The ultimate vision being that we want to RL everything. We want to do RL also in this loop.
