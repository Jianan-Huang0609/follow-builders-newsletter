The Cursor-OpenAI Split / Cursor 与 OpenAI 分手

OpenAI 以「信任」为由终止与 Cursor 的合作，coding agent 市场在一夜之间重新洗牌。

中文

OpenAI 的 Thibault Sottiaux（Codex 和 ChatGPT 团队）抛出了今天最大的新闻：OpenAI 决定终止与 Cursor 的合作，不再通过 Cursor 提供模型访问，将于 11 月 12 日生效，理由是「信任」。

声明给开发者留了缓冲和备选：你可以继续用自己的 OpenAI API key，或者用 OpenAI 官方的 Cursor IDE 扩展。OpenAI 把这次切割包装成对「广泛可选择性」和自身开源计划的加码。

真正的信号在潜台词里：当 coding agent 成为前沿模型的主要分发渠道，对这条渠道的控制权正在变成战略争夺的焦点。

我们不得不做出决定：无法继续通过 Cursor 提供模型访问，终止合作。核心是信任问题，11 月 12 日生效，给你们留时间规划。

很多人在 Cursor 里用过 GPT 模型，以下是可行的替代方案：继续使用你自己的 OpenAI API key……

English

OpenAI's Thibault Sottiaux (Codex & ChatGPT) drops the day's biggest news: OpenAI is ending its partnership with Cursor and will stop providing model access through it, effective November 12, citing 'trust' as the deciding factor.

The announcement is careful to give developers a runway and alternatives: you can keep using your own OpenAI API key, or OpenAI's own IDE extensions for Cursor. OpenAI frames it as doubling down on 'broad optionality' and its own open-source work.

The subtext is the real story: as coding agents become the primary distribution channel for frontier models, control over that channel is becoming a strategic battleground.

We unfortunately have decided that we cannot continue providing access to our models through Cursor and are ending our partnership. It boils down to trust and we've asked that this takes effect on November 12 to give you some time to plan.

Many have used the GPT models through Cursor and here are options we know should work in the future: We will continue to allow using your own OpenAI API key...

Data Is the New Moat / 数据成为新护城河

Google 花 1000 万美元买下破产航司的数据，Eon 联合创始人解释为什么在 AI 时代数据才是真正的护城河。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast
中文

Eon 的联合创始人 Ofir Ehrlich 和 Gonen Stein（前 AWS，创立了 CloudEndure 并卖给了亚马逊）给出了一个尖锐判断：在 AI 时代，真正的护城河是数据，而不是模型或算力——因为模型和算力「几乎没有切换成本」。

最有冲击力的例子：Google 花了 1000 万美元从破产的 Spirit Airlines 手里买下了数据——不是飞机，是数据——用来训练模型，据说另一个竞标方是 Mercor。他们预测这类「破产数据收购」会越来越多。

安全方面，他们描述了一种「加倍的」新威胁：拥有合法权限的 AI agent 在你的环境里几秒钟就能删掉一张表。方法上和防御勒索软件类似，但「速度是极端的」。

他们的核心论点：现在人人都能当 builder，这既是礼物也是治理噩梦——大量不受组织规则约束的非人类行动者在处理敏感数据。

如果你是一家公司……你拥有的最有价值的东西其实是你的数据。

两天前 Google 从破产的 Spirit Airlines 买了点东西。他们没买飞机，买的是数据。花 1000 万美元是因为他们觉得数据很重要……用来训练模型。

我们现在看到的是加倍的同类威胁，来自非人类行动者、来自拥有合法权限的 AI agent……一张表突然就被删了。

English

Eon co-founders Ofir Ehrlich and Gonen Stein (ex-AWS, who built CloudEndure and sold it to Amazon) make a sharp case: in the AI era, data — not models or compute — is the only real moat, because models and compute have 'almost zero switching costs.'

The headline example: Google bought Spirit Airlines' data out of bankruptcy for $10 million — not the planes, the data — to train models, with Mercor reportedly the other bidder. They predict we'll see far more of these 'out of bankruptcy data buys.'

On security, they describe a new threat 'on steroids': AI agents with legitimate permissions inside your environment can drop a table in seconds. The methodology is similar to defending against ransomware, but 'the velocity of that happening is extreme.'

Their thesis: everyone can now be a builder, which is both a gift and a governance nightmare — a flood of non-human actors handling sensitive data outside the organization's rules.

If you're a company... the most valuable thing that you have is actually your data.

Two days ago you saw Google buy something from the bankrupt Spirit Airlines. They didn't buy airplanes. They bought the data. They bought the data for $10,000,000 because they think it's very important... They're using it to train models.

What we're seeing now on steroids is that the same type of threat is coming from non human actors, from AI agents that essentially have legitimate access to the environment with legitimate permissions... a table is all of a sudden dropped.

从 Meta 到 Box，AI 圈领袖都在提醒：任何一套打法、任何一个「坚定信念」的保质期都短得惊人。

中文

最扎心的一句：「传统团队为『吃老本』而优化，AI 团队必须为『发明』而优化。」

AI 产品建设有约 3 个月的 playbook 半衰期。传统团队发现打法后吃五年，AI 实验室每隔几个月就主动扔打法，因为收益递减。

传统团队为吃老本优化，AI 团队为发明优化。

English

Madhu Guru, a Senior Director of AI at Meta (previously led Gemini and Veo at Google), argues that AI product building has a '~3-month playbook half-life' — traditional teams discover a playbook and milk it for five years, but AI labs throw playbooks away every few months as returns diminish.

His prescription for builders: define meta-principles for (1) continuously learning what the market wants now versus in three months, and (2) executing with extreme urgency.

The bluntest line: 'Legacy teams are optimized for playbook milking. AI teams need to be optimized for inventing.'

AI product building has a ~3-month playbook half-life... Most trad product teams are built to discover a playbook and then milk it for the next 5 years. However in the AI labs, we consciously throw away playbooks every few months cos we start seeing diminishing returns.

Legacy teams are optimized for playbook milking. AI teams need to be optimized for inventing.

Vercel CEO 画出 web 的两个极端，Peter Yang 则点出非技术人群的 AI 产品终态。

中文

他的大胆预测：中间地带会被「按需生成 UI 的 agent」吞掉。当你需要实用内容时，它会实时为你生成——「agent 就是新浏览器」。

web 在向两个极端演进：1) 极致精美的人类体验，服务于愉悦、探索、品牌、娱乐……2) 面向 agent 的内容、数据和 API，给机器的纯粹无损信号。

中间地带会被按需生成 UI 的 agent 吞掉。agent 就是这个模型里的新浏览器。

MCP 正在爆炸式增长。

His bold prediction: the 'in between' gets subsumed by agents producing just-in-time UI. When you need utilitarian content, it'll be generated for you on demand — 'the agent as the new browser.'

He backs it with data: MCP is 'growing explosively,' and it's not just Vercel's implementation — the mcp-handler npm package shows the broader surge.

I see the web evolving in two extremes: 1) ultra-fancy human experiences, in the service of delight, exploration, brand, entertainment... 2) agent-centric content, data and APIs. the opposite end of the spectrum: pure, lossless signal for machines.

The 'in between' is likely to be subsumed by agents producing just-in-time UI... Think of the agent as the new browser in this model.
