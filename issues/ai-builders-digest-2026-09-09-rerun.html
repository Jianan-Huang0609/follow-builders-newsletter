Theme 03

The Security Gap Is Still Real / 安全缺口仍然存在

模型对齐是基础，不是完整的 prompt injection 或 agent 安全方案。

中文

Boris Cherny 提醒了一个不太舒服的事实：仅靠对齐良好的模型，暂时还解决不了 prompt injection。实践中可行的防线是分层的：所有流量默认开启 prompt-injection probes，再配合 auto mode 和少量 scaffolding。

对齐良好的模型目前还不足以单独解决 prompt injection。

把最新模型和 prompt injection probes、auto mode 叠加起来，实践中已经解决了这个问题。

English

Boris Cherny makes the uncomfortable point that well-aligned models still cannot solve prompt injection by themselves. In practice, the workable defense is layered: prompt-injection probes are enabled for all traffic, alongside auto mode and a small amount of scaffolding.

well aligned models are not sufficient to solve prompt injection by themselves yet.

we have solved prompt injection in practice when layering our latest models with prompt injection probes ... and auto mode.

Building Through the Weirdness / 在不确定里继续建

我对 AI 的未来非常乐观。

Aditya Agarwal captures the mood of the moment: he remains intensely optimistic about AI, yet genuinely anxious about the pace of change and how little we understand these complicated machines. His answer is not to wait for certainty: 'The only way out is through. Let us build.'
