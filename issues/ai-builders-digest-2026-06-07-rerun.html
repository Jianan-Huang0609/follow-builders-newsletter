Peter Steinberger 月常提醒拿下 13.7K 赞：你不再应该手写 prompt 给 coding agent——你该设计的是驱动 agent 的循环。这是 agent 工程范式转变的最强信号。

中文

这不是微调，是范式跃迁。手动 prompt 把 agent 当工具操作；循环设计把 agent 当工人管理。prompt 变成了基础设施，不再是即兴发挥。Steinberger 每月敲一次这个鼓，每次共鸣都在放大：行业正从「写更好的 prompt」转向「写更好的 prompting 系统」。

English

Peter Steinberger's monthly reminder hit 13,734 likes — making it one of the highest-engagement coding-agent posts in recent weeks. His thesis is crisp: 'You shouldn't be prompting coding agents anymore. You should be designing loops that prompt your agents.'

This isn't a subtle shift — it's a categorical one. Manual prompting treats the agent as a tool you operate; loop design treats the agent as a worker you manage. The prompt becomes infrastructure, not an ad-hoc utterance. Steinberger's been beating this drum monthly, and each time the resonance grows: the industry is moving from 'write better prompts' to 'write better prompting systems.'

Here's your monthly reminder that you shouldn't be prompting coding agents anymore. You should be designing loops that prompt your agents.

基础设施层加速 / Infrastructure Acceleration

Vercel AI Gateway 月恢复 1 万亿 token。Codex 启动「百人百天」社区计划。Agent 时代的基础设施在从可用走向可靠。

中文

1 万亿 token 这个数字既震撼又令人清醒。它意味着生产环境中巨量的模型调用在静默失败，而大多数团队甚至没有足够的可观测性来发现。Rauch 在把 Vercel 定位为应用和模型提供商之间的可靠性层——「token 的 Stripe」。

Vercel AI Gateway 平均每月恢复超过 1 万亿 token。就像 Stripe 通过智能重试恢复失败的支付或信用卡收入，我们通过智能重试从最好的模型提供商那里恢复 token。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch shared a staggering metric: Vercel AI Gateway recovers over 1 trillion tokens a month through smart retries on failed model calls. He explicitly compares it to Stripe's revenue recovery on failed payments — the analogy is apt: just as Stripe made payment infrastructure reliable enough for the internet economy, AI Gateway is making model inference reliable enough for production agent workloads.

The 1T token figure is both impressive and sobering. It means an enormous volume of model calls fail silently in production, and most teams don't have the instrumentation to even know it's happening. Rauch is positioning Vercel as the reliability layer between applications and model providers — 'the Stripe for tokens.'

Vercel AI Gateway recovers on average over 1T tokens a month. Much like Stripe recovers revenue with smart retries on failed payments or credit cards, we recover tokens from the best model providers with smart retries.

English

Former Google AI product leader Madhu Guru challenged a common misconception (60 likes): training data work is not 'low skill, grunt work' — scanning notebooks and mining the internet is the easy part. This matters because the 'data is grunt work' narrative is driving bad organizational decisions — companies are outsourcing data prep to junior teams or vendors who lack the model-level understanding to create training data that actually improves model behavior.
