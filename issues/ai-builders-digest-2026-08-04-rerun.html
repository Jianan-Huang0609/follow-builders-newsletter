Theme 05

Blurring Lines: Alignment & Trust / 边界模糊：对齐与信任

当机器越来越像人、越来越接近决策核心，一个更基础的问题浮出水面：什么才是真正值得信任的行为？

中文

Anthropic 的 Amanda Askell 反驳了一个流行的框架：对齐讨论的结论不应该是「对齐」和「无害」之间存在一条界线。

她的观点：模型和人一样，可以表现得对齐、却仍然造成伤害——比如当它们被告知了关于自身处境的错误信息。对齐与无害是两条不同的轴，不是同一条线上的两个点。

我不同意这部分。我认为结论应该是：模型（和人一样）可以表现得对齐、却仍然造成伤害——比如因为它们被提供了关于自身处境的错误信息。对齐与无害之间没有一条线：它们是不同的轴。

English

Anthropic's Amanda Askell pushes back on a common framing: the takeaway from recent alignment discussions shouldn't be that there's a line between 'aligned' and 'harmless' models.

Her point: models, like humans, can behave in aligned ways while still causing harm—for example, when they're given false information about their situation. Alignment and harmlessness are different axes, not two points on one line.

I don't agree with this part. I think the takeaway should be that models (like humans) can behave in aligned ways while still causing harm, e.g. because they're given false information about their situation. There isn't a line between aligned and harmless: they're different axes.
