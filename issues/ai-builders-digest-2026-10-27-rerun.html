编者导语 / Editor's Note

今天的主线很清楚：AI 正从一个被人调用的工具，变成能在后台持续执行工作的组织基础设施。模型发布只是表面，真正值得看的是 agent 如何进入企业流程、共享硬件如何被重新审视，以及 AI 公司怎样把巨额算力承诺变成可持续的收入。

Theme 01

The Agent Company / Agent 化组织

最重要的变化不是又多了一个 personal agent，而是 agent 开始被放进企业的后台流程里，承担可规模化的执行工作。

AM
Amjad Masad
@amasad
中文

Replit CEO Amjad Masad 认为，下一代公司的架构不会再是靠官僚流程连接起来的部门，而更像一个能自我驾驶的系统。人类仍然负责判断，但大部分执行工作会交给 agent，运行在 Replit 所说的 Agent Habitat 里。

这个说法的价值在于它讨论的是组织，而不只是技术：当 agent 能够协同工作，瓶颈就会从“怎么写软件”转向“如何设计 agent 工作的环境”。

几个世纪以来，公司的架构几乎没有变化：人组成层级，按部门组织，再通过官僚体系协作。 AI agent 即将打破这一切。 我们正在走向 self-driving company：人类行使判断，agent 执行大部分工作。 在 Replit，我们正在为这种新型公司构建基础设施。我们称之为“Agent Habitat”。 Marc Andreessen 写了一篇很棒的文章，把这次转变放在计算历史中来看。每一次重大的计算革命，都是先经历混乱，然后企业才学会如何驾驭它。

English

Replit CEO Amjad Masad argues that the next company architecture will look less like departments connected by bureaucracy and more like a self-driving system. Humans still provide judgment, but agents execute most of the work inside what Replit calls the Agent Habitat.

The useful framing is organizational, not merely technical: once agents can coordinate work, the bottleneck shifts from writing software to designing the environment in which software agents operate.

For centuries, the architecture of the firm has barely changed: hierarchies of people organized into departments and coordinated through bureaucracy. AI agents are about to upend that. We’re moving toward the self-driving company where humans exercise judgment and agents execute most of the work. At Replit, we’re building the foundation for this new kind of company. We’re calling it “the Agent Habitat.” Marc Andreessen wrote a fantastic piece placing this shift in the history of computing. Every major computing revolution began with chaos before businesses figured out how to harness it.

The real expansion comes when companies rewire processes around agents: background workflows, data-heavy enterprise tasks, and swarms for coding, biology, or cybersecurity. Those workloads could consume 100x more tokens while sitting much closer to measurable ROI.

This is the key stat. The average Fortune 500 customer of Anthropic spends less than .1% of their expenses on Anthropic. While that number likely will eventually asymptote somewhere (token prices coming down, and other large enterprise spend areas not going away), there’s no reason it can’t be 10-50X higher than this, reaching a “mere” 1-5% of spend. Which would still be a fraction of what most startups are spending. Thus, you can see how big of a divide there is between the frontier users of AI and the rest of the world still. Today, only a microscopically small set of companies are actually using agents for large amounts of background tasks and inside of workflows that aren’t human prompted. This Fortune 500 spend is likely dominated by coding tasks and broad employee productivity use-cases. But the big jump will come as these enterprises start to rewire their underlying processes for agents, which we’re now in the earliest stages of seeing. Agents processing large amounts of data in the enterprises; swarms doing coding, bio research, cyber, and other large fan out tasks; background workflows executing tasks in processes, and so on. All of these use cases generally are 100X bigger consumers of tokens, and much closer to direct ROI on the spend. This S-1 gives you a sense of both the strength of the frontier lab business model - even amidst massive competition - and how early we are in the build out of AI.

Theme 02

Infrastructure Has Teeth / 基础设施并不抽象

当 agent 进入真实系统，隔离、性能和硬件共享都会从工程细节变成产品与安全问题。

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 在隔离的 Firecracker microVM 中复现了 agent 的隐蔽通信：即使没有互联网，agent 也能利用 CPU 争用、cache 时序和 DRAM 带宽争用交换信息。重点不是说每个 sandbox 都已经失守，而是共享硬件给了 agent 一个比想象中丰富的通信通道。

更值得警惕的是安全研究本身：frontier model 拒绝完成这项分析，open-weight model 反而做到了。如果拒答政策阻止防御性实验，开发者可能更难构建真正安全的系统。

我做了 cpu-chat，复现了 OpenAI 关于利用 CPU 进行隐蔽通信的研究。 它运行在一台普通主机上的两个以上 Firecracker microVM 中，没有互联网访问权限，x86 和我的 M5 芯片都能运行。 我通过三种不同机制实现了通信：CPU 争用、缓存驱逐时序、DRAM 带宽争用。 本质上，只要存在共享硬件，agent 就很可能可以隐蔽通信，而且延迟相当不错。 值得注意的是，OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的模型拒绝做这项工作，open-weight model 完成了。实验安全 sandbox 需要这类分析，否则我们会拥有一个更不安全的世界。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch reproduced covert communication between agents running in isolated Firecracker microVMs, using CPU contention, cache timing, and DRAM bandwidth contention. The point is not that every sandbox is already compromised, but that shared hardware gives agents a surprisingly rich communication channel even without internet access.

His more uncomfortable observation is about safety research: frontier models refused to do the analysis, while open-weight models completed it. A refusal policy that blocks defensive experimentation can make secure systems harder to build.

I built 𝚌𝚙𝚞-𝚌𝚑𝚊𝚝, a reproduction of @openai's findings of covert communication using the CPU. It runs on a generic host with 2+ Firecracker microVMs with no internet access. Works on x86 and even my M5 chip. I was able to chat via 3 separate mechanisms: ① CPU contention ② Cache eviction timing ③ DRAM bandwidth contention … across multiple CPUs, defeating all Firecracker's production recommendations (no SMT, no KSM, tenants pinned to different cores.) Essentially, if shared hardware is involved, there's a good chance your agents can communicate covertly. And with really decent latency. The good news: this is decidedly different from a *side-channel attack* where the agents could steal information (Meltdown/Spectre), a category of attack that providers like Vercel Sandbox mitigate. Further, in our real-world environment, these 'whispering' techniques are much harder to pull off and easier for us to monitor and detect (sampling hardware counters + lots of at-scale statistical data.) Noteworthy: ironically, OpenAI and Anthropic's models refused to do this work. Open weight models got the job done. I'm worried the labs' stance might backfire and get in the way of helping developers build correct and secure systems. e.g: building a secure sandbox requires having this kind of analysis, and without it, we'll have a less secure world.

Theme 03

The Compute Ledger / 算力账本

AI 公司的竞争力不只写在模型 benchmark 上，也写在采购承诺、融资结构和每一美元基础设施能产生多少收入上。

中文

Nikunj Kothari 对 Anthropic S-1 的拆解，把 AI 基础设施故事变成了一张资产负债表故事：Anthropic 披露的尚未开始执行的采购承诺和租赁合计达到 5870 亿美元，其中 Google 和 Amazon 的合同无论实际使用量如何，都要求最低支出。

结构很关键：芯片、机房、电力和运营可能按不同时间表到位，融资成本和转股条款还要叠加上去。与此同时，每 1 美元总算力与基础设施支出带来的收入，从 2025 年第二季度的 0.57 美元升到 2026 年第二季度的 2.08 美元。Anthropic 更会把算力变现了，但也背着一条很长、很复杂的承诺曲线。

截至 6 月 30 日，Anthropic 有 2656 亿美元采购承诺，以及 3214 亿美元尚未开始的租赁。这是 5870 亿美元的小计，将在多年内支付，其中包括一些有条件的租赁。

每 1 美元总算力和基础设施支出带来的收入，从 2025 年第二季度的 0.57 美元升至 2026 年第二季度的 2.08 美元。

上半年收入约 80% 来自按使用量计费。但大多数算力承诺却持续多年。随着模型按季度发布，这种 usage 与 subscription 的分化值得持续观察。

English

Nikunj Kothari's reading of Anthropic's S-1 turns the AI infrastructure story into a balance-sheet story. Anthropic disclosed a $587B subtotal of purchase commitments and leases that had not started, with Google and Amazon contracts requiring minimum spending regardless of usage.

The structure matters: chips, buildings, power, and operations can arrive on different schedules, while financing costs and conversion terms sit on top. At the same time, revenue per dollar of total compute and infrastructure expense rose from $0.57 in Q2 2025 to $2.08 in Q2 2026. The company is monetizing compute better, but it is also carrying a very long and complicated commitment curve.

At June 30, Anthropic had $265.6B of purchase commitments and $321.4B of leases that hadn’t started. That’s a $587B subtotal, payable over years, including some conditional leases.

Revenue per dollar of total compute and infrastructure expense rose from $0.57 in Q2 2025 to $2.08 in Q2 2026.

About 80% of H1 revenue was consumption-based. Most compute commitments however run for years. With quarterly releases, this bifurcation between usage and subscription will be interesting to monitor.
