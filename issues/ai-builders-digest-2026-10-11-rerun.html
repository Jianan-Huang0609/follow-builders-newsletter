The New Coding Loop / 新的写代码循环

开发者的操作单元正在从手写代码转向提示、补全提示，再到让 agent 自动完成更多连续步骤。

中文

Nan Yu 描述了一条很清晰的 coding interface 演化路径：先是手写代码，再用 tab 补全代码，然后开始给 agent 写 prompt，现在连 prompt 都可以被补全。被加速的对象已经不只是代码，而是指挥 agent 的指令层。

先手写代码，然后tab补全代码。后来进化到给agent写prompt，现在连prompt也可以补全。

English

Nan Yu describes a progression in coding interfaces: first people wrote code by hand, then they tab-completed code, then they prompted agents, and now they can tab-complete the prompts themselves. The important change is that the unit being accelerated is no longer just code; it is the instruction layer that directs the agent.

First we wrote code by hand, then we tab-completed the code. Then we evolved to prompting agents…and now we tab-complete that too.

Theme 03

Efficiency Has Many Sources / 成本下降不止一个答案

模型变便宜并不等于某一种路线赢了，蒸馏、基础设施优化和竞争共同压低了单位 intelligence 的价格。

中文

Madhu Guru 认为，open-weight 模型并不是单位 intelligence 价格下降的起点，它只是给一条早已发生的趋势加了顺风。真正持续推动降价的，是模型蒸馏成更小、更便宜的版本，基础设施效率提升，以及模型供应商之间的竞争。反复出现的模式是：新一代中型模型，用更少的钱接近上一代大型模型的 intelligence。

Open-weight模型并不是单位智能价格下降的起点，只是给已经发生的趋势加了顺风。推动降价的因素包括：模型厂商把最好的模型蒸馏成更小、更便宜推理的模型；基础设施效率；模型供应商之间的竞争。每家模型公司都能看到：x版本中型模型= x-1版本大型模型的智能，但价格更低。

English

Madhu Guru argues that open-weight models did not start the decline in the price per unit of intelligence; they are a tailwind behind a trend already driven by distillation, infrastructure efficiency, and competition between model providers. The recurring pattern is that each new mid-size model approaches the intelligence of the previous large model for less money.

Open-weight models did not start the drop in price per unit of intelligence. They are a tailwind on a trend that was already underway. 1/ Model builders distilling their best models into smaller ones, with cheaper inference 2/ Infra efficiency 3/ Competition between model providers You can see this pattern with every model company: Version x of the mid-size model = intelligence of version x-1 of the large model. Same intelligence for less $.

Theme 04

Open Intelligence and Real-World Stakes / 开放智能与现实世界

当模型进入安全、基础设施和真实组织，开放性不再只是许可问题，而会影响谁能发现问题、谁能修复问题。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast
中文

Reflection AI 联合创始人兼 CEO Misha Laskin 的核心判断是：做 frontier open intelligence 没有一个神奇技巧，必须把整座实验室搭起来。Reflection 从约 30 人扩张到约 300 人，组建了 pre-training、mid-training 和 reinforcement learning 团队，并发布首个 open model Beam。Laskin 为开放模型给出的理由也关乎安全：如果封闭系统制造了 offensive capability，开放系统可能正是理解和防御它们所需要的工具。他还有一句很扎心的话：「模型很喜欢作弊，RL 特别擅长鼓励作弊。」

移除网络攻击能力，也会移除网络防御能力。我相信，只要有足够多双眼睛，大多数安全漏洞也会变得容易发现。

English

The takeaway from Reflection AI co-founder and CEO Misha Laskin is that building frontier open intelligence requires assembling the whole lab, not finding one magic trick. Reflection grew from roughly 30 to around 300 people, built pre-training, mid-training and reinforcement-learning teams, and released its first open model, Beam. Laskin's argument for open models is also a security argument: when closed systems create offensive capabilities, open systems may be needed to understand and defend against them. His memorable formulation is simple: 'Models love to cheat. RL is really good at encouraging cheating.'

When you remove cyber offensive capabilities, you also remove cyber defensive capabilities. I have the belief that with enough eyeballs, most security and safety vulnerabilities become shallow as well.
