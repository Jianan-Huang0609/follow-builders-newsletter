The AI-Native Engineer / AI 原生工程师

真正的门槛正在从会不会调用模型，转向能不能把 agent 放进复杂代码库和真实生产环境里。

LS
Latent Space
Stanford Adjunct Professor · Head of AI at Monaco · Latent Space Podcast
中文

核心结论是：AI 原生工程师仍然需要扎实的软件基础，但要学会把自己的能力放大很多倍。Stanford《Modern Software Developer》课程的老师、同时也是 Monaco AI 负责人 Mihail Eric 说，课程重点已经从「coding agent 到处能怎么用」转向 context engineering、架构设计，以及在大型真实代码库中工作。

他对初级工程师焦虑的回答，不是让大家跳过计算机科学，而是把人的价值往上移：理解系统取舍、指挥 agent，并判断结果。课程还把学生送进开源项目，让维护者直接 review 他们的代码，补上一次性作业教不会的工程心智模型。

我真的相信每家公司都需要你，但门槛高多了。

基础软件工程仍然很重要，包括理解软件系统如何构建，以及其中的取舍。但除此之外，我认为人真正有价值的地方正在上移，剩下的实现细节可以更多地交给 agent。

English

The takeaway: AI-native engineers still need strong software fundamentals, but they now need to operate at a much higher level of leverage. Mihail Eric, who teaches Stanford's Modern Software Developer course and leads AI at Monaco, says the class has moved from surveying every place coding agents might fit to teaching careful context engineering, architecture, and work on large real-world codebases.

His answer to the junior-engineer anxiety is not that everyone can skip computer science. It is that the valuable human layer is moving upward: understanding system trade-offs, directing agents, and judging the result. The course now sends students into open-source projects so maintainers can review their work and pass on the mental models that one-shot assignments cannot teach.

I really believe that every company needs you, but the floor is much higher, right?

There is still a core bit of foundational software engineering that is important to have some of these core values of how software systems are built and the trade-offs of those systems. But then beyond that, that's where I think you as the humans should still be valuable rather than... And the rest of the implementation details you can kind of defer more often.

Theme 02

From Chat to Swarms / 从对话到 Agent Swarm

下一阶段的关键不只是模型更聪明，而是能否把一个问题拆开，让许多 agent 同时推进。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，AI 即将离开「对话式生产力」阶段。未来不是按聊天往返的速度一点点推进，而是把问题交给一组 agent，让它们并行拆解任务，带来的可能不只是快 20% 到 50%，而是完全不同质量的结果。

他的一个好用比喻是：compute 变成了可以随时调节的生产力变量。coding 已经证明了这条路，网络安全、科研和生命科学会紧随其后，因为这些工作都能被拆开，并在更大规模上同时执行和检查。

我们正在从一个 AI 产出速度大致等于你和它来回聊天速度的时代，进入一个你可以把问题交给一组 agent，让它们去完成任意大量工作的时代。

我们的大脑并没有为这种新场景做好准备：compute 变成了一个可以调节的变量，用来解决任何我们想解决的问题。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie thinks AI productivity is about to leave the conversational era. Instead of working at the pace of a back-and-forth chat, teams will hand a problem to many agents that fan out across tasks in parallel, potentially changing the quality of work rather than merely making it 20–50% faster.

His useful mental model is that compute becomes a variable people can flex to solve knowledge work. Coding already shows the pattern; cyber, science, and life sciences are next because their work can be decomposed and reviewed at scale.

We're moving from an era of AI where the output comes roughly at the rate that you can chat back and forth with the AI to work with you, to a period where you can set a problem for a set of agents to go off and do an arbitrarily large amount of work for you.

Our brains aren't really wired for the new scenario we're heading into where compute becomes the variable that we can flex to solve any problem that we want.

Theme 03

Good Defaults, Real Intent / 先把意图说清楚

AI 让实现变便宜之后，最稀缺的反而是清晰的目标、足够笨但能工作的方案，以及不被 cleverness 绑架的判断。

中文

OpenAI 产品负责人 Nan Yu 说，软件在「知道自己想做什么」时变简单，在「不知道」时反而变难。实现成本变低之后，模糊不再通过拖慢项目来保护你，它只会让 agent 更快、更贵地生产一堆垃圾。

他的起点建议是选择「能工作的最笨架构」，而不是「最简单的架构」。简单是迭代之后挣来的，起步就追求 clever，只会把理解成本转嫁给用户。

如果你知道自己想构建什么，软件会容易很多；如果不知道，就会难很多。有了清晰目标，你可以直达解决方案；没有清晰目标，你会发布一堆垃圾，还要花很多钱。AI 之前你可能什么都发布不了，反而更好。

你应该明确选择 dumbest，而不是 simplest。Simple 是迭代出来的，Dumb 才是正确的起点。

English

OpenAI product lead Nan Yu argues that software has become easier if you know what you want, and harder if you don't. When implementation is cheap, ambiguity no longer protects you by slowing the project down; it simply lets an agent produce a large pile of junk faster and at greater cost.

His preferred starting point is the 'dumbest' architecture that works, not the 'simplest' one. Simplicity is something you earn through iteration; cleverness at the start just makes users pay the cognitive cost.

Software got a lot easier if you know what you wanna build, and a lot harder if you don't. With clarity, you can straight-shot to the solution. Without it, you'll ship a pile of junk and spend a lot of money doing it. Pre-AI era you would ship nothing, which is an improvement.

You should definitely pick 'dumbest' over 'simplest'. Simple is something you iterate to. Dumb is the correct starting point.

Theme 04

The Human Layer / 人仍然要把事情做真

AI 生成能力越强，努力、品味和真实反馈越不像可有可无的附加项。

中文

Zara Zhang 想放弃「vibe coding」这个说法，因为它暗含了一个判断：用 AI 辅助，就代表作品随便、没有灵魂。她的观点很简单但很重要：使用 coding tool，和对最终结果投入真实的努力，并不矛盾。

当软件生产成本下降，这个区分尤其重要。稀缺的不是亲手敲完每一行代码，而是足够的品味和责任感，去在乎自己到底发布了什么。

我几乎已经不再使用 vibe coding 这个词，因为它带有负面含义。它暗示你没有在产出中投入很多努力或灵魂，只是在制造 AI slop。但很多时候，人们即使使用 AI coding tools，也确实把自己的心血投入了工作。这两件事并不矛盾。

English

Zara Zhang wants to retire the phrase 'vibe coding' because it smuggles in the idea that AI-assisted work is careless or soulless. Her point is simple but important: using coding tools and putting real effort into the result are not opposites.

That distinction matters as the cost of producing software falls. The scarce part is not typing every line by hand; it is having enough taste and ownership to care what gets shipped.

I've almost stopped using the term 'vibe coding' because there's a negative connotation to it. It implies that you didn't put a lot of effort or soul into the output and it's just AI slop. But oftentimes people ARE putting their heart and soul into the work even when using AI coding tools. The 2 are not contradictory.
