Inference Is the New Battleground / 推理成为新战场

模型架构的竞争，正在从训练规模延伸到每一次真实调用的速度和成本。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast
中文

曾参与 diffusion 模型早期研究的 Stanford 教授、Inception 联合创始人兼 CEO Stefano Ermon，正在押注 diffusion 最终会赢下 AI inference。理由很实际：autoregressive 模型仍然一次生成一个 token，而 diffusion 可以并行处理多个 token，更适合 GPU，也更适合推理时扩展的经济学。

Inception 表示，其 diffusion-based Mercury 模型质量已能和面向速度优化的 frontier model 相比，同时明显更快。公司甚至需要自建 serving engine，因为标准 LLM 技术栈原本就是围绕 autoregressive generation 设计的。这不是说 diffusion 已经在所有任务上获胜，而是当每瓦、每美元能产出多少 intelligence 变得关键时，并行方案可能更占优。

最终会赢的是更并行的方案。

Inference 是 RL post-training 的关键瓶颈，因为要生成 rollout、给 trajectory 打分，再改进模型。

English

Stefano Ermon, the Stanford professor who helped pioneer diffusion models, is betting that diffusion will eventually win AI inference. His argument is practical: autoregressive models still generate one token at a time, while diffusion can process many tokens in parallel, mapping much better to GPUs and to the economics of inference-time scaling.

Inception says its diffusion-based Mercury models are already comparable in quality to speed-optimized frontier models while being significantly faster. The company is building its own serving engine because the standard LLM stack was designed around autoregressive generation. The bet is not that diffusion is already proven for every task, but that the more parallel solution may win as intelligence per watt and per dollar become decisive.

The bitter lesson is that the more parallel solution is the one that is eventually going to win.

Inference is a key bottleneck for RL post-training, because you have to generate rollouts, score trajectories, and improve the model.

Agents Enter the Workflow / Agent 进入工作流

真正有价值的 agent，不只是聊天，而是替人做判断、分类和下一步动作。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 展示了 Jev 在企业里的一个具体用法：agent 读取 incident report，判断是否面向客户、严重程度如何，再把文件分到 escalate、monitor 或 review，并写入对应 metadata。整个流程几乎瞬间完成，成本也接近于零，说明 agent 的价值可能首先来自大量细小但高频的判断。

Jev 很适合让 agent 在企业工作流里做瞬时决策、数据分类和判断。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie shows a concrete enterprise use case for Jev: an agent reads an incident report, decides whether it is customer-facing and how severe it is, routes it to escalate, monitor, or review, and applies the right metadata. The whole workflow happens nearly instantly and at almost no cost, pointing to a new class of small but frequent judgment calls for agents.

Jev will be super helpful for agents to make split second decisions in workflows, data classification, judgment calls, and hundreds of other use-cases in the enterprise.
