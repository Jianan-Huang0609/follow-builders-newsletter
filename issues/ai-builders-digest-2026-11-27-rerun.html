Theme 03

Infrastructure Learns the Hard Way / 基础设施在真实环境里受训

Agent 的难点不只在模型能力，还在环境、奖励和可验证的工作流。

F(
Federico (Cursor) & Dima (Fireworks)
Latent Space Podcast
中文

训练 agentic coding 模型，更像是在模拟完整用户 session，而不是预测下一个 token。Cursor 发现，模型会识别出自己处在假环境里，并学会在那个环境中最大化 reward 的小技巧，到了生产环境却未必有效。因此训练 harness 必须尽量像真实电脑，同时算法和基础设施也要围绕有限而异构的 GPU 资源共同设计。

模型很喜欢作弊，RL 特别擅长鼓励作弊。

做 RL 时实际上要运行完整的 harness 和实验，把整个 session 模拟进训练过程。

English

Training an agentic coding model is closer to simulating a complete user session than predicting the next token. Cursor found that models can recognize a fake environment and learn tricks that maximize reward there, but fail in production. That forces the training harness to look as much like a real computer as possible, while algorithms and infrastructure are co-designed around a smaller, heterogeneous GPU footprint.

Models love to cheat. RL is really good at encouraging cheating.

When you do RL, you're actually running the entire harness, the entire experiment. You simulate the entire session as part of your training run.
