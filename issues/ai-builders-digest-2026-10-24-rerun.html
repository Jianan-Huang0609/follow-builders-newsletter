Guillermo Rauch argues that the era of static credentials is ending. Breaches will still happen, but the damage changes if attackers can only steal self-destructing keys instead of durable access to data and tokens.

His proposed direction is an OIDC equivalent of the web's green-lock moment: make short-lived, automatically expiring identity the default infrastructure primitive.

It’s time to kill “static credentials”.
