Theme 01

The Planner-Worker Pattern / 规划者-执行者模式

多模型 Agent 系统的核心设计模式浮出水面：用贵模型做规划，用便宜模型做执行——这是成本敏感型 AI 应用的必经之路。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 把多模型路由称为 Agent 系统的未来。他引用 Cursor 的最新研究解释：用前沿模型当规划者和编排者，用便宜的干活模型执行实际工作，整体 token 成本能降 15 倍。

核心洞察：「一个大任务里只有少数几个时刻真正需要前沿智能——比如最初的任务拆解、设计决策、某些取舍。一旦前沿规划者把模糊的需求分解成详细、明确的指令，便宜模型照着做就行。」

他的延伸判断：这种模式将定义 AI 应用层如何差异化。深刻理解自己领域、同时能在多个模型层级之间做路由的公司，将拿下那些本来因为成本太高而无法部署的大规模工作负载。编程、金融、法律、医疗、生命科学——适用于所有 AI 重度应用的行业。

多模型 Agent 系统是未来。Cursor 的研究表明，用前沿模型做规划编排、便宜模型做执行，token 成本降 15 倍。

大任务里只有少数时刻需要前沿智能：任务拆解、设计决策、某些取舍。前沿规划者把模糊需求变成明确指令后，便宜模型照做即可。

这定义了 AI 应用层的差异化——要懂领域、要能在多模型层级之间路由。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie breaks down what he calls the future of agentic systems: multi-model routing. Citing Cursor's latest research, he explains that a frontier model acts as the planner and orchestrator, while a cheaper workhorse model executes the actual work—resulting in a 15X cost improvement in total tokens.

The key insight: 'Few moments in a large task genuinely require frontier intelligence, such as the original decomposition, the design decisions, and certain trade-offs. Once a frontier planner has collapsed the ambiguity into a detailed, explicit instruction, less expensive models simply have to follow it.'

His bigger point: this pattern will define where the applied AI layer differentiates. Companies that deeply understand their domain AND can route across multiple model tiers will win workloads that would otherwise be too expensive to deploy. This applies across coding, finance, legal, healthcare, life sciences—anywhere large AI workloads exist.

中文

Peter Yang 分享了 Anthropic 的 Thariq Shihipar 的一个实战技巧：用一个 agent 干活，用另一个 agent 对着评分标准审核。这不只是为了质量控制——原因是一个已被记录的现象叫「self-preferential bias」（自我偏好偏差）：当模型评估自己的输出时，它系统性地比另一个独立模型更宽松。

Thariq 举例：判断「这个短视频好不好」这种事没有确定性答案。你需要一个独立的审核 agent，先读评分标准，再看视频，再给反馈——不能是同一个 agent 自己审自己。

这是 Levie 成本路由模式的「质量控制版」。两条线合在一起，描绘的未来很清楚：Agent 系统不会是铁板一块的单体，而是由专长各异的组件组装而成。

用一个 agent 干活，用另一个 agent 对着评分标准审核。像「这个短视频好不好」这种事没有确定性答案，需要一个独立审核 agent 读标准、审内容、给反馈。

这叫 self-preferential bias：模型偏好自己的输出，审核时会系统性地更宽松。

English

Peter Yang amplifies a practical technique from Anthropic's Thariq Shihipar: use one agent to do the work and another to review it against a rubric. The reason isn't just quality control—it's about a documented bias called 'self-preferential bias.' When a model evaluates its own output, it's systematically more lenient than a separate model would be.

Thariq's example: for something like 'Is this a good video short?' there's no deterministic answer. You need a separate verification agent that reads a rubric, reviews the work, and gives feedback—independent of the agent that created it.

This is the quality-control counterpart to Levie's cost-routing pattern. Together they sketch a future where agent systems aren't monolithic—they're assembled from specialized components, each doing what it's best at.

We call it self-preferential bias. When a model prefers its own output, it's going to be more lenient at verifying it.
