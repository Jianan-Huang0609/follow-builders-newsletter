Model Routing Is the Next Moat / 模型路由成下一战场

Levie 预警：token 预算成为运营成本大头时，model routing 将成为 AI 应用层的最大竞争壁垒。

中文

Aaron Levie 预警下一个前沿：「随着 token 预算在运营支出中占比越来越大，模型路由是必然结论。这也是应用 AI 层相比模型提供商的最大竞争壁垒之一。」洞察：能把每个查询路由到最便宜但质量仍可接受的模型的公司，拥有随规模复利的结构性成本优势。

模型路由将 AI 公司的成本结构从线性（成本与使用量成正比）变为亚线性——并创造出模型提供商无法复制的护城河，因为他们被激励最大化使用自家模型。

随着 token 预算在运营支出中占比越来越大，模型路由是必然结论。这是应用 AI 层相比模型提供商的最大竞争壁垒之一。

English

Aaron Levie on the next frontier: 'As token budgets take on a larger part of operating expenses over time, model routing is the inevitable conclusion. This is also one of the biggest areas of differentiation for the applied AI layer over model providers.' The insight: the company that can route every query to the cheapest model that still delivers acceptable quality has a structural cost advantage that compounds with scale.

Model routing transforms an AI company's cost structure from linear (cost proportional to usage) to sub-linear — and creates a moat that pure model providers can't replicate because they're incentivized to maximize usage of their own models.

As token budgets take on a larger part of operating expenses over time, model routing is the inevitable conclusion. This is also one of the biggest areas of differentiation for the applied AI layer over model providers.
