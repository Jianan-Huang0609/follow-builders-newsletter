Brilliant people with brilliant execution often create something when there was nothing, or make the impossible seem obvious in hindsight.

Theme 02

Agent Productivity Has a Location / Agent 生产力的新坐标

生产力提升不只发生在模型里，也发生在工作流、部署方式和数据中心的地理选择里。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 说，coding 团队已经开始出现比 AI 之前高一个甚至两个数量级的生产力提升。前提是你得有合适的 harness、深厚的领域理解、相对干净的 codebase、同时驾驭多个 agent 的能力，以及几乎没有摩擦的测试和部署流程。差距没有缩小，是因为有经验的团队在换一套工作操作系统，而模型也在持续接手更大、更难的问题。

这听起来很极端，如果是一年前我也不会相信，但我们开始看到很多例子：生产力比 AI 前高一个甚至两个数量级。 有合适的 harness、对要构建的领域有深入理解、可能还需要一个相对较新的 codebase（当然也有例外）、熟练地同时使用多个 agent，并且测试和部署的摩擦很低，coding 达到这种产出水平是现实的。 疯狂的是，这个差距似乎没有随着时间缩小。你可能以为 AI 会让两条线收敛，但事实是，你过去越有深度，现在越愿意换一种方式，杠杆就越大。随着模型能处理更大、更难的问题，这种优势还会复利。 现在软件工程里最明显，但其他适合电脑操作的领域，比如科学、AEC、金融、网络安全，也没有理由不会出现类似变化。会变得非常疯狂。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie says coding teams are beginning to see productivity gains one or two orders of magnitude above pre-AI output. The catch is that this requires a real harness, deep domain knowledge, a relatively clean codebase, fluency with multiple agents, and low-friction testing and deployment. The gap is not closing because experienced teams are learning a new operating model while the models themselves take on larger, harder problems.

This sounds extreme and I wouldn’t have believed it even a year ago, but we’re starting to see plenty of examples of productivity gains that are an order of magnitude or two above output levels pre-AI. With the right harness, a deep understanding of your domain of what you want to build, likely a relatively new codebase (with some exceptions), a fluency of how to work with multiple agents at once, and a setup that makes it so you can test and deploy with limited friction, outputs on this level feel realistic in coding. What’s crazy is this gap doesn’t appear to be diminishing over time. You’d think that we’d see a convergence in the lines because of AI, but it turns out the more depth you had before -and willingness now to take a different approach- gives you crazy leverage. And that is only compounding as the models can take on larger work and harder problems. Right now this is most pronounced in software engineering for the obvious reasons. But there’s no reason we don’t start to see this in a number of other computer-use-sensitive domains like other scientific fields, AEC, finance, cyber, and more. Going to get quite wild.

中文

Levie 的基础设施判断很容易被忽略：长时间运行的 agent 不需要部署在用户身边。只要数据可以加密传入、临时处理、完成后返回结果，inference 就能放在远程的“AI 工厂”里，因为任务价值往往比那一点延迟更重要。这会扩大数据中心投资的地理版图，而选择不建设算力容量的国家，可能等于主动放弃下一层生产力。

有意思的是，从理论上说，AI agent 的 inference 几乎可以在任何地方运行。AI 是少数对返回用户的延迟没那么敏感的技术。 长时间运行的 agent 会成为常态，这意味着可以把工作发到某个地方的 AI 工厂，主要在远程数据中心完成，再把最终结果返回给用户。数据可以加密传入，让 agent 处理完后立即丢弃。 而且任务的经济价值相对延迟敏感度很高，比如一个自动化任务能带来 500 美元价值，但晚一秒返回，所以 inference 不必离用户很近。 因为 inference 是临时过程，处理地点不需要长期保存敏感数据，你可以把 AI 工厂看成短暂存在的系统，这会稍微改变数据驻留的逻辑。 综合来看，部署 AI 数据中心的地点会更灵活。愿意利用这一点的州、国家，甚至太空，都有巨大的经济机会；选择放弃的地方则会损失机会。

English

Levie's infrastructure argument is easy to miss: long-running agents do not need to sit next to the user. Because the work can be encrypted, processed temporarily, and returned as a result, inference can happen in remote 'AI factories' where latency matters less than task value. That widens the map for data-center investment, while countries that refuse to build capacity may simply give up part of the next production layer.

The interesting thing about AI agents is that as a theoretical matter, their inference can kind of run anywhere. AI is one of the few areas of technology that isn’t *as* sensitive to latency back to the user. Long running agents are now going to be the norm, which means it’s ok to send off work to an AI factory somewhere and that work can essentially be largely done within that remote data center and a final result spit back to the user. The data can pass into these systems in an encrypted manner, be combed through by agents, and then disregarded right after. And because the economic value of the task is so high relative to the latency sensitivity (eg getting back $500 in value from an automated task, but it takes an extra second to return), you don’t have to have the inference to be particularly close to the user. Also, because inference is a temporary process with no sensitive data needing to be stored long term where the inference is happening, you can effectively treat AI factories as ephemeral systems, changing some of the data residency dynamics slightly. Combined, this means you have more flexibility in where you can begin to deploy AI data centers. This is both a huge economic opportunity for the states or countries (or space) that want to take advantage of this fact. And it’s a loss to the states or countries that decide to forgo the opportunity.

Theme 03

Capacity, Openness and the Frontier / 容量、开放与前沿

模型能力的上限，正在同时受到算力供给和开放边界的约束。

中文

Aditya Agarwal 押注的是一个很朴素的容量逻辑：如果数据中心越来越难建，而算力需求继续上涨，那么手里已有容量的 hyperscaler 会变得更有战略价值。真正的瓶颈可能不是大家对模型的胃口，而是新一轮训练能以多快的速度发生。换句话说，政策可能会通过物理基础设施来决定前沿推进的速度。

如果建数据中心变得更难，那么显然应该看多那些拥有现有容量的人（也就是 hyperscaler）。 对算力的需求并没有下降，反而还在继续上升。 所以掌握算力的人会大赚。 受影响的会是新模型训练的速度。 看来我们最终还是会由政治来决定前沿的节奏。政治体发言了。

English

Aditya Agarwal makes a simple capacity bet: if data-center construction gets harder while demand for compute keeps rising, existing hyperscaler capacity becomes strategically valuable. The likely bottleneck is not appetite for models but the rate at which new training can happen. In his framing, policy may end up pacing the frontier by constraining the physical layer.

If building datacenters becomes harder, it is obvious that you should be super long anyone who has existing capacity (aka hyperscalers). It's not like the demand for the spice is going way -- it is only continuing to go up. Thus the spice holders will win big. What will suffer is the rate of new model training. I guess we will pace the frontier after all. The body politic speaketh.

The Startup Theater / 创业融资剧场

当 AI 让生产和传播都变快，融资叙事也更容易被复制；识别套路，反而成了基本能力。

中文

Nikunj Kothari 调侃 Series A 融资的段子之所以好笑，是因为每一步都似曾相识：模糊的收入曲线、连续的 launch video、看起来像买来的媒体报道、时髦的 AI 关键词，以及“我们没在融资所以没有 deck 和 data room”。他最后的括号才是重点：整套流程是讽刺，但每一句单独看都是真的。在 AI 叙事越来越容易批量生产的市场里，能把信号和表演分开，本身就是尽调能力。

好，现在来说说我们如何让投资人对我们的 Series A FOMO，拿到七份 term sheet…… 首先，我们得模糊地发收入，或者客户数。这绝对关键。不放 y 轴，只要数字一直向右走。图表最好用，当然还要让投资人 quote tweet，顺便让他们邀功，他们很喜欢这么做。要写得模糊一点，比如“慢慢但坚定地增长”或“我一看到它就知道是指数增长”。 然后你可以再拍一个 feature launch video，最好多个，这样能获得最大曝光。接着 case study 很关键，让一家趋势公司出来谈你，最好让那些 labs 在文章里提到你。这样 partner 就会发冷邮件约咖啡。 还有，你知道你可以买“顶级”媒体的“文章”，甚至可以只针对投资人投广告。你以为我在开玩笑？不，哥们，认真的。 一旦有了关注度，最重要的就是使用“宏大愿景”，以及“长周期 agent swarm、颠覆世界模型、physical AI”之类的组合。 最后最重要的是，你当然没有 deck，因为我们根本没在融资，只是明年年初可能会考虑。然后我们还被提前投资了，所以也没有 data room。上不上车随你。 然后你再说，我们在为最好的合作伙伴优化，但说实话，我们都知道我只是想要最高估值。 （再说一次，整件事是讽刺，但每一行单独看都是真的）

In a market full of AI theater, the ability to separate signal from performance is part of diligence.
