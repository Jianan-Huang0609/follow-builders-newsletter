Theme 01

Agents Become the Workflow / Agent 开始重写工作流

真正的变化不只是多一个聊天窗口，而是让 agent 进入组织原本没有自动化的流程。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 说，企业真正意义上的 agent 部署率可能还不到 1%。大多数公司只有几个让 LLM 单次做判断或反馈的系统，还谈不上真正的自动化。

随着 computer use、长运行 agent、更好的 harness 和周边基础设施成熟，机会不再只是让 agent 替你完成一个任务，而是重写底层工作流，让组织能够部署自我改进系统和 agent swarm。

绝大多数企业工作流还没有真正以有意义的方式应用 agent，目前经济体里的部署率可能低于 1%。

重点不是终端用户让 agent 完成某个任务，而是组织重构底层工作流来利用 agent。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie says enterprise agent adoption is still below 1% in any meaningful sense. Most companies have a few single-pass LLM systems, not real automation.

That is changing with computer use, long-running agents, better harnesses and surrounding infrastructure. The opportunity is not merely asking an agent to do a task, but reengineering the underlying workflow so organizations can deploy self-improving systems and agent swarms.

The vast majority of enterprise workflows have not actually had agents applied to them in any meaningful way. The economy probably has this deployed < 1% right now.

This won’t be about end users prompting agents to complete a specific task for them, but instead the organization reengineering the underlying workflows to take advantage of agents in their processes.

Builder Reality Check / Builder 的现实校准

今天的其他信号提醒我们：产品要靠真实产出说话，创业和招聘的旧筛选方式也在失效。

中文

Peter Yang 认为招聘应该减少对工作年限、公司名头、简历和无休止面试的依赖。真实项目、可验证的作品和短期工作试做，比简历上管理过多少人更能说明问题。

English

Peter Yang argues that hiring should move away from years of experience, company-name filtering, resumes and endless interviews. Proof of work, real projects and short work trials are better signals than managing a large number of people on paper.

Filtering candidates based on years of experience or some company name

Prioritizing resumes and cover letters over proof of work and real projects

Endless rounds of interviews over short work trials
