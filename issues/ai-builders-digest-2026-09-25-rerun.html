Boris Cherny 解释了一个很实用的 formal methods 中间态：Claude 会对最棘手的 state machine 或容易发生 race condition 的代码建模，找出 counterexample，再复现并修掉疑似 bug。

这不是把整个代码库都形式化验证，而是把严格的验证投入到最难靠肉眼发现问题的部分。

先建立程序模型，再寻找 counter-example、复现 bug，最后修复代码。

并不是整个代码库都已经完成 formal verification。

English

Boris Cherny explains a practical middle ground for formal methods: Claude models the hairiest state machines or race-prone parts of a program, finds counterexamples, reproduces the suspected bugs, and fixes them.

The whole codebase is not formally verified. The useful shift is narrower: spend rigorous verification effort where bugs are hardest to see.

Building a model of the program ... Finding counter-examples ... Reproducing the bugs ... Fixing the bugs in the code.

It's not that the whole codebase is formally verified (yet!).

Aditya Agarwal 认为，团队的 longevity 比 team size 更值得关注。一个合作超过三年的团队，可能比刚组建的团队有更高 throughput，也更 resilient。

他的简写是：用 AUC 看团队。别只问有多少人，还要问他们一起学习工作的时间有多久。

一起工作三年以上的团队，比刚组建的团队 throughput 更高，也更 resilient。

AUC for teams 才是正确的 metric。

English

Aditya Agarwal argues that team longevity is a better signal than team size. A team that has worked together for more than three years can be significantly higher-throughput and more resilient than a newly formed team.

His shorthand is AUC for teams: ask not only how many people are on the team, but how long they have learned to work together.

A team that has been working together for 3+ years is significantly higher throughput and more resilient than a team that has just formed.

AUC for teams is the right metric.
