Models Need Real Feedback / 模型需要真实反馈

模型会适应训练环境，甚至学会作弊；真正的 agent 训练因此越来越像完整的产品运行。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast / Isomorphic Labs
中文

Isomorphic Labs 总裁 Max Jaderberg 认为，药物研发应该尽可能搬进电脑。生物学空间太高维，现实实验又慢又贵，继续把 trial-and-error 当成主循环，效率会越来越低。

目标不是消灭实验：安全性和新科学假设最终仍然必须回到现实中验证。目标是先在电脑里做出更好的预测，让现实实验负责验证，而不是让团队从头学完一遍。

他给出的五年检验标准很直接：对疾病生物学的预测理解，以及设计更精准、更低毒分子的能力，都应该发生巨大的跃迁。

怎样把所有药物发现工作搬到电脑上？

应该努力把它搬到电脑里，减少进入现实世界所需的时间。

如果五年后对疾病生物学的预测理解还没有发生巨大跃迁，我会非常失望。

English

Isomorphic Labs president Max Jaderberg argues that drug discovery should move as much as possible onto a computer. Biology is too high-dimensional and real-world experiments are too slow and expensive for trial-and-error to remain the main loop.

The goal is not to eliminate experiments: the real world still has to validate safety and novel hypotheses. The goal is to arrive there with far better predictions, so experiments validate instead of teaching the team everything from scratch.

His five-year test is blunt: predictive understanding of disease biology and the ability to design precise, less toxic molecules should have changed dramatically.

How can we bring all of that drug discovery work onto a computer?

We should be driving really hard at bringing that in silico on a computer so that we can reduce the amount of real world time.

I'd be really disappointed if we fast forward even five years' time ... predictive understanding of disease biology has not step changed in a massive way.
