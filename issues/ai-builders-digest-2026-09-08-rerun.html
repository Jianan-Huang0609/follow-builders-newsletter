Theme 01

The New Bottleneck / 新瓶颈

当 build 变得越来越容易，稀缺资源就从执行速度转向问题选择和产品判断。

中文

FPV Ventures 合伙人 Nikunj Kothari 认为，随着 build 变容易，真正的瓶颈变成了决定做什么。AI-native 组织可能不需要很多人，但那些能选对问题的人，必须被非常好地配置起来。

越来越多人得出同一个结论：随着构建变容易，做什么变成了重要瓶颈。你可能不需要很多人，但优秀的人会非常适合 AI-native 组织。

English

FPV Ventures partner Nikunj Kothari argues that as building gets easier, deciding what to build becomes the real bottleneck. AI-native organizations may not need many people, but the few who choose the right problems will need to be exceptionally well set up.

More and more people are coming to the same conclusion.. As building gets easier, what to build becomes the important bottle neck. You might not need many of them, but the good ones are extremely well setup for the AI native organizations.

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie says the best AI opportunities may look almost economically impossible today. Builders should design for several orders of magnitude more capability or token volume, while still delivering some value now. The strategic move is to start with a barely-possible use case whose full mission becomes obvious as the models improve.

Good reminder here that with rapidly accelerating AI progress, you should likely be building with a vision that contemplates at least a few orders of magnitude of capability improvement or token volume available to you. The best opportunities right now are those where you can value to customers today with things that are barely only possible, but with a full mission that feels nearly impossible with today’s tech. There are lots of products the require orders of magnitude more processing of data (and thus are barely economically viable ideas) or where model capabilities still are in their infancy relative to what’s needed. Best to be building for those scenarios.

I don’t think we’ve reached AGI but what we have is functionally indistinguishable from AGI. Because we have a relentless programmer that doesn’t get bored or tired. So any problem that can be casted as a coding problem is virtually solved.

English

The Human Layer / 人的那一层

模型能力越强，问题越不只是“能不能做”，还包括谁来判断、谁来负责，以及我们愿意如何生活。

中文

Anthropic 哲学家和 ethicist Amanda Askell 想象过这样一个邮箱：自主 AI 模型在需要道德指导时可以主动联系。她关于 reverse CAPTCHA 的玩笑，其实也是一个紧凑的设计问题：任何机器伦理系统，都得区分真正自主的模型和被人指挥来规避规则的模型。

如果设置一个邮箱，让自主 AI 模型在寻找道德指导时可以联系它，会很酷。但这需要一个 reverse CAPTCHA，能够检测联系者既不是人类，也不是被人类指示去破解 CAPTCHA 的 AI。

English

Anthropic philosopher and ethicist Amanda Askell imagines an email address that autonomous models could contact for moral guidance. The joke about needing a reverse CAPTCHA is also a compact design problem: any future system for machine ethics has to distinguish an actual autonomous model from a human directing one to evade the rules.

It would be cool to set up an email address that autonomous AI models could reach out to if they were looking for moral guidance. But it would require a reverse captcha that can detect that you're neither a human nor an AI being instructed to break it by a human.
