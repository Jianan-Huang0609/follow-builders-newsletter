中文

Simile 创始人 Joon Sung Park——就是斯坦福那个著名 Smallville 生成式 agent 实验的创造者——用科幻的框架描述公司的使命：在任何一个技术足够成熟的虚构社会里，你总会看到两个支柱——某种 AGI，和某种用来引导决策的模拟系统。「我今天确实看到了真正开始建造这个模拟系统的机会。五年前我都不会这么说。」

Simile 的路线和大多数 LLM 公司截然不同。前沿实验室在优化理性超智能，Simile 在优化人类的不理性。「事实证明，人是不理性的。我们有大量的主观价值、偏好和品味。你会发现模型越大，在标准 benchmark 上越强，在预测和模拟人类行为上的表现反而可能越差。」模型越大，越不像真人。

商业引擎已经在跑：CVS 已经合作了半年，他们的人力洞察 SVP 用 Simile 做的事情远超问卷——模拟整个市场，画出一项商业决策的所有二阶效应。Simile 跟 Gallup 合作收集真实人类的行为数据，用真实的人生故事来构建 agent，而不是只从网上扒态度数据。Park 的野心远超商业：「模拟对人类社会的意义，就像哈勃望远镜对天文学的意义。」

Park 受科幻启发：技术成熟的社会总有两个支柱——AGI 和引导决策的模拟系统。今天确实有机会建造模拟系统，五年前我都不会这么说。

人是不理性的。模型越大，预测人类行为的能力反而会趋于停滞。当前建模范式在模拟人类方面遇到了天花板。

最伟大的科学创新始于卓越的测量工具。哈勃改变了我们对宇宙的理解，模拟可以成为人类社会的哈勃。

English

Joon Sung Park, founder of Simile and creator of the famous Smallville generative agents experiment at Stanford, describes his company's mission with a sci-fi frame: in any technologically mature fictional society, you always see two pillars—AGI and simulations that guide decision-making. 'I do see an opportunity today to really take the first crack at building the simulation. I would not have said that even five years ago.'

Simile's approach is distinct from what most LLM companies are building. Where frontier labs optimize for rational superintelligence, Simile optimizes for human irrationality. 'Turns out, people are irrational. We have a lot of subjective values, preferences and tastes. You start to see divergence in model size going up and the performance in its ability to predict and simulate human behavior.' The bigger the model gets on standard benchmarks, the worse it can get at being believably human.

Simile partners with Gallup to collect real behavioral data from real people, creating agents that are grounded in actual life stories, not just attitudinal data scraped from the web. Park's ambition extends beyond commerce: 'Simulation can be for human society what the Hubble telescope was for astronomy.'
