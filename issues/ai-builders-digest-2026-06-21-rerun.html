Theme 01

Agent Workspaces Become the Interface / Agent 工作区成为新界面

今天最值得看的不是某个单点功能，而是几个 builder 都在指向同一件事：agent 真正能工作，靠的是人和模型都能理解的共享上下文。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 这条讲得很准：agent 成不成功，关键变量是它能不能拿到完成工作所需的上下文。真正重要的 primitive 不是一个神奇的新 UI，而是一个人和 agent 都能理解的共享工作区。

他的具体判断是 filesystem：计划、笔记、任务清单、政策、草稿、摘要、日志、纠错和决策，都应该放在一个清晰的 working set 里。这就是为什么类文件系统界面对 agent 这么重要。

agent 成功的主要变量，是它能否拿到完成工作所需的上下文；其中一个关键因素，是能否创建一个 agent 和人类都能理解的共享工作区。

这就是 agent 使用文件系统为什么重要：它创造了一个人和 agent 都能工作的统一系统，用来传递数据。

它们需要的是 working set：计划、笔记、任务列表、政策、草稿、摘要、日志、纠错、决策等。对这一层来说，filesystem-shaped interface 往往对模型和监督它的人都更清晰。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie argues that the main variable in agent success is whether the agent can get the context it needs. The important primitive is not a magical new UI, but a shared working area that both humans and agents can understand.

His concrete bet is the filesystem: plans, notes, task lists, policies, drafts, summaries, logs, corrections and decisions all belong in a legible working set. That is why file-system-shaped interfaces matter so much for agents.

The main variable in getting success with agents is whether you can get the agent the context it needs to do its work; and a major factor in that is if you can create a shared working area for that agent that a human can understand as well.

This is one of the reasons why agents using file systems is such a big deal. It creates a unified system that both the person and the agent can work within to pass around data.

What they need is a working set: plans, notes, task lists, policies, drafts, summaries, logs, corrections, decisions, etc. For that layer, a filesystem-shaped interface tends to be more legible to both the model and the humans supervising it.

Industrial AI Needs Industrial Infrastructure / 工业级 AI 需要工业级基础设施

今天唯一一条播客内容很重，但值得放进来：Lip-Bu Tan 讲 Intel，不是在讲公司 PR，而是在讲 AI 时代的 supply chain、foundry、材料和资本约束。

NP
No Priors
Podcast
中文

核心 takeaway：Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan 正在按一个简单但很硬的前提重建 Intel：AI 需求不是靠模型本身就能解决的。它需要更韧性的半导体供应链、更强的 CPU、advanced packaging、新材料，以及政府级别的资本。

Tan 把 Intel 转型描述成 crawl、walk、run：先强化资产负债表，简化产品，听客户，改善 foundry 的 yield 和 defect density，再走向 full-stack systems。对 builder 最有价值的一点是：agentic AI 和 physical AI 会改变算力版图。agent 需要编排、reinforcement learning 速度和软件栈访问，不只是 GPU training capacity，所以 CPU 需求可能重新上升。

他还给了一个很实用的投资视角：找 bottleneck。interconnect、optical、AI for EDA、电源管理、散热、gallium nitride、silicon carbide、indium phosphide、glass，甚至 artificial diamond 都被他提到，因为 AI infrastructure 正在变成材料和制造问题，不只是软件故事。

最值得记住的一句话是很强的 operator 思维：'first of all, you crawl and be humble, listen to customer.' 对 Intel 这么大的公司来说，这可能不是口号，而是生存策略。

我在 Cadence 和 Intel 一直相信：首先你要 crawl，要谦逊，听客户。

现在 agentic AI 让 CPU 变得非常有需求。

在 reinforcement learning、编排所有 agents 的速度方面，CPU 实际上更好。

这是服务业务，也是信任业务。客户把 wafer 订单交给你，如果 yield 不好，他们就会马上出现收入 miss。

Power、thermal，这些正在成为 bottleneck。

English

The takeaway: Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan is trying to rebuild Intel around a simple but brutal premise: AI demand will not be solved by models alone. It needs resilient semiconductor supply chains, stronger CPUs, advanced packaging, new materials, and government-scale capital.

Tan describes his Intel turnaround as crawl, walk, run: first strengthen the balance sheet, simplify products, listen to customers, improve foundry yield and defect density, then move toward full-stack systems. His most builder-relevant point is that agentic AI and physical AI change the compute map: CPU demand can rise as agents need orchestration, reinforcement learning speed and software-stack access, not just GPU training capacity.

He also gives a useful investor lens: look for bottlenecks. Interconnect, optical, EDA with AI, power management, thermal constraints, gallium nitride, silicon carbide, indium phosphide, glass and even artificial diamond all show up because AI infrastructure is becoming a materials and manufacturing problem, not just a software story.

The memorable line is pure operator thinking: 'first of all, you crawl and be humble, listen to customer.' For a company as large as Intel, that may be less slogan than survival strategy.

I always believed in when I was at Cadence and also at Intel is, first of all, you crawl and then be humble, listen to customer.
