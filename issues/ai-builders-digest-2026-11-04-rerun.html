模型能力、agent harness 和组织采用速度，正在把人与人之间的产出差距拉大。

中文

他的估计很激进：两年时间里，生产力提升的上限从大约 30–50% 变成了视领域而定的 5–10 倍。站在前沿的人和完全不关注 AI 的人之间，正在出现数量级差距。

生产力提升上限从 30–50% 变成了视领域而定的 5–10 倍。

考虑到 RSI 可能带来的进展，这个差距只会继续扩大。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie thinks the divergence in AI output may be at its widest even as adoption becomes mainstream. Better models, longer-running tasks, agentic harnesses, stronger datasets, and multi-agent systems have changed the ceiling of productivity.

His estimate is stark: in two years, the upper limit moved from roughly 30–50% better productivity to 5–10X depending on the field. The gap between people at the frontier and people ignoring AI is becoming an order-of-magnitude gap.

The upper limits on productivity gains have gone from 30-50% to now likely 5-10X depending on the field.

The distance is only getting wider from here given the rate of progress we're likely to see with RSI.

Theme 04

AI Cloud Is Software First / AI Cloud 首先是软件问题

GPU 只是入口，真正的竞争在于把不断变化的硬件变成可用的云产品。

UL
Unsupervised Learning
Ep 96 · Nebius CTO
中文

一句话 takeaway：AI cloud 不是 GPU 堆场。Nebius CTO Neela 认为，真正的护城河是软件，既能服务半小时只用一块 GPU 的客户，也能服务一次消耗数万块 GPU 的超大集群。

Nebius 把自己定义成 software-first：硬件是地基，但 virtualization、storage、networking、隔离能力，以及对不断变化的 NVIDIA 架构的适配，才是把容量变成产品的关键。她有一句很形象的话：既要做大规模，又要几乎像零售一样卖出去。

容量本身也没有解决需求问题。训练客户现在会把共享存储推到每秒数百 GB，而独立 AI cloud 争夺的是那些想成为大公司的中型客户。

我们的产品和业务建立在一个判断上：软件才是护城河。

既能构建大规模，又几乎像零售一样卖出去，这非常重要。

我们是在硬件的坚实基础上构建 cloud，不是一家 hardware-first 公司。

关键是吞吐量，也是延迟。

English

The takeaway: an AI cloud is not a pile of GPUs. Nebius CTO Neela argues that the moat is software that can serve both a customer running one GPU for half an hour and a customer consuming tens of thousands of GPUs in one interconnected cluster.

Nebius describes itself as software-first: hardware provides the foundation, but virtualization, storage, networking, isolation, and support for constantly changing NVIDIA architectures are what turn capacity into a product. The CTO's memorable line is that they are building large scale and selling it almost like retail.

The demand problem is not solved by capacity alone. Training customers are now routinely pushing shared storage to hundreds of gigabytes per second, while independent AI clouds compete for the mid-tier companies that want to become large companies.

Our product and business was launched on the idea that software is the moat.

We believe that this is extremely important. Being able to build large scale, but have the possibility almost like sell it in retail in a sense.

We are building the cloud on the solid foundation of hardware. We're not a hardware first company.

It's about throughput. It's about latency.
