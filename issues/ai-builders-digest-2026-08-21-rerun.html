Enterprise AI Goes Deep / 企业 AI 的内化时代

模型能力越强，企业客户的问题就从「能不能用」变成了「数据归谁」——以及要不要为自家业务专门训练模型。

中文

这是企业信任终于追上模型前沿的节奏：前沿模型越强，合规门槛越高，而「你的数据我们一份不留」正成为大客户唯一能接受的答案。

我们和客户已经一起打磨了一段时间。Mythos 级模型需要额外的安全措施，企业也需要满足自己的隐私与合规要求。客户可以自己拥有并控制数据，Anthropic 不保留任何数据。今年秋天推出。

Box CEO Aaron Levie 分享了一篇关于应用型 AI 后训练的文章：一旦你把一个领域理解得足够深、同类任务量足够大，就值得为这份工作专门设计模型——通过 reward shaping 同时优化成本与质量，比如偏好那些推理时消耗更少 token 但效果相当的轨迹。

他的判断是：这不会在所有领域都成立——通用前沿模型对大多数工作已经够用（甚至是必需的）——但对那些有深厚垂直经验、规模成本高或任务类型独特的公司，这条路很有吸引力。「很高兴看到市场上现在有这么多条通向胜利的路。」

这篇关于应用型 AI 用例后训练的文章很棒：降低特定任务的成本、提升准确率。这越来越会成为那些能贴近企业底层工作流的公司采用的方法。

关键在于：一旦你把一个领域理解得足够深、同类任务量足够大，为这份工作专门设计模型就变得合理了。

在后训练中，我们通过 reward shaping 激励高效的工具使用和推理，偏好那些推理时消耗更少 token 但效果相当的轨迹。这让我们能同时优化成本和质量，在保持成本稳定的同时获得显著的性能提升。

这不会在所有领域都成立——通用前沿模型（开源或闭源）对眼前的工作往往开箱即用，或者说必不可少。但一旦你有足够深的垂直专长，要么规模成本太高，要么任务类型足够独特，这条路就非常说得通。

对应用型 AI 公司来说这是非常有说服力的价值主张，很高兴看到市场上现在有这么多条通向胜利的路。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie spotlights what post-training looks like for applied AI: once you understand a domain deeply and have enough volume of similar tasks, it starts to make sense to purpose-design models for that specific work — using reward shaping to co-optimize cost and quality, e.g. preferring trajectories that consume fewer tokens at inference time for equivalent performance.

His judgment: this won't make sense everywhere — general-purpose frontier models will be good enough (or necessary) for most work — but for companies with deep vertical expertise, high scale costs, or unique task types, it's a compelling path. "Awesome to see multiple paths to winning in the market right now."

Great post on what post training looks like for applied AI use-cases to bring down costs and improve accuracy on certain tasks. This will increasingly be an approach that companies that can get closer to the underlying workflow in an enterprise will take.

The key is that once you understand a domain well enough and have enough volume on a set of similar tasks, it can start to make sense to purpose design models just for that work.

In post-training, we incentivized efficient tool use and reasoning through reward shaping, preferring trajectories that would reduce tokens consumed at inference-time given equivalent performance. This allowed us to co-optimize for both cost and quality, gaining significant performance while keeping cost stable.

Now, this won't make sense in every domain, as general purpose frontier closed or open models will be good enough out of the box -or necessary- for the work at hand. But once you have deep enough vertical expertise, and either the costs are too high to do at scale *or* you have a unique enough task type not being trained on otherwise, this will make a ton of sense.

Very compelling value proposition for being an applied AI company, and awesome to see multiple paths to winning in the market right now.

Making AI Output Actually Good / 让 AI 输出真正变好

企业做不好 AI 系统的根因往往不是模型不行，而是没有 eval 策略；有时候，一句「再想想」就够了。

中文

Meta 的 AI 总监 Madhu Guru（前 Google，带过 Gemini、Veo、Nano Banana）继续他的「如何构建好的 eval」系列，观点很直接：企业做不好 AI 系统，根因多半是缺少 eval 策略——不是模型不行。

他给出了一套按成本/真实度光谱排列的分层 eval 体系：hill-climb eval 用来推高产品边界、需要持续刷新；regression eval 用来发现爬坡时弄坏了什么；smoke test 守护安全和产品身份这类绝对不能错的底线；launch eval 最接近真实流量——控制力最弱，但信号最诚实。

如何构建好的 eval - 第 4 部分

企业在构建像样的 AI 系统上挣扎的根因，是缺少 eval 策略。

你需要一套针对自己独特用例的分层 eval 策略，覆盖成本/真实度光谱上的多个 eval。

你需要的关键 eval 类型：Hill-climb eval 推动产品边界，需持续刷新以提升质量、扩展功能；Regression eval 检查爬坡时有没有弄坏今天的产品；Smoke test 守护安全和绝对底线，不一定要难，但绝不能错（比如产品身份）；Launch eval 接近真实流量的在线测试，控制力弱，但最真实。

English

Meta's AI director Madhu Guru (ex-Google, where he led Gemini, Veo and Nano Banana) continues his "how to build great evals" series with a blunt thesis: enterprises struggle to build decent AI systems mostly because they lack an eval strategy — not because the models are bad.

He lays out a laddered eval stack across the cost/realism spectrum: hill-climb evals that push your product frontier and need constant refreshing, regression evals to catch what you broke while climbing, smoke tests for safety and basics like product identity, and launch evals that approximate real traffic — less control, but the most honest signal.

How to build great evals - part 4

The reason enterprises struggle with building decent AI systems is the lack of an eval strategy.

You need a laddered eval strategy, for your unique use cases, with multiple evals on the cost/realism spectrum.

Here are the key kinds of evals you need: Hill-climb evals: this pushes the frontier of your product and you need to continually refresh this to improve quality and expand your feature base. Regression evals: Did we break the product of today while hill climbing? Smoke test evals: safety and the absolute basics that just can't go wrong. Not necessarily hard questions, but important to not get wrong. Eg product identity. Launch evals: often close to an online test with real-ish traffic. Less control, but the most realistic.

English

大脑非常字面上、非常清晰地、就是一台计算机。
