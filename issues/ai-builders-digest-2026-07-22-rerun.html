Theme 01

Product Sense is the New Moat / 产品感是新的护城河

Madhu Guru——这位从 Web3 转向 AI 的 builder——提出了一个让所有人停下来的观点：在模型能力拉平的时代，产品判断力是最后一个不被商品化的竞争力。

中文

Madhu Guru 抛出了一个和主流「造更多、更快」叙事相反的观点：现在是有产品感最好的时代。逻辑是反直觉但精准的——当 AI 让执行变得便宜且充裕，瓶颈就从「能不能造」变成了「该不该造，为谁造」。

100 个赞说明这个观点打中了很多人没说出口的感受：工具已经民主化了，但品味没有。赢的人不是能跑最多 agent 的人，而是知道把 agent 往哪指的人。

毫不夸张地说，现在是有产品感最好的时代。

English

Madhu Guru drops a thesis that cuts against the grain of 'build faster, ship more': it's literally the greatest time ever to have product sense. His logic is counterintuitive but precise—when AI makes execution cheap and abundant, the bottleneck shifts from 'can we build it' to 'should we build it, and for whom.'

A hundred likes on this take suggests it resonated because it names what many builders are feeling but haven't articulated: the tooling has democratized, but taste hasn't. The people who win won't be the ones who can spin up the most agents—they'll be the ones who know exactly where to point them.

No Moats, Only Speed / 没有护城河，只有速度

Booking CEO Glenn Fogel 用 27 年从濒死走到 $1800 亿市值的经历，和 VC Nikunj Kothari 对创业者的一句警告，从两个方向击穿了「护城河」这个概念。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast
中文

Glenn Fogel 的职业生涯会让任何 AI 炒作周期显得渺小。他在 2000 年加入 Priceline——正好在纳斯达克见顶之后。股价从 $300 亿市值跌到 $1 一股。他没走。反向拆股到 $6。27 年后接近 $6000——四分之一个世纪涨了一千倍。去年 Booking 做了 $1860 亿旅行预订，超过 10 亿间夜。

这不是失败主义。这是一个亲眼看到 $300 亿的公司塌到几亿、再爬到 $1800 亿的人才会有的视角。经历过这些之后，你不信护城河。你信速度、执行、永不安逸。

不存在护城河这种东西。没有哪里能保护你免受创新冲击。今天我们确实在某些领域有竞争优势，但这些明天就可能消失。长期来看唯一能赢的方法是不断开发新服务、新做法。

我们上市时市值 $300 亿……到我加入时大概掉到 $150 亿。九个月后只剩几亿。股价 $1 一股。我们做了反向拆股到 $6。我留下来了，已经 27 年。从 $6 走到去年夏天接近 $6000，涨了一千倍。

Glenn Fogel has one of those career arcs that makes the AI hype cycle feel small. He joined Priceline in 2000—right after the Nasdaq peaked. Our stock went from where we were when we went public, we were $30 billion... by the time I joined, we're probably down about $15 billion. In nine months, our market cap is now down to just a couple hundred million. Our stock is now trading at a dollar a share. We do a reverse split. It goes to $6 a share. I stay, and I've been there twenty seven years. We went from that reverse split $6 to last summer, we came very close to $6,000. Up a thousand times.

Theme 03

The Economics of Intelligence / 智能的经济学

从 Aaron Levie 的 15X 成本优化到 Garry Tan 的'Compute 决定一切'，今天的讨论在追问同一个问题：AI 部署的成本结构会如何重塑竞争格局？

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 把多模型 Agent 系统定位为 AI 部署的决定性经济模式。引用 Cursor 的最新研究，他解释了核心洞察：用前沿模型做规划与编排，用便宜的干活模型执行，总 token 成本改善 15 倍。

这个经济逻辑迷人的地方在于，它映射了人类组织已经存在的工作方式——资深的人做决策，初级的人执行。但应用到 AI 上，它创造了一种全新类型的商业模式杠杆。深刻理解自己领域、同时能在多个模型层级之间做智能路由的公司，将拿下那些本来因成本太高而无法部署的大规模工作负载。这就是 Fogel 在 Booking 规模上讨论的 token 经济学的 builder 版本。

多模型 Agent 系统是未来。Cursor 的研究表明，用前沿模型做规划编排、便宜模型做执行，token 成本降 15 倍。这定义了 AI 应用层的差异化——要懂领域、要能在多模型层级之间路由。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie frames multi-model agentic systems as the defining economic pattern of AI deployment. Citing Cursor's new research, he explains the core insight: use a frontier model as a planner and orchestrator, and a cheaper workhorse model for execution, resulting in a 15X improvement in total token costs.

The economic logic is seductive because it mirrors how human organizations already work—senior people make the decisions, junior people execute. But applied to AI, it creates a new kind of business model leverage. Companies that deeply understand their domain AND can route intelligently across model tiers will win workloads that would otherwise be too expensive to deploy. This is the builder version of what Fogel was saying about token economics at Booking scale.

Multi-model agentic systems clearly are the future. Great post by cursor. Their new research shows that a frontier model as a planner and orchestrator with a workhorse cheaper model can materially lower the cost of total tokens on a project, resulting in a 15X cost improvement.

This provides the template for where the applied layer in AI will differentiate. You can only drive this efficiency if you know the domain well and have the ability to work with multiple model tiers.
