从模型分数到任务完成 / From Scores to Finished Work

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 说，Gemini 4 Argon 在 Box 的 Complex Work 评测中比 Gemini 3.1 Pro 高了 14 个百分点，测试内容是跨行业的复杂企业文档任务。

真正有意思的不是分数，而是它改变了哪些失败方式：Argon 更能把任务做完，保留题目要求的细节，发现缺失数据，并让最终建议和自己的判断保持一致。Box 给出的例子包括准确完成 funding-gap 计算、从护理方案里移除危险药物、选对该优化的地区，以及发现仪表数据并不完整。

模型的价值正在从“写出一段像样的话”，转向“把一串工作做完，而且不悄悄漏掉最难的部分”。

Gemini 4 Argon 在 Box AI Complex Work 评测中比 Gemini 3.1 Pro 高了 14 个百分点。

它在数学、逻辑、分析和领域知识上更强，也更努力地把任务完整做完，而不是半途停下。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie says Gemini 4 Argon jumped 14 points over Gemini 3.1 Pro on Box's Complex Work evaluation, which uses hard enterprise-document tasks across industries.

The interesting part is not the headline score but the failure mode it changes: Argon was more likely to carry a task through, preserve the requested detail, catch missing data, and make its final recommendation consistent with its own diagnosis. In Box's examples, that meant correct funding-gap math, removing dangerous drugs from a care plan, choosing the right region to optimize, and noticing an incomplete meter log.

The model is becoming more useful when the job is not to produce a plausible paragraph, but to finish a chain of work without quietly dropping the hard parts.

At Box, we've been testing Gemini 4 Argon on our Box AI Complex Work eval for the past week. It's a huge upgrade over Gemini 3.1 Pro, jumping by 14 percentage points across all our tests, and firmly at the frontier.

Gemini 4 is far more capable on math, logic, analytics, and deep knowledge ... It also works far harder to complete tasks fully without stopping short of the desired result.

Agent 的安全覆盖面 / The Agent Security Surface

Aaron Levie 认为，网络安全会成为未来 agent 最大的应用空间之一，恰恰因为 agent 会同时放大攻防两面：它们可以顺着系统每个缝隙扩大攻击面，也可以让安全团队审查每一行代码、测试海量 exploit 组合，并比人更快调查事故。

机会不只属于某一个安全产品，而是横跨 labs、数据提供商、网络与 firewall、身份、端点、SDLC，以及企业数据保护。

网络安全会成为未来 AI agent 最大的领域之一。

English

Aaron Levie sees cybersecurity as one of the biggest future spaces for AI agents, precisely because agents expand both sides of the equation. They can increase the attack surface by working through every crack in a system, but they can also let security teams review every line of code, test huge numbers of exploit permutations, and investigate incidents faster than humans.

The opportunity is not a single security product. It spans labs, data providers, networks and firewalls, identity, endpoints, the SDLC, and enterprise data protection.

Cyber is going to be one of the biggest spaces for AI agents in the future.

The ability to have agent swarms review every line of code, test any number of permutations of exploits, and research any incident faster than humans, will be a huge boost in how we secure systems in the future.

另一条通往智能的路 / Learning from Experience

TD
Training Data
Training Data
中文

Ineffable Intelligence 的 David Silver 和 Lasse Espeholt 押注的是另一条路线：超级智能来自通过经验学习、自己发现知识的系统，而不只是来自在人类语言上训练的模型。Silver 的核心类比是人类历史：我们一开始什么都不知道，却集体发现了语言、数学、科学和技术。

他们的反共识选择既是技术性的，也是组织性的。Silver 说，离开 DeepMind 是因为这条路线值得一个全力聚焦的顶级实验室，而不是 LLM 产品竞赛里的一个角落。不过，LLM 时代也教会他一件重要的事：当系统在足够大的规模上见过足够多数据后，原本看起来很慢、很难的能力，经过 fine-tuning 可能会突然变快。

他们押注的是一个很大的目标：造出能够永远持续学习、持续发现知识的系统。

我们学到的一切，都是自己发现的。这是 AI 还没有破解的一件不可思议的事。

解决 AI 的正确路径是从经验中学习，让系统自己发现事物；这会带来智能，最终带来超级智能。

如果规模合适，很多看起来非常难、非常慢的事情，在那之后都可能变得非常快。

English

David Silver and Lasse Espeholt of Ineffable Intelligence are betting that superintelligence will come from systems that learn and discover through experience, not only from models trained on human language. Silver's core analogy is human history: we began knowing nothing and collectively discovered languages, mathematics, science, and technology.

Their contrarian move is institutional as much as technical. Silver says he left DeepMind because this path deserved an elite lab focused on it completely, rather than another corner of the LLM product race. The LLM era still taught him something important: once a system has seen enough data at scale, capabilities that seemed slow or difficult can become surprisingly fast after fine-tuning.

The wager is ambitious: build a system that can keep learning and discovering knowledge forever.

Everything we learned, we discovered it for ourselves. And that's an incredible thing that no one has cracked yet in AI.

The right approach to solve AI was through learning from experience, through systems that discovered things for themselves ... and that would be the thing which would lead to the emergence of intelligence and ultimately superintelligence.

If you get things at the right scale, a lot of things which seem incredibly hard or they might be very slow, ... after that, the things that were slow can become very fast.
