Serval 联合创始人兼 CEO Jake Stauch 的核心判断是：企业 agent 要想真正自主，guardrails 就必须是产品本身。Serval 把 coding agent 交给 IT 团队，同时加上审批、staging、确定性 workflow 和 audit trail，让 agent 可以在获得更高权限的情况下安全触碰企业系统。

Stauch 更激进的地方在于，他选择直接挑战 ServiceNow，而不是在它上面再加一层薄薄的 AI。因为在他看来，code 比拖拽式 workflow builder 更适合表达企业自动化，而 AI 终于让真正负责内部系统的人也能用 code 来构建。

他有一句很准确的话：“Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails.” 对大型、受监管的企业来说，个人 agent 的魔法感不如可追溯性重要：谁批准了动作、agent 做了什么、为什么允许它做，都必须说得清楚。

另一方面，自主性在企业里很可怕。Serval 是边界，Serval 是 guardrails。我们让拥有大量规则、审批、流程和政治因素的大型企业，可以安全、放心地规模化采用这些工具。

所以，如何构建一个让所有人都容易成为 builder 的系统？核心洞察是，IT 确实想要构建东西。

English

The takeaway from Serval co-founder and CEO Jake Stauch is that enterprise autonomy only works when guardrails are treated as the core product. Serval brings coding agents to IT teams, then wraps them in approvals, staging, deterministic workflows and an audit trail so they can safely touch systems with elevated permissions.

Stauch's contrarian bet is to attack ServiceNow directly rather than build a thin AI layer on top of it. His reasoning is that code is a better way to express enterprise automation than drag-and-drop workflow builders, and AI finally makes code accessible to the people who actually run internal systems.

The memorable line is blunt: “Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails.” In large, regulated companies, the magical personal-agent experience is less important than a system that can explain who approved an action, what it did, and why it was allowed.

On the other hand, autonomy is very scary in the enterprise. Serval is the boundary. Serval is the guardrails. We are the way that a large enterprise with a lot of rules and approvals and process and politics can adopt these tools safely and comfortably at scale.

And so it's how do you build a system that makes it easy for everyone to be a builder. And the core insight here was that IT actually wants to build stuff.
