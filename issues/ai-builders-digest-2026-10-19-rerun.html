Peter Yang 给出了一个适合“Google 账号连了几十个 App 和 agent”的审计 prompt：逐一检查连接，区分普通登录和 Gmail、Drive、Calendar 等数据权限，标记权限过宽、陌生供应商与重复连接，并在任何改动前先给出建议。

先展示建议，只有我明确批准的连接才能断开，然后验证确实移除了。

English

Peter Yang offers a prompt for people with dozens of apps and agents connected to Google: audit every connection, distinguish sign-in from access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar and other data, flag broad or unfamiliar permissions, and show recommendations before changing anything.

The workflow is deliberately cautious: ask which apps are still used, explain what might break, require explicit approval, then verify the disconnection. As agents gain more access, permission hygiene becomes an everyday operating skill.

If you have way too many apps and agents (45!) connected to your Google account like me, here's a prompt to audit and remove them:

Show me your recommendations before changing anything. Only disconnect apps I explicitly approve, then verify they're removed.

Theme 02

The Agent Stack Gets Real / Agent 技术栈进入深水区

当 agent 真正写入数据库、运行代码和接受训练，基础设施必须面对速度、隔离与作弊。

AP
Andy Pavlo
The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
中文

数据库教授 Andy Pavlo 认为，AI 和数据库的关系已经走过了 vector database 时代。现在 agent 会创建、branch、写入，甚至删除数据库，核心问题变成：如何让它们在不碰生产环境的情况下大胆试错。

他有一句很醒目的警告：「不了解数据库历史的人，正在面对一片西部荒野。」数据库 branching 提供了一种实用的安全模式：给 agent 一个真实数据的 snapshot，让它在副本上改，验证通过后再把改动上线。

更大的变化是架构层面的：数据库不再只是应用背后的被动存储，而会成为 agent 的工作空间，因此隔离、审计和恢复都要变成一等产品能力。

每个月都会有 agent 删除生产数据库内容、导致数据丢失的故事。

不了解数据库历史的人，正在面对一片西部荒野。

Branching 可以对真实数据库做 snapshot，让所有改动发生在 snapshot 上而不影响生产环境。

English

Database professor Andy Pavlo argues that the AI/database relationship has moved beyond the vector-database moment. Agents now create, branch, write to, and sometimes delete databases, so the core question becomes how to let them experiment without touching the production system.

His memorable warning is that agent development is moving at frightening speed: "It's the wild west for people that don't know their history databases." Database branching offers a practical safety pattern—give an agent a real snapshot, let it change the copy, then promote only vetted changes.

The bigger shift is architectural: databases are no longer passive storage behind an application. They are becoming an active workspace for agents, which makes isolation, auditability and recovery first-class product requirements.

Every month, there's a story where someone lost their production database because the agent deleted something.

It's the wild west for people that don't know their history databases.

Branching allows you to make a snapshot of the real database, have all the changes go to the snapshot and not affect production.

The Builder Economy / Builder 经济的下一层

工具更容易用之后，竞争焦点转向分发、用户选择和 AI 时代的商业组织方式。

中文

Swyx 分享了一个 AI 内容频道背后的分发数据：从 5 月开始，月度独立观众基本一直超过 100 万，但其中 58% 仍是新观众，68% 没有订阅。观看高峰在太平洋时间早上 7–11 点，下午 5 点后几乎没人看。

这不只是一个漂亮的受众数字，更说明转化和时间段才是难题：触达越来越容易，把一次发现变成长期关系才是产品问题。

从五月起月度独立观众基本一直超过 100 万。

每月 58% 的观众是新观众，68% 没有订阅。

观众主要在太平洋时间每天 7–11 点观看，下午 5 点后没人看。

English

Swyx shares the distribution reality behind an AI-focused media channel: more than one million unique viewers a month since May, but 58% are still new viewers and 68% are not subscribed. Most viewing happens from 7–11am PT; almost nobody watches after 5pm.

The lesson is less about a vanity audience number than about conversion and timing. Reach is increasingly abundant; turning discovery into a durable relationship is the harder product problem.
