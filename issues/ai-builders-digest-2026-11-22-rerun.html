From Models to Molecules / 从模型走到分子

真正的垂直应用不会停在 demo：它要把模型、领域知识和现实世界的反馈闭环接起来。

NP
No Priors
No Priors · President, Isomorphic Labs
中文

Isomorphic Labs 总裁 Max Jaderberg 把药物研发描述成一个巨大而高维的设计问题，而行业至今仍主要依赖试错。公司的核心目标，是把药物发现、药物设计和疾病生物学搬到计算机里，因为合成真实分子、再用真实实验测试，仍然是时间和成本最大的瓶颈。

组织方式和模型同样重要：Isomorphic 把 AI 研究者、biology 专家和 chemistry 专家放在一起，在内部培养跨学科能力。这样每个药物项目都能反哺同一个平台，让经验不断复利，而不是困在一次性的定制项目里。

怎样把所有药物发现工作搬到计算机上？怎样摆脱真实世界的限制？

最大的时间瓶颈、最大的成本瓶颈，是合成真实分子，再在真实 assay 和实验中测试。

如果底层有一个平台不断吸收经验，那么做的药物越多，就越有希望获得复利式提升。

English

Isomorphic Labs president Max Jaderberg describes drug discovery as a huge, high-dimensional design problem still dominated by trial and error. The company's north star is to bring drug discovery, drug design, and disease biology onto a computer, because synthesizing real atoms and testing them in real assays remains the biggest time and cost bottleneck.

The organizational implication is just as important as the model: Isomorphic combines core AI researchers with biology and chemistry experts, then builds cross-disciplinary expertise internally. The goal is for each medicine project to improve a shared platform, so learnings compound instead of remaining trapped in bespoke projects.

How can we bring all of that drug discovery work onto a computer? So how can we get out of the real world?

The big time bottleneck, the big cost bottleneck is synthesizing real atoms and then testing them out in real assays and experiments.
