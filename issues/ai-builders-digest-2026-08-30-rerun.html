From Sight to Substrate / 从复明到基质独立

Neuralink 联合创始人 Max Hodak 谈视网膜假体获批、大脑作为计算机，以及基质独立。

NP
No Priors
No Priors Podcast
中文

Neuralink 联合创始人、现 Science 创始人兼 CEO 的 Max Hodak 提出一个看似简单却极具颠覆性的论断：大脑真的、字面意义上就是一台计算机，而如果你能把视觉、听觉、平衡、运动的信号输入和输出大脑，这「本身就是目的，是核心对象」。

概念验证就是 Prima——Science 的视网膜植入物（「眼睛的耳蜗植入」），刚刚拿到欧洲 CE 批准，首批商业化销售几周内开始。他对路线图直言不讳：现在它像「透过一根吸管看世界」、还只是黑白的，但灰度和彩色只是工程路径上的下一步。

更深的议程是「基质独立」（substrate independence）：他认为两大死因——心血管疾病和转移到大脑的癌症——都将变得可攻克，还半开玩笑说自己「最终要是被自己的胰腺杀死，会相当失望」。

最有意思的科学论断是「柏拉图表征假说」：大模型内部的数学对象和神经科学在大脑里发现的东西非常像，而 Science 已经找到了动物神经记录与 AI 模型内部表征之间的对齐。他的反直觉建议：现在研究神经科学最肥沃的方式，是去做 AI。

大脑真的、字面意义上是台计算机……如果你能把视觉、听觉、平衡、运动的信号输入输出大脑，这本身就是目的，是核心对象。

现在的视网膜假体……之前从没有人能以这种方式在盲人患者的脑海中恢复有形态的视觉图像。它像透过吸管看世界，只有黑白，我们需要加上灰度的层次。

我最终要是被自己的胰腺杀死，会相当失望。

讽刺的是，研究神经科学现在最肥沃的方式，大概是去做 AI……在模型上做神经科学要容易得多。

English

Max Hodak, co-founder of Neuralink and now founder/CEO of Science, argues a deceptively simple thesis: the brain is very literally a computer, and if you can get visual, auditory, balance and motor signals in and out of it, that's 'an end in itself.'

The proof of concept is Prima, Science's retinal implant — a 'cochlear implant for the eye' — which just got CE approval in Europe, with the first commercial sales starting in weeks. He's blunt about the roadmap: today it's like 'looking through a straw' and only black and white, but grayscale and color are an engineering path away.

The deeper agenda is substrate independence: he thinks the two leading causes of death — cardiovascular disease and cancer that metastasized to the brain — become attackable, and he half-jokes he'd 'be disappointed if I'm murdered by my pancreas.'

His most intriguing scientific claim is the platonic representation hypothesis: the mathematical objects inside large AI models look a lot like what neuroscience finds in the brain, and Science has already found alignments between animal neural recordings and AI model internal representations. His counterintuitive advice: the most fertile way to study neuroscience right now is to work on AI.

The brain very literally, very clearly, plainly is a computer... If you can get the visual signal, auditory signal, balance, motor, in and out of the brain, that is an end in itself. That is the central object.

The retinal prosthesis right now... Nobody had previously ever been able to restore a form vision image in the mind's eye of a blind patient in this way. It's like looking through a straw. It's only black and white. We need to add depth of grayscale.

I'm gonna be ultimately fairly disappointed if I'm murdered by my pancreas.

ironically, it's probably working on AI... it is just way easier to do neuroscience on the models.
