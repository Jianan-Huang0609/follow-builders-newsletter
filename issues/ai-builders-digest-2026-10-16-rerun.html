中文

Garry Tan 认为，资产征收税在百分比上看起来不大，但对财富锁定在私人公司股权里的创始人来说，金额可能完全无法承受。他举的例子是：一个持有公司 50% 股权、公司估值 20 亿美元、同时拥有 20 倍投票权的创始人，可能要为账面财富缴税，而这些财富并不能直接变成现金。

你是一个持有公司 50% 股权、拥有 20 倍投票权的创始人。你要对 100 亿美元缴 5% 的资产征收税，也就是对你的 10 亿美元账面财富缴 5 亿美元。

这不是对 startup 征收 5% 的税，在这个简单例子里其实是 50%。

English

Garry Tan argues that an asset-seizure tax can look small in percentage terms while becoming enormous for founders whose wealth is locked in private-company shares. In his example, a founder owning 50% of a company at a $2B valuation with 20x voting rights could face a tax on paper wealth that cannot simply be turned into cash.

You are a founder who owns 50% of your company and you have 20x voting rights. You owe 5% on $10B in the asset seizure tax. You owe $500M of your paper $1B.

It's not a 5% tax on startups. It's a 50% tax in this simple case.
