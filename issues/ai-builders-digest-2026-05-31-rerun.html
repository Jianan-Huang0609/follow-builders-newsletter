World Models / 世界模型

Gemini 联席负责人 Oriol Vinyals 谈 Google 的差异化路线：世界模型 + 持续学习。

OV
Oriol Vinyals (Co-Lead, Gemini)
Podcast
中文

Gemini 联席负责人 Oriol Vinyals 阐述了 Google 的差异化押注：「语言里显然有很多关于世界的信息，但用所有视频来训练能不能达到同等的理解水平——甚至填补纯文本缺失的因果推理？」Omni 模型就是 Google 对世界模型的押注——把视频和图像压缩成概念理解，而不只是生成能力。

关于世界模型的定义：「纯粹的一面是表征学习——把视频序列压缩成概念：运动、物体、物理。但你和 Omni 互动时的感觉更多：你可以改变视频的行为方式，从单张图生成动画，施加控制。」世界模型不只是理解——而是可控的模拟。

关于持续学习，Vinyals 指了一条实操路径：「我们很多人把这叫持续学习——用文件系统做非参数记忆。把想法写到文件里，组织成目录，跨多次对话。这比写回模型权重方便多了。」Agent 的长期记忆可能根本不需要放在模型权重里。

关于机器人数据的飞轮：「如果从机器人获取更多数据进入模型，增强世界模型能力，事情就开始值得做了。我们可能会看到机器人加速。」物理 AI 和世界模型构成了强化循环。

用所有视频和图像来训练，能不能达到语言模型用语言达到的同等理解水平，甚至填补纯文本缺失的因果推理？

世界模型的纯粹一面是表征学习——把视频压缩成概念、运动、物体。但和 Omni 互动时感受到的是你可以改变视频行为、从图片生成动画。

我们把这叫持续学习——用文件系统做非参数记忆。把想法写到文件里组织成目录。比写回权重方便。

如果从机器人获取更多数据进入模型增强世界模型能力，事情就值得做了。机器人可能会加速。

English

Oriol Vinyals, co-lead of Gemini, lays out Google's distinctive bet: 'Language clearly has a lot of information about the world, but could training on all the videos ever produced get us to the same level of understanding, potentially filling gaps with cause-effect reasoning that pure text misses?' The Omni model is Google's bet on world models — compressing video and imagery into conceptual understanding, not just generation.

On the definition of world models: 'A pure aspect would be representation learning — compressing video sequences into concepts: movements, objects, physics. But what you feel when interacting with Omni is more: you can change how the video behaves, animate from a single image, exert control.' World models aren't just about understanding — they're about controllable simulation.

On continual learning, Vinyals pointed to a practical path: 'Many of us call this continual learning — using the file system as a non-parametric memory. Write thoughts to files, structure into directories, across multiple episodes. It's more convenient than integrating back into the weights.' The agent's long-term memory might not need to be in the model weights at all.

On the robot-data flywheel: 'If we get more data captured from robots, that data could make it into the model, enhancing world model capabilities. And more powerful models accelerate robotics.' Physical AI and world models form a reinforcement loop.

Could training on all the videos ever produced and images get us to the same level of understanding that language models using language get, although probably slightly superficially and some missing links with cause-effect?

A pure aspect of world model would be representation learning — compressing videos into concepts, movements, objects. But what you see with Omni is being able to change how the video behaves, animate from an image.

Many of us call this continual learning — using the file system as non-parametric memory. Write thoughts to files, structure into directories. It's more convenient than integrating back into the weights.

If more data captured from robots makes it into the model, enhancing the world model capabilities, things start to be worth doing. We might see an acceleration in robotics.
