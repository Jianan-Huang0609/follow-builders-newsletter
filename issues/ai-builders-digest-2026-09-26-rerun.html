Theme 01

The Hidden AI Layer / 被忽略的 AI 中间层

真正把 GPU 喂饱、把模型放进企业流程的，不只是模型本身，而是数据、存储、权限和调度。

RH
Renen Hallak
The MAD Podcast with Matt Turck
中文

一句话结论：AI 最不显眼的那一层，可能会成为最值钱的一层。VAST Data CEO Renen Hallak 把 GPU 和应用之间的软件基础设施称为 AI 时代的操作系统，它要处理数据访问、模型管理、推理、agent memory、权限和安全。

更关键的判断是：企业会逐渐拥有由自身经验产生的 intelligence。agent 在使用中持续 fine-tune，沉淀下来的 weights 和 skills 就会变成组织自己的 IP。难点因此不只是存储更快，而是建立一层信任机制：企业能使用模型，却不暴露自己的数据；模型公司能提供能力，却不泄露自己的 weights。Hallak 给出的规模提醒很直接：一个 AI cloud 客户原本预计三年需要 500 PB，后来又追加了 2 EB。

这期内容最有用的 mental model 是：AI factory 可以在本地、机房或云上，只要组织真正控制自己的信息、模型、weights 和 agents。

有时候这让我害怕。一个 AI cloud 客户说未来三年大概需要 500 PB，上周回来却说还要在这 500 PB 之上再加 2 EB。

我有时称它为软件基础设施，有时称它为这个新时代的操作系统。

我们白天做推理，晚上做 fine-tune。

English

The takeaway: AI's least visible layer may become one of its most valuable. VAST Data CEO Renen Hallak describes the software infrastructure between GPUs and applications as an operating system for the AI era, handling data access, model management, inference, agent memory, permissions, and security.

His sharper prediction is that enterprises will gradually own the intelligence created through their own experience: agents fine-tune during use, and the resulting weights and skills become organizational IP. The infrastructure challenge is therefore not merely faster storage. It is creating a trust layer where an enterprise can use a model without exposing its data, while the model builder protects its weights. Hallak's scale warning is blunt: one AI-cloud customer moved from planning 500 petabytes to asking for two additional exabytes.

The episode's most useful mental model is simple: the AI factory can be on-prem, in a colo, or rented from a cloud, as long as the organization controls its information, models, weights, and agents.

Sometimes it scares me. We had a customer, one of these AI clouds, they said we're probably going to need about 500 petabytes over the next three years. Last week, came back to us and said, we're gonna need an extra two exabytes on top of that 500 petabytes.

I like to call it software infrastructure, and other times I like to call it the operating system for this new era.

We infer during the day, fine-tune at night.

Peter Steinberger 给了一个很实用的 agent 委派经验：“清理一下”太模糊，agent 很快就会停。换成一个有野心、可度量的约束，比如删除 20% 最没用的测试，同时让 coverage 保持在 2% 的波动内。重点不是让 agent 无休止地工作，而是把成功标准定义得足够清楚，让它能真正围绕目标优化。

如果你只是告诉 agent 清理一下，它会停得太早。给它一个有野心的目标。可以试试：删除 20% 最没用的测试，同时让代码覆盖率保持在 2% 以内的变化。

English

Peter Steinberger offers a practical lesson in delegating to agents: 'clean up' is too vague, so the agent stops early. Give it an ambitious, measurable constraint instead, such as removing 20% of the least useful tests while keeping coverage within 2%. The point is not to make agents work harder for its own sake; it is to define success tightly enough that the agent can optimize toward it.

If you just tell the agent to clean up, it will stop far too early. Give it an ambitious goal. Try "remove 20% of the least useful tests while maintaining code coverage within 2%"
