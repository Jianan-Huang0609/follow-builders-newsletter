Aaron Levie 看到的 agent adoption 呈现出明显的两极分化：coding 及其邻近任务已经起飞，其他知识工作还处在非常早期。即便在 coding 里，真正让 background agents 并行工作的也只是少数，大多数开发者仍然是和 agent 一对一协作。

原因不是大家没兴趣，而是工作流本身要重做，数据要重新接线，责任与问责要重新定义，治理、合规和安全也要升级。所以，今天看到的 adoption 可能还只是未来的 1%。

现在 AI agent adoption 仍然非常两极化：coding 和 coding adjacent 任务已经起飞，其他一切还没有。

大多数工作流仍然需要重新设计，才能和 agent 一起工作。

AI 工厂的中间层 / The Operating System Between GPUs and Models

真正的 AI 工厂不只是 GPU 堆起来，还要管理数据、模型、推理、权限和组织自己的 intelligence。

RH
Renan Hallak
VAST Data CEO · The MAD Podcast
中文

AI factory 不是简单地堆一屋子 GPU。Renan Hallak 描述的，是一层新的基础设施：它要管理 compute、storage、networking、database、training、inference，最终还要管理一组组 agent。数据本身也变了：图片、视频、声音和自然语言，比传统企业 workload 占空间更多，也需要更快的访问速度。

更锋利的判断在于 ownership。agent 从组织的真实经历中学习后，fine-tuned intelligence 会变成新的 IP。企业可以把模型放在 on-prem、colo 或 AI cloud，但必须掌握自己的数据、weights、models 和 agents。基础设施层要提供这种安全边界，包括让双方都看不到不该看到内容的 confidential inference。

他给出的记忆点是：这一层正在成为新时代的 operating system，未来甚至可能出现 intelligence marketplace，让组织租用技能，却不暴露底层 weights。

English

The AI factory is not just a room full of GPUs. Renan Hallak describes a new infrastructure layer that has to manage compute, storage, networking, databases, training, inference and eventually teams of agents. The data is also changing: images, video, sound and natural language require much more space and much faster access than old enterprise workloads.

His sharper point is about ownership. As agents learn from an organization's experiences, that fine-tuned intelligence becomes IP. Enterprises may run models on-prem, in a colo or in an AI cloud, but they need to retain control of their data, weights, models and agents. The software infrastructure layer has to make that safe, including confidential inference where neither side sees what it should not see.

The memorable mental model: this layer is becoming the operating system for a new era, and eventually an intelligence marketplace may let organizations lease skills without exposing the underlying weights.

English

His answer is to treat software more like an artistic medium. Theme 05

安全不是附加项 / Security Becomes a Core Function

随着 agent 和 AI adversary 变强，安全会同时变成 verification、资源分配和创业机会。

中文

Guillermo Rauch 预计，security 会在 software company 里变成更大的职能。它既是 verification engineering，比如判断代码是否大概率 memory-safe，也是 capital allocation：决定哪些 attack surface 最值得投入 token。

AI adversary 越来越强，小团队越难获得信任；但人们对中心化系统的依赖和糟糕的网络安全现状，也给小团队留下了大量可以突破的空间。

English

Guillermo Rauch expects security to become a larger function inside software companies. He frames it as both verification engineering, such as asking whether code is probably memory-safe, and capital allocation: deciding which attack surfaces deserve the most tokens.

For startups, increasingly capable AI adversaries make trust harder for tiny teams. But the same dependence on centralized systems and the poor state of cybersecurity also leave large areas open for small teams to disrupt.
