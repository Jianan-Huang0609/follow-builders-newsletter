The New Builder Skill / 新的 Builder 能力

真正稀缺的能力，开始从亲自完成每一步，转向知道什么时候该引导、什么时候该放手。

中文

Guillermo Rauch 认为，管理引导力度是他最看重的能力。有时 AI 比你知道得更多，需要你给它空间；有时它会跑偏、带着错误偏见，或者缺少上下文，需要你收紧方向。真正的工作不是把 prompt 写得更用力，而是学会什么时候介入。

有时你会过度引导 AI，它比你更懂；有时你又引导得不够，它会跑偏、带着错误偏见或错过上下文。

你如何管理这个超能力，是我最看重的能力。

English

Guillermo Rauch calls the ability to manage guidance the number-one skill he looks for. Sometimes AI knows more and needs room; sometimes it goes off-track, carries the wrong bias, or lacks context and needs tighter direction. The work is not simply prompting harder. It is learning when to intervene.

There’ll be times where you over-guide the AI. It knows better than you and is more intelligent than you.

How well you manage this superpower is the #0 skill I look for.

What AI Should Not Remove / AI 不该替你拿走的东西

效率之外，builder 也在重新审视困难、关系和现实世界中的共同创作。

中文

Ryo Lu 担心，agent 让我们在还没学会困难原本要教什么之前，就轻松拿到了结果。同样的便利，也可能让我们绕过和别人一起工作的笨拙过程：意见不合、修复关系，最后做出任何一个人都做不出来的东西。

他的结论不是反对工具，而是希望工具帮助我们更多地进入真实世界，找到人，分享未完成的东西，一起经历困难。像一支爵士乐队，而不是服务器农场的无声噪音。

我们学会了如何得到结果，却不一定理解它是怎么工作的。

我不想要没有困难的人生，我想要可以一起经历困难的人。

English

Ryo Lu worries that agents make it easy to get results before learning what the difficulty was teaching us. The same convenience can also let us avoid the awkward, slow process of working with other people: disagreeing, repairing, and eventually making something none of us could make alone.

His conclusion is not anti-tool. He wants tools that help us spend more time with the world, find people, share unfinished work, and struggle together—more like a jazz band than the silent static of a server farm.

We learn how to get results without necessarily understanding how it works.

I don’t want a life without struggle. I want people to struggle with.

Small Products, Strong Habits / 小产品如何留下来

一个产品能否留下来，往往不在功能数量，而在它能不能把行为变成习惯。

完成 30 节课后，Once 会寄书给孩子，让他们自己阅读。

English

Peter Yang says Once Reading is the only learning app he and his four-year-old have kept using for months. His explanation is refreshingly concrete: a daily parent-child habit, lightweight gamification such as taking a silly photo after each lesson, and a 30-lesson milestone that sends the child books to read independently.
