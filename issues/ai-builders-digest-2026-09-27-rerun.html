Theme 01

Agents Doing the Work / Agent 开始真正干活

最值得注意的变化，不是 agent 更会聊天，而是它们开始直接进入 PR、bug 修复和数据分析。

中文

Claude Code 联合创作者 Boris Cherny 说，Tag 现在每天写掉他一半以上的 PR，几乎包办全部数据分析，还能修掉大多数产品反馈和 bug。关键在于，Tag 不是一个被动等指令的 Slack bot，而是一个主动、可编程、有记忆、能调用连接器的工作伙伴。

他的例子包括自动确认已解决的 thread、端到端复现 bug 并提交修复 PR，以及围绕异常数据跑一百个假设、验证后画出图表。自动化的单位，正在从一次 prompt 变成一个持续工作的 teammate。

Tag 每天写掉我超过 50% 的 PR，也做了大约 100% 的数据分析，修复大多数产品反馈和 bug。

它是主动的、可编程的，有记忆，也能访问你的连接器。

English

Claude Code co-creator Boris Cherny says Tag now writes more than half of his daily PRs, handles essentially all of his data analysis, and fixes most product feedback and bugs. The important detail is that Tag is not a passive Slack bot: it is proactive, programmable, has memory, and can use connected tools.

His examples range from reacting to resolved threads, reproducing bugs end to end and opening a fix PR, to testing a hundred hypotheses against strange data and turning the result into a chart. The unit of automation is moving from a single prompt to a persistent teammate with a workflow.

Tag writes >50% of my PRs every day. It also does ~100% of my data analysis, and fixes most of our product feedback + bugs.

It is proactive, programmable, it has memory, it has access to your connectors.

Theme 02

The Measurement Layer / 先把 agent 测明白

AI 在企业里扩散的前提，不只是模型更强，而是组织知道什么叫做变好。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 把 evals 放在企业采用 AI 的门口：无法测量的东西，就无法自动化。传统软件可以测试确定性流程，但企业往往没有办法建立一个有用的基线，去理解 agent 正在处理的非确定性工作。

如果不知道什么有效、什么坏了、哪里变好了，所有部署都只是猜测。机会不只在于给模型做更好的 eval，企业也需要一套属于自己的、针对业务环境的 agent 表现画像。

无法测量的东西，就无法自动化。Evals 是 AI 在企业扩散的门槛之一。

所有变化、升级和部署，都建立在好的 evals 之上。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie puts evals at the gate of enterprise AI adoption: you cannot automate what you cannot measure. Traditional software can test deterministic processes, but enterprises often have no useful baseline for the non-deterministic work agents are doing.

Without a clear view of what works, breaks, changes, or improves in your environment, every deployment is guesswork. The opportunity is not only better model evals for labs; every enterprise will need its own domain-specific picture of agent performance.

You can’t automate what you can’t measure. This means that evals are one of the gates to diffusion of AI in the enterprise.

All changes, upgrades, and deployments are downstream from good evals.

It is a small but useful shift in mental model: model effort is a control knob, not a quality setting that should always be pushed to the top.

Theme 03

SaaS After Agents / Agent 时代的 SaaS

产品的第一用户正在从人变成 agent，数据可访问性和可操作性会重新定义软件采购。

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 认为，企业采购 SaaS 的标准会从“对人是否好用”转向“对 agent 是否好用”。agent 必须拿到业务数据，这也是为什么企业软件突然开始重视 CLI、MCP，以及过去被忽略的 API。

当 agent 能读懂公司的 ontology、操作业务数据之后，Rauch 预计有很长一尾 SaaS 应用不会再被购买，而是直接生成：更安全、更高效，也更贴合每家公司和员工。

新的采购标准会是产品对 agent 是否友好，而不是对人是否符合人体工学。

我怀疑有很长一尾 SaaS 应用以后不会再被购买，而是被生成出来。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch expects the enterprise SaaS procurement bar to shift from human ergonomics to agent ergonomics. Agents need business data, which is why enterprise vendors are suddenly prioritizing CLIs, MCPs, and APIs that had been neglected.

Once agents can navigate a company's ontology and work with its data, Rauch expects a long tail of SaaS apps to stop being bought and start being generated: more secure, more performant, and tailored to each company and employee.

The new 'procurement bar' will be how ergonomic your product is for agents, rather than humans.

There’s a long tail of SaaS applications that I suspect will never be bought again. They'll be generated.

Theme 04

Rebuilding the Company / 重做组织本身

真正激进的 AI 转型，不是给每个人发一个小工具，而是重新设计组织如何工作。

NP
No Priors
No Priors / Sequence Holdings
中文

Sequence Holdings 联合创始人兼 CEO Michael Lee 认为，AI 转型不该止步于给每个人发一台小机器、把旧的人类流水线加速一点。当机器可以 24/7 工作，并随着电力扩展，企业应该重新思考组织是什么，把技术和人各自最擅长的部分重新组合。

他的做法是成立一家永久控股公司，买下合适的 incumbent，保留它们的优势，再把 frontier engineering 带进来。文化目标也很明确：让工程师成为组织里被推崇的人，把公司重心放在打造市场领导者，而不只是部署资本。

当机器可以 24/7 工作、随着电力扩展……正确答案是重新思考组织应该是什么。

我们的工作是每年做一笔交易。

English

Sequence Holdings co-founder and CEO Michael Lee argues that AI transformation should not stop at giving every human a small machine to speed up the old assembly line. When machines can work 24/7 and scale with electricity, companies should rethink what an organization is, combining what technology does best with what humans do best.

His operating model is a permanent holding company that buys the right incumbents, keeps their advantages, and brings frontier engineering to bear. The cultural target is equally explicit: make the engineer the celebrated persona, and focus the company on forging market leaders rather than merely deploying capital.

When you have machines that can work 24/7, that can scale with electricity ... the right answer is to think about how you start to reorganize what an organization should be.

Our job is to do one [deal per year].
