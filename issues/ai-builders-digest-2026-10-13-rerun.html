能力曲线正在分叉 / The Capability Gap

同一个 AI 世界里，大多数人刚开始试用，少数人已经在管理成群的 agent。

AK
Andrej Karpathy
@karpathy
中文

Andrej Karpathy 描述了一条陡峭的 AI 使用漏斗：约 60 亿人几乎没接触过 LLM，10–20 亿人只是偶尔使用，约 2000 万人在数学和代码工作中使用 frontier 模型，而真正接触到最先进内部系统的，只有约 5000 人。

奇怪之处在于，这几种现实同时存在。鸿沟由问题的规模、领域是否数字化且可验证，以及模型访问权限共同拉大。对最窄处的那群人来说，过去要做几周或几个月的项目，现在可能只需要给 agent 一个 prompt。

这条漏斗由几个因素驱动：1. 影响取决于野心、问题规模和时间跨度；2. 系统能力的不均匀性；3. 访问权限：免费层、付费层、内部系统。

而这一切正在同时发生。

English

Andrej Karpathy describes a sharp funnel in AI adoption: roughly 6 billion people have barely touched LLMs, about 1–2 billion use them casually, around 20 million use frontier models professionally in math and code, and only about 5,000 people see the most advanced internal systems.

The strange part is that all these realities coexist. The gap widens with ambition, with domains that are digital and verifiable, and with access to better models. For the small group at the narrow end, projects that once took weeks or months can now be completed by agents from a prompt.

The funnel is driven by a combination of factors: 1. The impact scales with ambition, problem size, and horizon. 2. The jaggedness of the system. 3. Access. Free-tier, paid-tier, internal.

And it is all happening at the same time.
