The Enterprise Layer Gets Deeper / 企业层开始变深

模型能力越强，真正的差异越不在聊天框，而在行业流程和长期运行的系统。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 做了一个和「模型会把一切商品化」相反的判断：模型越擅长长时间运行和调用工具，行业专业化可能越有价值。护城河会转移到工作流、领域数据、模型路由、agent 协同，以及真正改变企业工作方式所需要的 change management。

模型越强、越能处理长任务和更好的工具调用，按行业做差异化的能力反而越强。

在 agent 调用工具、任务运行数小时或数天的世界里，这些系统的专业化会更高。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie makes the opposite bet from the 'models will commoditize everything' story: as models get better at long-running tasks and tool use, domain specialization may become more valuable. The moat moves into workflows, domain data, model routing, agent coordination, and the change management needed to alter how a business actually works.

as models are getting more capable, and can handle longer running tasks and better tool use, the ability to differentiate on a domain by domain basis goes up instead of down.

in a world of agents using tools, tasks that run on for hours or days ... specialization of these systems goes up more.
