Theme 01

From Agents to Swarms / 从 Agent 到 Agent Swarm

当 agent 不再只是单个助手，而是围绕目标自行分工，工作方式会出现新的分水岭。

中文

Box CEO Aaron Levie 认为，下一轮生产力跃升来自 agent swarm：一群围绕目标行动、自己寻找完成路径的 agent。演进路线很清楚：chat 加快了交互，单个 agent 把几小时压缩成几分钟，而 swarm 可能彻底移除人与工具交互这一瓶颈。

所以机会不只是再做一个聊天界面，而是构建 harness、应用型产品和 FDE 工作，把这种协作模式接入编程、游戏、交易、科研、安全等数字环境。

Agent swarm 有潜力再次推动 AI 的产出跃升。影响会很明确：采用这种工作模式的团队，将和不采用的团队拉开差距。 大致的演进是：chat 提供快速的 AI 响应，让工作稍微变快，但我们仍受交互次数限制；然后是能够自行完成整项任务的 agent，把几小时或几天的工作加速到几分钟；现在则是围绕目标行动的 swarm，它们会找到任何完成项目的路径，生产力收益没有内在上限。 在任何能于自包含数字环境中创造价值的任务里，swarm 都可能比其他方式更快完成工作。 这包括游戏开发、编程、交易、生命科学研究、IT 自动化、网络安全、数学突破、动画等领域。构建 agent 运作所需的 harness、应用型 AI 产品和基础设施，会带来大量机会，也会催生一整类新的 FDE 工作。

English

Box CEO Aaron Levie sees the next productivity jump coming from agent swarms: groups of agents that pursue a goal and find their own route to completion. The progression is clear—chat sped up interactions, single agents compressed hours into minutes, and swarms could remove the interaction bottleneck altogether.

That makes the opportunity less about building another chat interface and more about the harnesses, applied products, and FDE work that wire this pattern into coding, games, trading, science, security, and other digital environments.

Agent swarms have the potential to drive yet another leap in output from AI. The impact will be a clear separation between teams that adopt this pattern of work and those that don’t. The progression has roughly been: chat gave us quick turn AI responses that let us work a bit faster, but we were still rate limited by our interactions; then agents that can go off and complete entire tasks for us, accelerating hours or days of work into minutes; to now swarms of agents that are goal seeking and will find any route to the successful completion of a project, where there’s no inherent limit to the productivity gains. We can imagine a future where, for any task in which value can be created within a self-contained digital environment, swarms will be able to complete the work faster than any other approach. Essentially any area where value creation is currently bound by the amount of research you can do, the amount of decisions made with a set of software tools, the amount of data processed or consumed, and the amount of code (or code adjacent) that can be generated. This means swarms will play a big role in game development, coding, trading, life sciences research, IT automation, cyber, math breakthroughs, animation, and plenty of other areas of work that have these characteristics. There’s a lot of opportunity to build the harnesses, applied AI products, and infrastructure for how these agents operate. And most importantly there’s a ton of opportunity for those that can wire up work in this way. It’s going to be an entire new field of FDE work available.

Tools That Meet the Real World / 工具进入真实工作流

真正有用的 AI 产品不是一次性 demo，而是能持续协作、处理边界情况并进入已有工作环境的工具。

中文

Dan Shipper 对 Gemini 4 Carbon 的 day-zero 测试提醒我们：模型选择越来越取决于任务。它的 coding 明显强于 Argon，可以连续数小时做大型项目，在音频、视频和 3D 任务上也有优势，但有时不知道该停，还会产出巨大而不够模块化的文件。

他的实用建议是：给 Carbon 一个边界清晰的大任务，然后认真审查结果。它适合作为强力的第二意见，还不是替换整套工具链的理由。

Google 刚发布 Gemini 4 Carbon。它的 coding 比 Argon 好很多，但写作我可能还是用 Argon。我们在 coding、写作、设计和知识工作中测试了它。它可以连续数小时做一个有野心的项目，但不总知道什么时候该停。 在一次 Senior Engineer benchmark 中，Carbon 得分 44%，Argon 为 21%。它还完成了 3D 工作室模型、Everycut 视频编辑器更新的大约 95%，能发现其他模型漏掉的音频问题，也能从会议录音生成 6 个带标题的短片。缺点是有时会把 6 个任务做成 15 个，甚至重复别人正在做的工作；它的文件也可能很大、不够模块化。 我的结论是：偶尔会用。给它一个有明确终点的大任务，然后检查结果；特别值得在音频和视频任务上作为第二意见试试。

His practical rule is simple: give Carbon one large job with a clear finish line, then review the result. It is a promising second opinion, not a reason to replace every tool in the stack.

The Shape of AI-Native Software / AI 原生软件的形状

当 agent 成为软件的主要使用者和协作者，产品设计与底层语言都会开始重新分化。

中文

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch 认为，agentic coding 会让编程语言变得更专用，而不是更统一。Rust 已经展示了强大语言生态的样子；下一代工具可能会包括为特定场景打造的语言，甚至直接使用 C、LLVM IR 或 assembly。

底层逻辑是：AI 让软件变得更流动。当 agent 能够跨越定制化技术栈工作，就没必要再把所有问题硬塞进一种通用语言。

Rust 是非常出色的语言和生态，尤其考虑到它诞生在 agentic coding 之前。 有了 agent，新语言会大量出现，甚至是为特定用途打造的语言，也可能直接使用 C、LLVM IR 或 assembly。 AI 是流动的，不再是一种方案适合所有场景。

English

Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch argues that agentic coding will make programming languages more specialized, not less. Rust already shows what a strong ecosystem can look like; the next generation may include purpose-built languages, discretionary C, LLVM IR, or even assembly for particular use cases.

The underlying idea is that AI makes software more fluid. There is less reason to force every problem into one general-purpose language when an agent can work across a tailored stack.

Rust is a superb language and ecosystem, especially given it was invented pre agentic coding. With agents, new languages will blossom, even purpose-built ones for specific usecases, and discretionary C or even LLVM IR, assembly… AI is fluid. No more one-size-fits-all. https://t.co/YTwRelXWCW

Human Collaboration Still Matters / 人的协作仍然重要

AI 让个人能力扩张，但更强的工具不等于更好的孤岛；产品和工作方式仍然要把人连接起来。

T
Thariq
@trq212
中文

Thariq Shihipar 给当下的 AI demo 文化做了一个重要纠偏：我们需要少一点一次性展示，多一点能让人和模型协作、把愿景真正做出来的工具。

区别在这里：demo 只能证明模型某一次能产出东西；产品则必须帮助人持续引导、修改和推进，直到结果真正属于自己。

我们需要更少的一次性 demo，更多展示如何与模型协作、把你的愿景变成现实的工具。

English

Thariq Shihipar makes a useful correction to the current AI demo culture: we need fewer one-shot showcases and more tools that let people collaborate with models to bring a vision to life.

The distinction matters. A demo proves that a model can produce something once; a product has to help a person steer, revise, and keep going until the result is actually theirs.

we need fewer one shot demos and more tools that show how you can collaborate with models to bring your vision to life https://t.co/OlEhSqu9Mo
