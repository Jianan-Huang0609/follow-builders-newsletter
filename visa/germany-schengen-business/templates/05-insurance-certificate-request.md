# 模板 05 · 向公司索取团体商旅险证明

**收件人**：HR / Mobility / 差旅管理
**时机**：随时可发，但要留出「对不上就自购」的时间

许多公司为国际出差统一投保了商旅险，并可应请求出具 Certificate of Coverage。
**但公司团险经常不写遣返条款或不写具体保额** —— 拿到后必须逐项核对，
对不上就自行另购一份申根签证险（几十到一百多元），别硬用。

---

```
Subject: Certificate of coverage — business travel insurance, for Schengen visa application ({{出差编号}})

Dear colleagues,

I am applying for a Schengen business visa for a trip to Germany from
{{起始日期}} to {{结束日期}} ({{出差编号}}). The German consulate requires
proof of travel medical insurance, and the business trip assessment states
that coverage certificates for the company travel insurance are available
on request.

Could you please issue a certificate of coverage in English for this trip?
For the visa application the certificate has to show all of the following
explicitly — could you confirm which of these it covers:

  1. my name as insured person ({{护照姓名}})
  2. validity covering the whole period of stay, {{起止日期}}
  3. valid throughout the entire Schengen area
  4. minimum coverage of EUR 30,000
  5. outpatient and inpatient medical treatment
  6. repatriation for medical reasons and repatriation of remains
  7. no explicit exclusion of COVID-19 treatment

If any of points 4–6 is not stated on the certificate, please let me know —
I will then take out an additional private policy for the visa application.

Thank you very much.

Best regards,
{{英文名}}
```

---

**注意**：第 6 条是拒签高发点。保单上没有 *repatriation* 字样，
就等于缺项，不管保额写得多高。
