# 模板 04 · 英文个人说明信

**由谁出具**：申请人自己签字
**作用**：把材料串成一个故事，并**主动解释异常**

非必需材料，但当银行流水有异常（例如活期余额因自动理财归集长期显示 0）时，
主动解释远好过等签证官自己猜。

---

```
{{护照姓名}}
{{住址}}
{{城市}}, P. R. China
{{手机号}} · {{邮箱}}

{{日期}}

To the Visa Section
Consulate General of the Federal Republic of Germany, {{领馆城市}}

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am applying for a Schengen business visa for a trip to Germany from
{{起始日期}} to {{结束日期}}, and would like to set out the purpose of the
trip and my circumstances.

Purpose of travel
I have been employed by {{雇主英文全称}} since {{入职年月}} as a {{职位}}.
{{一到两句：为什么是你去、这个活动是什么}}. My employer has approved and
assigned me to attend it. The invitation letter of {{邀请方公司}} and the
letter of assignment of my employer are enclosed.

Costs
All costs of the trip — air fare, accommodation, travel medical insurance and
daily expenses — are borne by {{与派遣函一致}}. My travel medical insurance
covers the entire Schengen area for the whole period of stay with a minimum
coverage of EUR 30,000, including repatriation.

A note on my bank statements
{{如无异常则删除整段}}
My salary account is with {{银行}}. The balance column of the statement
frequently shows 0.00 because the account is linked to an automatic sweep
product, which moves the balance into a money market fund at the end of each
day and back when needed. The funds are not spent — they are simply held in
the linked fund rather than in the current account. I therefore enclose an
official asset certificate issued by the bank, which shows the actual total of
my holdings, together with {{月数}} months of salary payments from my employer
visible in the statement.

Ties to China
I am in continuous full-time employment, my leave has been approved with a
fixed return-to-work date of {{返岗日}}, and {{其他约束：长期项目 / 家庭 /
房产}}. I have previously travelled to {{已访问国家}} and have always complied
with the conditions of my visas and returned within their validity.

I would be grateful for your favourable consideration and remain available
for any further document you may require.

Yours faithfully,

[ 亲笔签名 ]
{{护照姓名}}
```
