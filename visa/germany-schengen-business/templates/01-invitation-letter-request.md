# 模板 01 · 向境外邀请方索要邀请函

**收件人**：境外主办方 / 会务联系人
**时机**：确定行程后立刻发，这是整套材料里周期最长的一份

境外同事多半没给中国同事开过签证邀请函，不知道德国领馆的硬性格式。
这封信的重点不是「请给我一封邀请函」，而是**把格式要求逐条列给对方，一次开对**。

---

```
Subject: Invitation letter for Schengen business visa — {{活动名称}}, {{城市}}, {{起止日期}}

Dear {{名}},

I am {{英文名}} ({{护照姓名}}) from {{部门}} at {{雇主全称}}. I will join
{{活动名称}} in {{城市}} from {{起始日期}} to {{结束日期}}
(business trip ID {{出差编号}}).

As a Chinese passport holder I need a Schengen business visa, and
{{推荐人}}'s mail of {{日期}} named you as the contact for invitation letters.
May I ask you to issue one?

The German consulate in China applies a fixed format and rejects invitations
that are missing any of the following, so I am listing them here to save us a
second round:

  - printed on {{邀请方公司}} letterhead, showing the company's current
    address, telephone, fax, e-mail and a contact person
  - company stamp and the date of issue
  - handwritten signature of the responsible manager, with name and position
    printed underneath (electronic signatures are not accepted; a scan of the
    signed original is fine)
  - my name exactly as in my passport: {{护照姓名}}
  - my date of birth: {{出生日期}} and passport number: {{护照号}}
  - purpose of the visit: {{出访目的}}
  - exact dates of the stay: {{起始日期}} to {{结束日期}}
  - place of stay and itinerary: {{城市}}, Germany
  - a clear statement of who bears the travel and accommodation costs
    (please state whether these are borne by {{邀请方公司}} or by my
    employing entity in {{所在城市}})

My appointment at the visa centre is planned for {{预约时间}}, so a signed scan
by e-mail before {{截止日期}} would be a great help. The original by post is
not needed.

Thank you very much — I am looking forward to the event.

Best regards,
{{英文名}}
{{职位}}
{{雇主全称}}
{{工作邮箱}}
```

---

**注意**

- 不确定对方称谓时直接用 `Dear {{名}}`，不要猜 Mr / Ms。
- 署名同时给出护照写法和公司系统写法，对方照抄就不会错。
- 明确说「扫描件即可，不需要寄原件」，能省掉一到两周国际快递。
